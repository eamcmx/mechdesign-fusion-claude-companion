Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds each of the nine assemblies as Parts 9 and 10 left them and makes
its bill of materials: every part counted from the model, numbered by Chapter 49's scheme and the numbers Chapters 50
to 56 added, described as a shop or a buyer orders it, with its mass, so that purchasing and the shop work from one
list.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the machine: one of all, wrist, flap, axle, robot, changer, clamp, vacuum, bag or unit, all

DERIVED (never typed):
- RangeSweep.py's machine(): each machine as its detail chapter built it (the bag gripper with its stops)
- lib/bom_detail.py: each component's line from its name (Chapter 49's names, then the kinds Volume 3 added), the
  descriptions as they now stand, the weldment, the numbers that supersede others, and the makers' masses with the
  saved page of each
- lib/materials_fits.py: each made kind's material (Chapter 51)

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build each machine through RangeSweep.py's machine(), loaded, not copied.
- Take the census from Fusion: every occurrence that has a body, its name, Fusion's mass, its materials and volume.
- Count a bought part once per set: a bearing's rings and balls, a tapered bearing's cone and cup, a reducer's case and
  output are one piece; a body that comes with a bought part (a cylinder's piston rod, a shock absorber's lock nut) is
  no line of its own.
- Leave out the loads and the station (the bag, the cartons, the rollers), and say how many.
- Give the jaw weldment one line, its tube, its two stubs and its welds under it.
- Where two numbers name one designation, the lower number stands: one line, the other number recorded as superseded.
- A made part's mass is Fusion's; a bought part's is its maker's where a saved page gives it, otherwise Fusion's, and
  the line says which.
- Write the part number and the description into each component of the design, so that Fusion's own parts lists carry
  them, and read them back.
- Write each machine's list to results/bom/<machine>.csv and all of them to results/bom.json; close each design
  without saving but the last, which stays open with its numbered components for a look.
- Refuse a machine that is not one of the nine.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- a new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType) for each machine, made by its own script; masses in kg, lengths in
  mm, the API's cm inside.
- The joints are each machine's own: the wrist, the flap and the rear axle keep their scripts' As-built joints; the
  robot, the tool changer, the three grippers and the compliance unit have no joints, as-built or otherwise, and
  place their groups by transforms. The bill adds none and moves nothing.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives each machine's kinds and pieces made and bought, its mass as drawn and with the makers' masses, and
  a version tag.
- Every occurrence is a line, a body of a line, or a load: the lines and the loads add up to Fusion's mass of the
  machine.
- Every line has a number except the welds and the bodies that come with a bought part; no superseded number is left.
- The tool changer: 7 made kinds in 12 pieces, 3 bought kinds in 9.
- The bag gripper: the weldment P13-016 twice, with its tube, its 2 stubs and 4 welds under it; 10 loads left out.
- Every component carries its line's part number when read back.
- Refused: a machine called crane.
