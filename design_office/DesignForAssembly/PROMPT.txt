Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the detailed bag gripper with its stops, puts it through Boothroyd
and Dewhurst's test for unnecessary parts in the model, and then makes the three fixings the test found missing, so
that the designer sees which pieces must be separate, which could go, and the gripper with every part held.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the machine: one of those the test is answered for, which is only the bag gripper, bag

DERIVED (never typed):
- design_office/RangeSweep/RangeSweep.py's machine('bag'): the gripper as its detail script builds it, with its stops
- design_office/lib/bom_detail.py: each component's line of the bill
- design_office/lib/dfma.py: each line's answer to the test (which question keeps how many of its pieces, or none),
  each piece's operation and time (Matuszek et al., 2021, Table 2), the theoretical minimum, the assembly time and the
  design efficiency E = 3 Nmin / t (Range, 2013); the three kinds with no fixing and the fixing each takes; the layout
  from projects/palletizer/lib/bag_gripper.py

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build the gripper through RangeSweep.py's machine(), loaded, not copied.
- Answer each question for a future redesign, not for the parts as they are (Dewhurst & Meeker, 2014, p. 5).
- Give every piece of the bill its answer as an attribute of its occurrence, the weldment's pieces through its tubes,
  and count the pieces, the theoretical minimum and the questions from the attributes read back, not from the lib.
- Colour the pieces: blue the essential, red the candidates, yellow the kinds with no fixing and the new screws.
- The fixings, each with its holes cut: the eight tines of each jaw joined to its tube; each end block joined to its
  end plate, the eight M8 x 30 between them removed and their holes filled (four in each plate, at y = +-50 mm and
  z = -40 and -92 mm); two ISO 4762 M8 x 20 through the beam's side walls into each spigot, at the spigot's
  mid-depth and the beam's mid-height, clearance holes in the walls, holes tapped 20 mm deep from the wall's outer
  face into the spigot; two ISO 4762 M8 x 30 from inside each end plate into its cylinder bracket's root, 42.5 mm
  from the cylinder's pin along y and 30 apart up the arm, clearance holes in the plate, holes tapped 16 mm deep in
  the bracket. These places and depths are typed in the script from the gripper's layout.
- Check the new screws for interference against every other piece of the gripper (the bag and the station left out).
- Count the bill again after the fixings, write everything to design_office/results/dfa.json, leave the design open,
  save nothing.
- Refuse a machine the test is not answered for.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- a new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), as RangeSweep.py makes it; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.
- No joints, as-built or otherwise: the bag gripper's script places its groups by transforms, and the fixings add
  none.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the pieces, the essential ones by question, the pieces after the fixings, what was joined, removed
  and added, the new screws' interferences, the mass before and after, and a version tag.
- 114 pieces answered, every first-level line of the bill among them; 19 essential read back (base 1, move 15,
  separate 3); E = 3 x 19 / 640.2 s.
- 16 tines and 2 end blocks joined; the eight M8 x 30 removed; four M8 x 20 and four M8 x 30 added.
- The bill after: no tine or end block line, ten M8 x 20, four M8 x 30, 96 pieces.
- No interference of the new screws; the mass changes by less than 0.2 kg.
- Refused: a machine called crane.
