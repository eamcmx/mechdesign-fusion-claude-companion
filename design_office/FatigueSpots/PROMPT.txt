Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the bag gripper with its stops and measures in the model the
geometry the fatigue calculations of design_office/lib/fatigue_detail.py take for the gripper's ends, so that the hand
calculations rest on the parts as drawn: the stop brackets' bolts, the brackets' roots, the long stub shaft's keyseat
with its key, and the short stub shaft's circlip groove.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the spot: one of all, bolts, roots, keyseat or groove, all

DERIVED (never typed):
- design_office/RangeSweep/RangeSweep.py's machine('bag'): the bag gripper with its stops, through
  projects/palletizer/PalletizerDetail/PalletizerDetail.py
- the parts by their names: Cap screw ISO 4762 M8x40 stop 1, Nut ISO 4032 M8 stop 1, Stop bracket closed 1, Stop
  bracket open 1, Stub shaft long A, Key 8x7x25 A, Stub shaft short A, the Bearing 6206 round the short stub

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build the gripper through RangeSweep.py's machine(), loaded, not copied.
- Measure from the parts' faces, not from the numbers the scripts were built with.
- The bolts' grip: along the bolt's axis, from the head's underside (the edge of the head's cylinder nearer the
  shank) to the nut's face nearer the head.
- The roots: list every cylindrical face of each bracket under 10 mm in radius that is not a bolt hole, and every
  torus; a bracket with none has sharp roots.
- The keyseat: the stub's diameter there, and the key's length along the stub's axis, its height out from the axis
  and its width, from its mesh (its round ends have no vertices); the keyseat's depth as the stub's radius less the
  key's nearest distance from the axis.
- The groove: its diameter and its place, and the bearing's, as distances from the gripper's middle.
- Colour the brackets and the key red, the bolts and the nuts yellow; the stubs keep the colours
  design_office/MaterialsFits/MaterialsFits.py gave their fits.
- Write the measures to design_office/results/fatigue_spots.json.
- Refuse a spot that is not one of the four.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), made by the gripper's own script; lengths in mm, the API's cm
  inside.
- No joints, as-built or otherwise: the bag gripper's script places its groups by transforms, and nothing is moved.
- The companion's check, design_office/checks/fatigue_spots_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md says
  (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(which); the dialog's input ids spot.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives each spot's measures, the gripper's own first line, and a version tag.
- The grip 28.6 mm, the bolt's head 8 and its shank 8 across, within 0.01.
- Both brackets' roots sharp, with their two bolt holes each.
- The stub 30 across, the key 8 x 7 x 25 and the keyseat 4.0 deep, within 0.02.
- The groove 28.6 across, outside the bearing.
- Refused: a spot called crane.
