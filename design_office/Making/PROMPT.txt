Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the program for one made part: it takes the part out of its
machine as Part 10 left it and programs it in Fusion's Manufacture workspace (the setups and their stock, the
operations with tools from Fusion's sample libraries, the toolpaths generated, each setup's machining time, and the NC
program from Fusion's generic posts), so that the designer sees what the shop will run and how long it takes; and,
asked for the census, measures every made kind of the nine machines in Fusion for the sort by process.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the part: one of bracket, spacer, tine or census, bracket

DERIVED (never typed):
- design_office/RangeSweep/RangeSweep.py: the machines as their detail chapters left them, loaded and called, not copied;
  projects/palletizer/StopBracket/StopBracket.py: Chapter 62's change to the opening bracket;
  design_office/BillOfMaterials/BillOfMaterials.py: each machine's bill, its made lines and the components that are each
  line's pieces
- lib/lugs_brackets.py: the opening bracket's hand volume; lib/drawings_detail.py: the spacer's length, its grinding
  range and the length it is turned to; lib/making.py: the tine's inside corner radius (10 mm), the plate it is
  nested on (2000 x 1000 mm), the spacing between parts and at the plate's edge (10 mm), the number of tines (16)
- the tools from Fusion's sample libraries 'Milling Tools (Metric)', 'Hole Making Tools (Metric)', 'Turning Tools
  (Metric)' and 'Cutting Tools (Metric)'; the posts fanuc.cps, fanuc turning.cps and jet.cps from Fusion's own install

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- bracket: the opening stop bracket P13-018 with its arm turned and its root R2.5, in its own frame (the bolting face
  on z = 0, the arm up z), milled from a block 2.5 mm over each side and 3 mm over top and bottom, in three setups:
  (1) the bolting face faced and the two 9 mm holes drilled; (2) the part bolted to a fixture plate through its own
  holes: the top faced, adaptive roughing leaving 0.3 mm, the walls finished by a 3D contour, the arm's root cut by a
  2D contour of the arm's walls with a 10 mm cutter whose corner radius is the drawing's 2.5 mm, its floor at the
  foot's face; (3) down the lug's axis: the bore to the M30 x 1.5's minor diameter, the thread milled at 1.5 mm
  pitch, the lug's round finished over its 25 mm length.
- spacer: the rear axle's pinion spacer A01-016, its axis along z, turned from 60 x 40 tube: faced, the outside
  roughed and finished, the bore roughed, parted off; faced and parted each half the grinding allowance long, so the
  part comes off at the length it is turned to, for grinding to each axle.
- tine: the bag gripper's tine P13-012, its flat L in x-y and its 10 mm on z, in a component of its own; its inside
  corner (the one straight edge along z inside the box) rounded to lib/making.py's radius; sixteen occurrences of it
  nested by Fusion's Arrange, 2D true shape, on the plate with the spacing and the frame; one cutting setup over the
  nested copies, the stock the frame wider each side and the plate's thickness; one 2D profile with the sample
  laser over the copies' top faces.
- census: each of the nine machines built; each made line of its bill measured on the first component that is one
  of its pieces (a weldment on each of its pieces): every face's kind and area, a plane's normal and a point on it, a
  cylinder's, cone's or torus's axis, a point on it and its radius, the body's volume and smallest box; written to
  results/making_census.json; each machine closed unsaved.
- Every operation generated; each setup's machining time read from Fusion at the programmed feeds, with rapid moves
  taken at 5000 mm/min and 10 s for each tool change, and its NC program written; nothing else saved but the census
  and each part's results.
- Refuse a part that is not one of the four.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- a new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType) for the part, parametric, the body in a base feature, no joints
  and no as-built joints; the Manufacture workspace's CAM product, milling, turning and cutting setups; lengths in mm,
  the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives each setup's operations, its machining time and its NC file with its length in lines, and a
  version tag.
- The bracket's volume within 0.01 % of lib/lugs_brackets.py's; its two holes, its bore and its four root edges
  found.
- Every operation with a toolpath and no warning but Fusion's own note that it moved a lead-in or a lead-out off the
  stock left; every setup timed and posted, its NC file listing its tools or its cutting head.
- The root's cutter with a corner radius of 2.5 mm in the program.
- The spacer as drawn, faced and parted 0.40 mm long each, turned to 68.6 mm.
- The tine: 126 250.0 mm3 sharp and 126 464.6 mm3 with its corner R10 (by hand), outline 1055.708 mm, 10 mm thick,
  in 1020; the round 10 mm clear of the bag's side; all sixteen nested, none left over, Fusion's share of the plate
  that is tine equal to 16 x 12 646.46 / (2000 x 1000) = 10.12 %; one pierce a tine, the cut's length between the
  sixteen outlines' 16 891.3 mm and 2 % more, its time between the outlines' own at the laser's feed (1013.5 s at
  1000 mm/min) and 5 % more, the pierces on top.
- The census: the 168 made kinds of results/bom.json in 330 pieces, each with at least one body; sorted by
  lib/making.py, the routes and the shapes each adding up to 168; the pinion spacer turned, the tine a plate profile
  10 mm thick, the turned opening bracket 3D, the jaw a weldment measured on its 4 (the tube, the two stubs and the welds), the carrier cast, the crown wheel a gear.
- Refused: a part called crane.
