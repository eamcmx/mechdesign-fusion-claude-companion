Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of the nine detailed assemblies, the bag gripper with its
stops, and weighs it: every group's mass, centre and moments of inertia about its centre, and for the robot each of
its eleven moving bodies in the form of projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_weighed.json, which the robot's cycle in
projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_links.py reads, so that the cycle can be run again on the detailed robot.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the machine: one of robot, wrist, flap, axle, bag, clamp, vacuum, changer or unit, robot

DERIVED (never typed):
- design_office/RangeSweep/RangeSweep.py's machine(): the build, through each detail script, the bag gripper with its
  stops
- the parts' materials: the library materials design_office/lib/materials_fits.py decides, as the detail scripts give
  them

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build each machine through RangeSweep.py's machine(), loaded, not copied; copy none of the detail scripts.
- Weigh with Fusion's own physical properties of each part that has bodies: the mass, the centre of mass, and the
  moments of inertia about the world's axes moved to the centre by the parallel-axis rule.
- A group is a top-level occurrence and all the parts under it; a group's name that comes twice is numbered, so that
  no group is lost from the sum.
- For the robot at q = 0: the turret, the lower arm, the crank and counterweight, the parallel rod, levelling rod 1,
  the triangle, the upper arm, levelling rod 2, the wrist housing, the tool flange and the balancer, each with its
  mass, its layer y, its centre against its own pivot and the moments of inertia its motion needs, in
  projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_weighed.json's form; the base apart.
- The robot's bodies are its groups, set to q = 0 with set_pose(G, 0, 0, 0) and measured from its HOME points: the
  turret (group Turret, pivot (AX, AZ)), the lower arm (Lower arm, about A), the crank and counterweight (Crank, about
  A), the parallel rod (Parallel rod, moving with C), levelling rod 1 (about E), the triangle (moving with B), the
  upper arm (about B), levelling rod 2 (about G), the wrist housing (group Wrist without RV-80N output J4 and Tool
  flange, moving with W), the tool flange (those two, spinning, moving with W) and the balancer (its barrels and
  rods, with the turret). A body turning on q2 (the lower arm, levelling rod 1) or moving with C gives c, its
  centre's z from its pivot, and Ia, It, In about its centre: Ia about Z, It about Y, In about X; one turning on q3
  (the crank, the upper arm, levelling rod 2) gives c along x, Ia about X, It about Y, In about Z; the turret, the
  triangle, the wrist housing, the tool flange and the balancer give off, the centre's (x, z) from the pivot, and
  Izz.
- Write each machine's result to design_office/results/mass_<machine>.json.
- Refuse a machine that is not one of the nine.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Each machine's own new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), made by its own script; masses in kg, lengths in
  mm, moments of inertia in kg m2; the API's cm and kg cm2 inside.
- The joints are each machine's own: the wrist, the flap and the rear axle keep their scripts' As-built joints; the
  robot, the tool changer, the three grippers and the compliance unit have no joints, as-built or otherwise, and
  place their groups by transforms. The weighing adds no joint and moves nothing but the robot, set to q = 0 by its
  own set_pose() for its moving bodies.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the machine, its parts, its mass and centre, its groups, the machine's own first line, and a
  version tag.
- Each of the 9 weighed, its groups' masses summing to its total within 0.1 %, its file written.
- The robot's 11 moving bodies written, each with a mass, each within 10 % of the concept's mass and moments of
  inertia.
- Refused: a machine called crane.
