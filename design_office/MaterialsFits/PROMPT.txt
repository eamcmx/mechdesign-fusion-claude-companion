Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of the nine assemblies and gives every made part the material
design_office/lib/materials_fits.py decides for it, and every fitting face its tolerance class and its roughness,
found on the model and decided by rule.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the assembly, one of the nine of design_office/lib/detail_inventory.py's MACHINES, the bag gripper

DERIVED (from design_office/lib/materials_fits.py, design_office/lib/fusion_joints.py and design_office/lib/detail_inventory.py, never typed):
- build(): the assembly built by its own script, the bag gripper by design_office/StandardParts/StandardParts.py, its
  standard parts detailed
- parts(): each component's kind, number, status, designation and group, read as design_office/lib/detail_inventory.py
  reads them
- records(): the seats of the bought parts on the made ones (a bearing's bore or outside diameter, a dowel's or pin's
  cylinder, on one axis and of one diameter, touching or within 0.2 mm) and the made parts each key touches; the
  cylindrical fits of made parts in made parts
- material(): each made kind's material as the machine's parts list names it (design_office/lib/named_materials.json),
  the default grade where a list names none; FUSION: the entry of Fusion's library that stands for it
- decide(): each seat's and fit's class, roughness and kind of joint

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse an assembly that is not one of the nine.
- Build with the assembly's own script and find the joints with fusion_joints.records(); copy neither, and type no
  class, roughness or material: every one comes from decide() and FUSION.
- A material the library does not hold is reported in the message and the model's material kept, never guessed.
- Give the library's material to every body of every made part; leave the bought parts as their scripts made them.
- Colour each decided face by its kind of joint: bearing seats red, sliding fits blue, located, keyed and pressed fits
  green, dowel holes and key seats black; give it an attribute in the group 'mdfc', named 'fit', whose value is the
  class and the roughness ('k5 Ra 0.8', 'N7 Rz 16.0'). A fit's shaft face carries the shaft's class, its bore the
  hole's; a face the rules give two classes carries both, joined by ' + ', one for each zone of it.
- Weigh the made parts before and after the materials are given.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- The assembly's script makes its own new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType); nothing is moved and no joint is
  added; appearances from Fusion's library ('Paint - Enamel Glossy (Red)' and the others); lengths in mm in the
  records, the API's cm inside.
- The joints are the assembly's own: the wrist's, the flap's and the rear axle's scripts make As-built joints; the
  other six (the robot, the tool changer, the three grippers and the compliance unit) have no joints, as-built or
  otherwise, and place their groups by transforms.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the assembly, how many made parts got their materials and which materials the library lacks, the
  made parts' mass with the library's materials and with the model's generic ones, the faces coloured by kind, and a
  version tag.
- Every made part's bodies carry FUSION's material for its alloy, or the model's where the library lacks it.
- Every seat and fit decide() gives a class carries it in its 'mdfc' 'fit' attribute and its colour; no other face
  carries the attribute.
- The bag gripper: the four 6206-2RS1 seats k5 on the stub shafts and J7 in the aluminium end plates; the lever's hub
  on the bearing's k5 seat; the key seats N9 in the shafts and JS9 in the hubs; the dowel's hole H7.
- The made parts' mass equals the sum, body by body, of the volume times the library's density.
- Refused: an assembly called 'gearbox'.
