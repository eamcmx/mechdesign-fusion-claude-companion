Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of the nine assemblies of
design_office/lib/detail_inventory.py and writes every part's part number and description into its component, so that
a parts list drawn from the model carries them.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the assembly (the robotic wrist, the flap actuation system, the rear axle, the palletizer's robot, the tool changer,
  the clamp gripper, the vacuum gripper, the bag gripper or the compliance unit), the bag gripper

DERIVED (from design_office/lib/detail_inventory.py, never typed):
- MACHINES and SCRIPTS: each assembly's title, its check's parts list and its script; OWN: the designations of names
  that would otherwise mean two parts, or two names for one part
- numbering() and number_map(): each name on the assembly's parts list with its number and description
- kind_of(): the name on the parts list that a component's name stands for

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse an assembly that is not one of the nine.
- Build the assembly with its own script's build() and its defaults; never redraw it.
- A made part's number is the machine's letter (W, F, A, P), the subassembly's two digits (W01, F01, A01; the
  palletizer's robot P01, the tool changer P10, the clamp, vacuum and bag grippers P11 to P13, the compliance unit
  P14) and a three-digit item in the order of the made parts' names; the assembly itself is item 000, written into
  the root component.
- A bought part's number is S and a four-digit serial, one for each designation across the nine assemblies: the
  first eight's in the order of their designations, then the compliance unit's new ones, so that no number given
  moves. Its description is the designation; a name that two assemblies give different parts, or one assembly two
  names for one part, takes the designation OWN gives it. A bearing's rings and balls, a reducer's case and output,
  a slip ring's rotor and stator get the one number of the part they make up.
- A body that comes with a bought part (a cylinder's piston rod, the bearings inside a support unit) gets no number;
  its description says which part it comes with.
- A component whose name is on no parts list (the bag, the station's rollers) is left unnumbered and named in the
  message.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- The assembly's script makes its own new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType); the numbers go into
  Component.partNumber and Component.description, once for each component however many occurrences it has.
- Nothing is moved and no joint is added, as-built or otherwise; no length is used, so the API's cm do not arise.
- The assembly's script is loaded from its folder beside this one as a module; nothing of it is copied.
- The companion's check, design_office/checks/part_numbers_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md says
  (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(key); the dialog's input ids assembly; refusals
  that say 'No assembly'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the assembly, its number, the components numbered, the made kinds with their first and last numbers,
  the bought kinds, the bodies that come with a bought part, the names left unnumbered and a version tag.
- Every component with a body is numbered, or comes with a bought part, or is one of the assembly's non-parts.
- One number for one kind: components of the same kind share a number and no number names two kinds.
- The bag gripper: 13 made kinds, P13-001 to P13-013, 8 bought kinds, 2 piston rods with their cylinders, the bag and
  the rollers left unnumbered; the root P13-000.
- The compliance unit: 6 made kinds, P14-001 to P14-006, 10 bought kinds, its 6 new designations S-0101 to S-0106,
  the housing's and the carriage's plugs one part, S-0102; nothing left unnumbered; the root P14-000.
- Refused: an assembly that is not one of the nine.
