Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of the real assemblies Fusion's static and modal Simulation
studies are run on: the part under study and the neighbours that carry its load, copied out of the machine's own
detailed model into a design of their own, one component each with its material, where they sit in the machine, so
that the study supports and loads the part the way the machine does.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the assembly: one of stop, stop_diagonal, fitting, hinge, arms or arms_closed, stop

DERIVED (never typed):
- design_office/RangeSweep/RangeSweep.py: the bag gripper with its stops, the flap system and the palletizer robot as
  their detail scripts build them, loaded and called, not copied; the robot posed by it
- projects/palletizer/StopBracket/StopBracket.py: the change to the opening bracket, its arm turned 45 deg about its
  own axis, called on the machine's own bracket
- design_office/lib/range_sweep.py: the opening stop's layout, the absorber's axis, the design's push and the DYSC's
  rating
- projects/flap_actuation/lib/flap_design.py: the spar fitting's and the hinges' layout, the actuator's ultimate push,
  the hinges' ultimate load
- design_office/lib/lugs_brackets.py: the opening bracket's hand volume, turned and on its diagonal; the spar web
  patch's
- projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_design.py: the cycle's high point (q2, q3) and the robot's points there: the
  shoulder A, the flange
- projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_links.py: the arms' sideways compliance by hand (lateral()), at the point the
  force acts

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- stop: jaw A's opening bracket with its arm turned 45 deg and its root rounded 2.5 mm, its DYSC-25-25-Y1F, the lock
  nut and the end plate; stop_diagonal: the same with the arm on its diagonal and the same root.
- fitting: the spar fitting, both GE 20 ES and both trunnions, and the rear spar web cut to 200 mm either side of the
  base plate.
- hinge: the right hinge bracket, its rib, the GE 20 ES in its eye, the hinge pin and the flap's two hinge fittings.
- arms: the robot at the cycle's high point; its lower arm without J2's reducer output, its upper arm with its rear
  lever and torque tube but without the pin D's washer and screw, and its wrist from the wrist pin to the tool flange
  without J4's motor and the levelling link: 27 parts, by their names in the robot's model: Fork plate 1, Fork plate
  2, Hub boss J2 side, Hub boss J3 side, Balancer cross pin Q, Lower arm box, Elbow hub, Elbow pin, Upper arm cheek 1,
  Upper arm cheek 2, Bearing 32213 elbow 1, Bearing 32213 elbow 2, Yoke bulkhead, Upper arm box, Front fork cheek 1,
  Front fork cheek 2, Bearing 32213 wrist 1, Bearing 32213 wrist 2, Torque tube, Rear lever, Pin D, Wrist pin, Wrist
  hub, Wrist housing, RV-80N case J4, RV-80N output J4, Tool flange. The robot's model ends the upper arm's box open,
  120 mm short of the wrist pin, beside the front fork's cheeks, which then touch it only along an edge; for the study only, cut the
  box's last 10 mm and put in their place an end plate 10 thick, 340 wide (the cheeks' outer faces) and 200 deep (the
  box's), of the box's steel, which the box's walls and the cheeks' ends both butt on: 28 parts.
- arms_closed: the same 28 parts and the two diaphragms the lower arm's box lacks, for the study only: the box is a
  tube open at both ends, so put a 12 mm plate filling its inside (276 by 196) at its fork end, 650 to 662 mm from
  the shoulder, and another 122 to 110 mm short of the elbow's axis, against the hub, of the box's steel: 30 parts.
- Every body copied from the machine's model, none rebuilt, but those plates; the bolts left out, for the study's
  bolt connectors.
- The faces each study picks found on the bodies: the bolt holes, the fixed faces, the face the load goes on; for
  arms the hub's two bosses (held: their outsides, 280 across, from y 190 to 500 either side of the arms' plane) and
  the tool flange's outer face (30 mm under J4's output; 1000 N across the arms' plane).
- Save nothing; refuse an assembly that is not one of the six.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- a new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType) for the assembly, parametric, each part a component of the root
  with its bodies in a base feature, no joints and no as-built joints (the study's contacts and connectors join
  them); the machine's document closed unsaved; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.
- The companion's check, design_office/checks/study_assemblies_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md says
  (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(which); the dialog's input ids assembly; at
  module level ASSEMBLIES, END_PLATE.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the assembly, each part's bodies, material and volume, the picks found, and a version tag.
- Every part the machine's own volume and material, but the upper arm's box, 10 mm of its 8800 mm2 section shorter,
  and the end plate, 680 000 mm3, and arms_closed's two diaphragms, 649 152 mm3 each; the opening bracket's within
  0.01 % of design_office/lib/lugs_brackets.py's, turned for stop and on its diagonal for stop_diagonal; the web
  patch's within 0.01 % of its hand volume.
- Every pick found on a face.
- The loads: the stop's push of 2180.0 N along the absorber's axis and the DYSC's 4000 N; the fitting's 20 848.4 N
  both ways; the hinge's 13 956.0 N; the arms' 1000 N across, and the give it causes by hand at its point.
- arms: the tool flange where the high point puts it; the hand's give at the flange's outer face from lateral().
- Refused: an assembly called crane.
