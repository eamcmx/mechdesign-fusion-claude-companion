Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the rear axle of a car: a final-drive pinion and ring gear and an
open bevel differential with its two half shafts, assembled and ready to turn with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- final-drive module at the heel m_f (mm), 4
- pinion teeth z1, 17
- ring gear teeth z2, 58
- final-drive face width b_f (mm), 30
- differential module at the heel m_d (mm), 5
- side gear teeth z_s, 22
- spider teeth z_p, 15
- spiders N (count), 2
- differential face width b_d (mm), 20
- pressure angle alpha (deg), 20

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- final drive: ratio z2 / z1; cone angles delta1 = atan(z1 / z2), delta2 = 90 deg - delta1; cone distance
  R_f = (m_f z1 / 2) / sin(delta1); virtual teeth z_v = z / cos(delta); teeth by Tredgold's approximation as in
  Chapter 17 (backlash 0.2 mm on the virtual pitch circle)
- differential: delta_p = atan(z_p / z_s), delta_s = 90 deg - delta_p, R_d = (m_d z_s / 2) / sin(delta_s), the same
  teeth rules
- each gear's blank as in Chapter 17: root cone, back cone, back face 2 mm behind the heel root, a hub of radius
  bore / 2 + 6 mm, 8 mm long; bores: pinion 24.4 mm, spiders 20.4 mm (pin 20 mm), side gears 30.4 mm (half shafts
  30 mm), ring gear the case's outside diameter + 0.4 mm
- the case: a tube from radius (spider hub end + 3 mm) to 8 mm more, closed by two end walls 8 mm thick that start
  1.5 mm outside the side gears' back faces, with holes 0.8 mm larger than the side gears' hubs; N radial pins of
  20 mm from the axis into the tube's wall, one per spider; tube, walls and pins are one body
- assembly: the spider i sits at the angle psi_i = 360 i / N deg about the axle; it can mesh with both side gears
  only if 2 z_s / N is a whole number
- torques for an engine torque of 200 N m in a first gear of 3.5: pinion 700 N m, case 700 z2 / z1, each half
  shaft half of that

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Both pinions' virtual teeth (z1 / cos(delta1) and z_p / cos(delta_p)) at least 2 / sin^2(alpha) = 17.1, or refuse.
- b_f at most R_f / 3 and b_d at most R_d / 3, or refuse.
- N from 2 to 4, and 2 z_s / N a whole number, or refuse giving the number.
- The axle is world Y, the propeller shaft world X; both pitch cone apexes of each pair at the origin. The ring gear
  faces +Y from the -Y side; the pinion lies on +X. Spider 0 lies on +Z.
- Phases: the pinion's tooth 0 points at the ring (-Y) and the ring is turned half a tooth so a gap meets it. The
  left side gear's tooth 0 points at spider 0. Each spider shows the left side gear a gap where the side gear has a
  tooth and a tooth where it has a gap; the right side gear is turned to do the same with spider 0.
- The case turns z1 / z2 of the pinion's angle; the half shafts turn with the case when the side gears do not turn
  in it; turning the left side gear by +d in the case turns the right one by -d and each spider by z_s / z_p of d.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, each created where it belongs with its own z along its axis and its origin at its apex
  (Matrix3D.setWithCoordinateSystem): Housing L and Housing R (tubes round the half shafts, |y| from 60 to 300 mm,
  grounded), Pinion shaft (grounded), Pinion, Ring gear, Case, Spider 1 to N, Side gear L, Side gear R, Half shaft L,
  Half shaft R (30 mm, from |y| = 25 to 300 mm).
- Bevel gears as in Chapter 17: blanks from TemporaryBRepManager cones through a base feature, one tooth lofted from
  heel to toe, joined and patterned.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: revolute for the pinion on its shaft, the case on Housing L, each spider on the case and each side
  gear on the case; rigid for the ring gear to the case and each half shaft to its side gear. Try the joint
  directions and keep the one that turns the part about its axis, measuring the sign (Chapter 17). Motion Links,
  their directions set from the signs: pinion to case at z2 : z1; left side gear to each spider at z_p : z_s; left
  side gear to right side gear at 1 : 1, opposite.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the ratio, the cone angles, both R, the virtual teeth, the torques and a version tag.
- Defaults: ratio 3.4118; delta1 = 16.336 deg, R_f = 120.880 mm, z_v1 = 17.72; delta_p = 34.287 deg,
  R_d = 66.568 mm, z_vp = 18.15; case torque 2388.24 N m, 1194.12 N m per half shaft.
- 12 components with one body each; 6 revolute and 3 rigid As-built Joints; 4 Motion Links.
- No interference at rest.
- One turn of the pinion in 30 deg steps: the case and both half shafts turn 105.517 deg about +Y, measured on the
  bodies; no interference at any step.
- The left side gear turned 30 deg in the case: half shaft L at case + 30 deg, half shaft R at case - 30 deg, each
  spider 44 deg on its pin; no tooth collision while the side gear turns through one tooth pitch in 13 positions.
- Refused: z_p = 12 (z_vp = 13.67); N = 3 (2 z_s / N = 14.67); b_d = 24 (R_d / 3 = 22.19).
