Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the retracting main landing gear of a light aircraft: a leg on
a trunnion and a two-link drag brace that locks it down over centre, assembled and ready to retract with Drive
Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- leg length L, trunnion to axle (mm), 900
- wheel radius Rw (mm), 250
- brace point on the leg b, from the trunnion (mm), 400
- brace pivot on the airframe, forward of the trunnion Ax (mm), 125
- brace pivot on the airframe, above the trunnion Az (mm), 75
- upper brace link a1 (mm), 300
- over-centre angle of the knee when down (deg), 3

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- x forward, z up, the trunnion at the origin, the leg turning forward by alpha (0 = down, 90 deg = up); the brace
  point B = b (sin(alpha), -cos(alpha)); the brace pivot A = (Ax, Az)
- the lower brace link a2: the knee angle is 180 deg - over-centre when the leg is down, so
  |AB|^2 = a1^2 + a2^2 - 2 a1 a2 cos(180 deg - over)
- dead centre: the leg angle aft of down where |AB| = a1 + a2 (bisection); the leg must move that far aft to unlock
- the drive angle of the upper link at A: down (knee on the locked side, the right of A to B), at dead centre (along
  A to B) and up (knee on the fold side, the left of A to B), and the knee's angle when up
- the brace's line passes at a distance from the trunnion; a drag load of 10 000 N at the ground (L + Rw below the
  trunnion) puts P (L + Rw) / distance into the brace
- the torque at A to lift a gear of 300 N at 500 mm from the trunnion: W r_g sin(alpha) d(alpha)/d(theta), its
  largest value during retraction

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- The over-centre angle from 1 to 10 deg, or refuse.
- |AB| must fall steadily through the 90 deg of retraction, or refuse: the brace would have to stretch.
- When up, the knee folded to at least 20 deg, or refuse giving the angle.
- Each part on its own level along y, joined by pins: airframe plate -65 to -25 mm, leg -20 to 20, upper brace 25 to
  35, lower brace 40 to 50 mm. Nothing touches anything else.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created where they belong: Airframe (a plate with the trunnion pin and the brace
  pin; grounded), Leg (with its trunnion boss, the pin at B and the wheel), Upper brace (with the knee pin), Lower
  brace.
- Bodies from TemporaryBRepManager through base features; joint points from an XZ sketch through
  modelToSketchSpace (Chapter 11).
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints, all revolute about y, the direction tried and its sign measured (Chapter 17): the leg on the
  airframe, the upper brace on the airframe, the lower brace on the upper (the knee), the lower brace on the leg
  (closing the loop). No Motion Links.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives a2, the leg's travel aft to unlock, the three drive angles, the knee when up, the brace's
  distance and force, the largest drive torque and a version tag.
- Defaults: a2 = 191.332 mm; the leg 0.0902 deg aft at dead centre; drive angle -105.912 deg down, -104.815 deg at
  dead centre, 22.815 deg up; knee 66.73 deg up; brace line 101.797 mm from the trunnion, 112 970 N in the brace;
  largest drive torque 106.12 N m.
- 4 components with one body each; 4 revolute As-built Joints; no Motion Link.
- No interference at rest.
- The upper brace driven from down to up in steps: the leg at the angle worked out independently at every step,
  measured on its body; aft of down by 0.0902 deg at dead centre, then 90 deg forward; nothing interfering.
- Refused: an over-centre angle of 12 deg; Az = -100 mm (the brace would have to stretch); a1 = 385 mm (the knee
  folded to 16.98 deg).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
