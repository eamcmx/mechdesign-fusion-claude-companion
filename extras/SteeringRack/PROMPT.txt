Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the rack-and-pinion steering of a car's front axle: a pinion, a
rack, two tie rods and two steering knuckles with their wheels, assembled and ready to steer with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- track t, kingpin to kingpin (mm), 1400
- wheelbase L (mm), 2500
- steering arm length a (mm), 150
- steering arm angle beta to the car's axis (deg), 15
- rack position x_r, behind the kingpins (mm), -170
- half spacing of the rack's ball joints c (mm), 300
- rack travel per pinion turn p (mm), 50

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- x forward, y left, z up; kingpins at (0, +-t/2); each steering arm points backwards and inwards at beta, its tip
  at a from the kingpin; the tie rod's length is the distance from the arm tip to the rack's ball joint at rest
- each knuckle's angle delta for a rack travel u: where the arm tip's circle about the kingpin meets the tie rod's
  circle about the ball joint, on the branch of the straight-ahead position
- Ackermann: for an inner angle delta_i the outer wheel should be at atan(1 / (cot(delta_i) + t / L)); the error is
  the outer angle minus that, taken as the worst up to 30 deg of inner steer
- the transmission angle between each tie rod and its arm, its smallest value over that range
- steering ratio at straight ahead: pinion degrees per mean degree of the wheels; turns lock to lock to 30 deg of
  inner steer each way; the pinion's pitch radius p / (2 pi)

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- The Ackermann error at most 1 deg up to 30 deg of inner steer, or refuse giving it.
- The smallest transmission angle at least 35 deg, or refuse giving it.
- Tie rods at least 100 mm long, or refuse giving their length.
- The pinion above the rack, its axis along +x: a turn about +x moves the rack towards +y, which steers the car to
  the right.
- Nothing touches: the rack, the tie rods and the arms on separate levels (z -15 to 15, 15 to 25, 25 to 35 mm), the
  wheels 200 mm outboard of the kingpins.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created where they belong: Frame (cross member, side members, kingpins and the rack's
  support; grounded), Rack (with a pin at each ball joint), Pinion (with the column's stub), Tie rod L, Tie rod R,
  Knuckle L, Knuckle R (boss, arm with a pin at its tip, stub axle and wheel).
- Bodies from TemporaryBRepManager through base features. The pinion's teeth are not drawn: Chapter 16 checked a
  rack's mesh, and here a Motion Link stands for it.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: slider along y for the rack on the frame; revolute about z for each knuckle on its kingpin and for
  each tie rod on its arm tip and on the rack (closing a loop on each side); revolute about x for the pinion, the
  direction tried and its sign measured (Chapter 17). A Motion Link of 360 deg of the pinion for p of the rack, its
  direction set from the measured signs.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the tie rods' length, the rack travel to 30 deg, the worst Ackermann error, the smallest
  transmission angle, the steering ratio and a version tag.
- Defaults: tie rods 362.049 mm; 62.0 mm of rack to 29.721 deg inner (outer 23.971 deg); worst error 0.719 deg;
  smallest transmission angle 35.19 deg; ratio 17.87 : 1; 2.48 turns lock to lock.
- 7 components with one body each; 7 revolute and 1 slider As-built Joints; 1 Motion Link.
- No interference at rest.
- The pinion turned in 36 deg steps to 62 mm of rack each way: the rack at p / 360 per degree and each knuckle at
  the angle worked out independently, measured on the bodies, at every step; nothing interfering at any step.
- Refused: the rule of thumb (arms aimed at the rear axle's centre, beta = 15.642 deg, rack on the arm tips' line at
  x_r = -144.4 mm, c = 400 mm: error 1.080 deg); beta = 12, x_r = -195, c = 400 (transmission angle 30.6 deg);
  c = 640 (tie rods 32.8 mm).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
