Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the bevel-gear differential of a robot wrist as one
complete, jointed assembly: driving the yoke's joint pitches the wrist, driving the output gear's joint rolls it, and
the two side gears follow as they would on the two motors.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- module m at the heel (mm), 2
- teeth z, the same on all four bevel gears (even), 40
- pressure angle α (deg), 20
- face width b (mm), 16
- backlash on the pitch circle (mm), 0.2
- yoke pin diameter d (mm), 8

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- miter gears: pitch cone angle δ = 45°; pitch radius r = m·z/2; cone distance R = r/sin δ; k = (R − b)/R
- Tredgold's approximation: each tooth is the tooth of a virtual spur gear of pitch radius r_v = R·tan δ (z/cos δ teeth)
  drawn at the heel; tip r_v + m, root r_v − 1.25·m, base r_v·cos α, thickness π·m/2 − backlash/2 on the virtual pitch
  circle; below the base circle the flank is a radial line
- heel root point (radius, height from the apex) = (R·sin δ − 1.25·m·cos δ, R·cos δ + 1.25·m·sin δ); toe root = k × it
- back face height z_back = heel root height + 2 mm; hub radius d/2 + 6.2 mm, hub end h_end = z_back + 8 mm
- sweep radius S = √((h_end + 5)² + 18²): no turning part reaches further from the apex

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- z must be even (each gear meets two others 180° apart) and b ≤ R/3: refuse with a message otherwise.
- All four pitch cones share their apex at the origin. Pitch axis = world Y, roll axis = world Z.
  Output gear on +Z, idler on −Z, side gear R on +Y, side gear L on −Y.
- Mesh phase: the side gears point tooth 0 at +Z; the output and the idler point a GAP at +Y (tooth 0 half a tooth
  pitch, π/z, away from +Y).

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- One component per part, one body each: Frame, Yoke, Output gear, Idler gear, Side gear L, Side gear R.
- Create each component WHERE IT BELONGS and never move it afterwards. Each gear component's frame: origin at the
  apex, local z along the gear's axis pointing away from the apex, local x where its tooth 0 points (+Y for the
  output and the idler, +Z for the side gears); matrix.setWithCoordinateSystem(origin, x, y, z) with y = z × x.
- Gear blank, in the gear's own frame, made with TemporaryBRepManager and added through a base feature: the root
  cone from toe root to heel root, the back cone (r·sin δ + h·cos δ = R) from the heel root up to z_back, a flat back
  face, the hub to h_end, and a bore of d + 0.4 mm right through.
- Teeth: the virtual tooth outline lies in the plane tangent to the back cone at the heel: a point (u, v) of it
  (u along the tooth's width, v outward from the virtual pitch circle) is at (R·sin δ + v·cos δ, u, R·cos δ − v·sin δ).
  The toe section is the heel section scaled by k toward the apex. Each section: a sketch on a plane through three of
  its points, the points mapped in with the inverse of the sketch transform, one closed polyline with shared
  SketchPoints. Loft heel to toe, joined to the blank; circular pattern of the loft z times about the gear axis.
- Output gear only: a flange on its hub end, radius 18 mm, 5 mm thick, with the bore.
- Yoke: a cross of two pins of diameter d, through the apex: the roll pin along Z from −h_end to h_end + 5,
  the pitch pin along Y through the side gears and the frame, to 6 mm outside the frame's cheeks.
- Frame: two cheeks 8 mm thick (y from h_end + 4 to h_end + 12 on each side), 50 mm wide in x, from the base plate up
  to z = 25 mm, each with a bore of d + 0.4 mm on the pitch axis; a base plate 6 mm thick, 2·S wide in x, reaching the
  cheeks' outer faces in y, its top at z = −(S + 15). Plate and cheeks are one body.
- The API works in cm and radians: convert every mm and degree input.
- Joints: AsBuiltJoints only. The frame is grounded. The yoke turns on the frame about Y ("Pitch"). The output gear
  turns on the yoke about Z ("Roll"); the idler turns on the yoke about Z; each side gear turns on the yoke about Y.
  A sketch-point joint does not always take the axis you expect: for each revolute joint try the X, Y and Z joint
  directions and keep the one that turns the part about the wanted world axis (turn it 0.2 rad, compare the part's
  rotation), and note its sign (+1 if a positive value turns the part right-handed about that world axis).
- Fusion has no tooth contact: the meshes are Motion Links, all 1:1, from "Roll" to each gear on the yoke. About +Y,
  relative to the yoke, side gear L turns +roll and side gear R −roll; about +Z the idler turns −roll. Set Reverse
  from these signs and the joint signs.
- The companion's check, projects/bevel_differential_wrist/checks/bevelwrist_check.py, drives the script as
  CHECK_INTERFACE.md says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(m, z, alpha, b,
  backlash, d), the dialog's input ids the same.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message at the end gives z, m, R and a version tag.
- "Pitch" 30°: the yoke and all four gears turn 30° about Y, and the output does not turn on the yoke.
- "Roll" 40°: the output turns 40° on the yoke; side L +40°, side R −40° and the idler −40° on the yoke.
- Pitch 30° with roll 40°: the side gears' world angles about +Y are θL = 70° and θR = −10°, so
  pitch = (θL + θR)/2 = 30° and roll = (θL − θR)/2 = 40°.
- No two parts overlap at rest, at (pitch, roll) = (30°, 40°) and (−45°, 120°), nor while the roll goes through one
  tooth pitch (360/z) in 6 steps.
- z = 30 is refused (b = 16 > R/3); z = 41 is refused (odd).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
