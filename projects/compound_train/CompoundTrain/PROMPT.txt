Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a two-stage reverted compound spur gear train on a base plate:
input and output gears turn on the same fixed spindle, a cluster gear on a countershaft carries the two middle gears,
and the train is assembled and ready to turn with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- module of the first stage m1 (mm), 1.5
- module of the second stage m2 (mm), 2
- input gear teeth z1, 20
- first cluster gear teeth z2, 60
- second cluster gear teeth z3, 20
- face width b (mm), 10
- shaft diameter d_s (mm), 8

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- centre distance a = m1 (z1 + z2) / 2; the output gear's teeth z4 = 2 a / m2 - z3, so that the second stage has
  the same centre distance (a reverted train: input and output on one axis)
- ratio of the train, input speed over output speed: (z2 / z1) * (z4 / z3); the output turns the same way as the
  input (two external meshes)
- contact ratio of each stage (the length of action over the base pitch), pressure angle 20 degrees
- levels: stage 1 from z = 1 to 1 + b above the plate, stage 2 from z = 3 + b to 3 + 2b

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- z4 must come out a whole number, or refuse, giving m1 (z1 + z2) / m2.
- Every gear at least 18 teeth (fewer undercut at 20 degrees), or refuse.
- Each driven gear is turned so that a tooth of its driver sits in one of its gaps on the line of centres.
- Gears turning at different speeds never touch; the gap between the two stages is filled by a spacer on the
  spindle and by the cluster's hub on the countershaft.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created where they belong: Base plate (grounded), top face at z = 0; Spindle at the
  origin and Countershaft at (a, 0), shafts of d_s standing on the plate, 3 mm taller than stage 2; Gear 1 (z1) on
  the spindle at stage 1; Cluster on the countershaft: gear z2 at stage 1, gear z3 at stage 2 and a hub of
  1.6 d_s between them, one body; Spacer on the spindle between the stages, outside diameter 1.6 d_s; Gear 4 (z4)
  on the spindle at stage 2. Every gear bore d_s + 0.2 mm.
- Each tooth outline is one closed polyline of sketch lines sharing their points; the API works in cm and radians.
- Joints, As-built only: rigid for Spindle and Countershaft to Base plate and Spacer to Spindle; revolute about z
  for Gear 1 and Gear 4 on Spindle and Cluster on Countershaft. Two Motion Links, one per mesh, each the opposite
  way at the ratio of its teeth.
- The companion's check, projects/compound_train/checks/compound_train_check.py, drives the script as
  CHECK_INTERFACE.md says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(m1, m2, z1, z2, z3, b,
  ds), the dialog's input ids the same; the joints 'Cluster turns', 'Gear 1 turns', 'Gear 4 turns'; the components
  'Cluster', 'Gear 1', 'Gear 4'; at module level train(); refusals that say '= 68.57 teeth', 'undercut'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives a, z4, the ratio, each stage's contact ratio and a version tag.
- Defaults: a = 60 mm, z4 = 40, ratio 6 (3 x 2), contact ratios 1.671 and 1.635.
- Gear 1 driven 90 degrees: the cluster turns -30 degrees and Gear 4 turns +15 degrees, by their joints and by
  their bodies.
- No interference at rest, and no tooth collision while Gear 1 turns through one tooth pitch (18 degrees).
- z3 = 24 gives z4 = 36 and a ratio of 4.5 with nothing else edited.
- Refused: m2 = 1.75 (z3 + z4 would be 68.57 teeth); z3 = 12 (undercut).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
