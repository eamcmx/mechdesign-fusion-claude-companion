Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a single-disc cycloidal drive, assembled so that Drive Joints on
the input shaft turns the disc on its eccentric and the output flange at the reduced speed.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- number of ring pins N_p (count), 11
- pin circle radius R (mm), 30
- ring pin diameter (mm), 6
- eccentricity e (mm), 1.5
- disc thickness t (mm), 8

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- disc lobes N_d = N_p - 1; ratio -N_d (the output turns the other way, 1 / N_d of the input)
- the disc's outline: the curve the centre of one ring pin traces in the disc's frame while the input turns N_d
  times (the disc's centre at e (cos theta, sin theta), the disc turned by -theta / N_d), offset towards the
  disc's centre by the pin radius plus a gap of 0.1 mm; 72 points per input turn, one closed polyline
- the outline's radius then runs from R - r_pin - 0.1 - e to R - r_pin - 0.1 + e
- the disc turns -theta / N_d absolutely, -theta (N_d + 1) / N_d relative to the eccentric
- output: 6 pins of 5 mm on a circle of radius 17 mm; their holes in the disc 5 + 2 e + 0.2 mm across
- eccentric: radius 10 mm about the point e from the input's axis; the disc's bore 0.1 mm larger in radius

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- N_p from 6 to 40, or refuse.
- The pins must not overlap on their circle: 2 R sin(pi / N_p) > pin diameter + 1 mm, or refuse.
- The curve of the pin's centre must not loop: K1 = e N_p / R must be less than 1, or refuse giving K1.
- The drawn outline's straight chords cut across the valleys of the curve: measure how near they come to the pin's
  path (sampled on and between the outline's points, less the pin's radius) and refuse an outline that comes within
  half the gap, 0.05 mm, saying how near.
- At theta = 0 the disc's centre is at (e, 0) and a lobe valley faces ring pin 0 at (R, 0).

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created at the origin with their geometry drawn in place: Housing (grounded): a plate
  from z = -6 to 0 with a bore for the input, and the N_p ring pins standing on it to z = t + 2; Input: an 8 mm
  shaft from z = -6 to t + 8 with the eccentric from z = 1 to 1 + t; Disc from z = 1 to 1 + t; Output: a flange
  from z = t + 2 to t + 6 with its 6 pins down to z = 1.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: Input revolute about z on Housing at the origin; Disc revolute about z on Input at the eccentric's
  centre; Output revolute about z on Housing at the origin. Motion Links: Input to Disc, N_d x 360 deg of the input
  for (N_d + 1) x 360 deg of the disc, reversed; Input to Output, N_d x 360 deg for 360 deg, reversed.
- The companion's check, projects/cycloidal_drive/checks/cycloidal_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(n_p, R, d_pin, e, t), the dialog's input
  ids the same; the joint 'Input turns'; the components 'Disc', 'Housing', 'Output'; at module level cycloid(),
  disc_outline(), least_gap(); refusals that say 'overlap', 'K1 = e N_p / R = 1.100', 'valleys are too sharp'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives N_d, the ratio, K1, the outline's smallest and largest radius, the gap and a version tag.
- Defaults: N_d = 10, ratio -10, K1 = 0.550, outline from 25.4 to 28.4 mm, the chords at worst 0.095 mm from a pin;
  Fusion's minimum distance from the disc to the housing's pins the same, within 0.005 mm.
- The input stepped one turn, 30 deg at a time: the output turns -3 deg per step, -36 deg in all; the disc's
  centre stays e from the axis.
- No interference at any of the 12 steps.
- Refused: N_p = 40 with R = 30 (pins overlap: 2 R sin(pi / 40) = 4.71 mm); e = 3 (K1 = 1.100); e = 2.5 (the
  chords 0.105 mm into a pin).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
