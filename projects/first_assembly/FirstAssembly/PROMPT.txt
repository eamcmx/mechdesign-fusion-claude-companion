Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a block with a shaft standing in a hole through it, assembled
so that the shaft can turn with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- block length L_b (mm), 60
- block width W_b (mm), 40
- block height H_b (mm), 20
- shaft diameter d (mm), 10
- shaft length L_s (mm), 50
- shaft position x (mm), 15
- shaft position y (mm), 0

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- the block is centred on the origin in x and y, its top face at z = 0, its bottom at z = -H_b
- hole diameter d + 0.2 (0.1 mm clearance on the radius), through the block at (x, y)
- the shaft stands in the hole from z = -H_b up to z = L_s - H_b

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- At least 3 mm of block material all round the hole: |x| + (d + 0.2)/2 + 3 <= L_b/2 and
  |y| + (d + 0.2)/2 + 3 <= W_b/2. Otherwise refuse with a message, before anything is drawn.
- The shaft must be longer than the block is high (L_s > H_b), or refuse.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Two components, one body each: Block and Shaft. The Block is grounded.
- Create the Shaft component WHERE IT BELONGS, at (x, y, 0), with the translation in the matrix given to
  addNewComponent. Never move it afterwards: a later feature undoes the move in a parametric design.
- Draw the shaft in its own component at its own origin; the API works in cm, so convert every mm input.
- Join with an As-built Joint (not a Joint): revolute, the Shaft turning on the Block about z through the shaft's
  centre.
- The companion's check, projects/first_assembly/checks/first_assembly_check.py, drives the script as
  CHECK_INTERFACE.md says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(L_b, W_b, H_b, d, L_s,
  x, y), the dialog's input ids the same; the joint 'Shaft turns'; the components 'Block', 'Shaft'; refusals that say
  'Only 2.9 mm', 'longer than the block'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message at the end gives the shaft's position, the two volumes and a version tag.
- With the defaults: the shaft is at (15, 0) mm; its volume is pi * 5^2 * 50 = 3927.0 mm^3; the block's volume is
  60 * 40 * 20 - pi * 5.1^2 * 20 = 46365.7 mm^3.
- No interference between the two parts.
- Driving the joint 90 degrees turns the shaft 90 degrees about z and leaves the block where it is.
- x = 22 is refused (only 30 - 22 - 5.1 = 2.9 mm of wall left).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
