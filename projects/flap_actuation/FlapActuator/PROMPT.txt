Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the electromechanical actuator of one trailing-edge flap of a
light twin: a ball screw turned through a spur gear, an idler and a pinion by a bought motor unit (brushless motor,
planetary gearhead, spring-applied brake) pushes a rod out of an aluminium housing that hangs from the rear spar on
two trunnions, and the rod ends in a rod end on the flap's horn. Assembled from real parts, ready to move with its
joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the screw's operating factor f_p (HIWIN F 3.8), 3.0
- the gearhead's ratio, 24

DERIVED (from projects/flap_actuation/lib/flap_design.py, never typed):
- actuator_design(): the force over the stroke and its cube mean; the nut's life, static rating and the largest f_p
  it passes; the screw's torque and its back-driving torque; buckling and critical speed; the thrust bearings'
  life; the gears' stresses; the motor unit's speed, torque, power and brake; the rod as a column; the trunnions
- the positions of LAY: the actuator along its own axis from the gimbal

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Nut HIWIN R32-10T4-RSI on a 32 x 10 screw; thrust bearings two SKF BSD 2562 CGA back to back; rod end SKF SI 20 ES;
  idler bearings two 6201; gears module 1.5, 20 deg, 54 on the screw, 30 on the idler, 18 on the pinion, 26 on the
  encoder's pinion, 14 mm wide.
- Refuse a nut that lasts less than 60 000 strokes, a nut whose static rating is less than the ultimate force,
  gears under 1.5 against yield at the ultimate or in fatigue at the limit, a rod that buckles under twice the
  ultimate force, a motor faster than 6000 rev/min; say by how much.
- The rod end's centre 378.96 mm from the gimbal's axis retracted and 461.04 mm extended.
- Nothing more than 25 mm forward of the gimbal's axis (the spar's fitting); the actuator inside the wing's skins
  with the flap anywhere from 0 to 40 deg.
- The guide key on the rod runs in a slot along the housing's bore, to the gland.
- Inside the rod's sleeve (LAY's sleeve, r_sleeve): the ball nut on LAY's nut, held against the sleeve's end by a
  retaining ring in a groove from y = 69 to 71, and keyed by the 6 x 6.5 x 32 feather key of RSI_KEY at the nut's
  middle on -Z, 3.5 mm in the nut's groove and 3 mm in the sleeve. The guide key 6 mm wide from y = 80 to 140 on +Z,
  sunk 1 mm into the sleeve (from z = 29.5) and standing to z = 34 in the housing's slot.
- Nothing touches anything else, retracted, part way or extended.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). The actuator along +Y from the gimbal's axis (y = 0) to the
  rod end, Z up, X along the span.
- Six subassemblies: Housing (grounded), Screw, Rod, Idler, Pinion, Encoder pinion; every part a component.
- As-built Joints: the screw, the idler, the pinion and the encoder's pinion revolute about their axes; the rod a
  slider along Y, limited to the stroke. Motion Links: 54:30 to the idler and 54:26 to the encoder's pinion, each
  turning the other way; 54:18 to the pinion, the same way; one turn of the screw moves the rod 10 mm aft.
- The ball groove is the one common/BallScrewAxis/BallScrewAxis.py cuts: the section swept along the screw with one
  turn of twist per lead.
- The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the nut, its life and static margin, the torques, the gears' factors, the motor unit and the brake,
  the rod end's position, the parts list and a version tag. Defaults: life 4.27 of 60 000 strokes, f_p up to 4.87,
  C0 3.41 of the ultimate force, 23.71 N m, 30.80 N m backwards, factors 9.73 and 4.91, 2955 rev/min, 0.428 N m.
- 43 components in 31 kinds, by component name and count: Actuator housing 1, Ball nut R32-10T4-RSI 1, Ball screw
  32x10 1, Bearing 6201 2, Bearing BSD 2562 CGA 2, Cover screw M5x16 6, Encoder 1, Encoder pinion z26 1, Encoder shaft
  1, End bolt M8x20 1, End stop 1, Gear cover 1, Gearhead shaft 1, Guide key 1, Idler gear z30 1, Idler pin 1, Jam nut
  M20x1.5 1, Key 8x7x16 1, Motor screw M5x12 4, Motor unit 1, Nut key 6x6.5x32 1, Nut retaining ring 1, Pinion z18 1,
  Piston rod 1, Ring nut M66x1.5 1, Rod end SI 20 ES 1, Rod seal 1, Screw gear z54 1, Stop bolt M8x16 1, Trunnion 2,
  Washer M8 2.
- No interference retracted and with the flap at 10, 20, 30 and 40 deg.
- The rod end's centre 378.96 mm from the gimbal retracted and 461.04 mm extended, the screw turned 8.2084 turns.
- At least 2 mm of room to the skins above and below; the mass, with the motor unit at its specified 1.6 kg, at
  most 9 kg.
- Refused: f_p 5.0.
