Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the flap actuation system of
projects/flap_actuation/FlapSystem/FlapSystem.py in detail: the motor unit drawn to the catalogue products its
specification is now written against, the pinion keyed on the gearhead's shaft as that shaft is made, the bearing
seats on the hinge pins and the horn pin split out as the zones that are hardened, and every made part's material and
every fitting face's class and roughness as design_office/lib/materials_fits.py decides them.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the gearhead's ratio, one of the two-stage GP 52 C's ratios, 26

DERIVED (from projects/flap_actuation/lib/flap_detail.py, projects/flap_actuation/lib/flap_design.py and design_office/MaterialsFits/MaterialsFits.py, never typed):
- numbers(i): the motor's point at that ratio, its speed at 28 V, its current, the brake it needs, the bus current
- MOTOR, GEARHEAD, OVERALL, SPEC, PIN: maxon's figures, the catalogue's overall length, Table ch33motor's envelope,
  the pinion's layout along the actuator's axis
- pinion_force(), pinion_rim(), pinion_key(), hinge_zones()
- MaterialsFits.apply(): the materials and the fits on the open design

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build with FlapSystem.py's own build() and apply MaterialsFits.py's apply(); copy neither, nor
  projects/flap_actuation/FlapActuator/FlapActuator.py. Change the parts by replacing, before the build, the functions
  that draw them, each replacement recognising only the calls it means and passing every other through: the body adder
  for the motor unit's gearhead, motor and brake, the gear for the pinion, the part for the gearhead's shaft, the
  cylinder that cuts the shaft's hole through the housing's back wall. Restore every replaced function after the
  build.
- The hole through the housing's back wall for the gearhead's shaft opened from 14 to 17 across, so that the key's
  corner, 7.76 from the axis, passes it.
- The motor unit behind its flange (6 thick from the housing's back face): the GP 52 C 52 across and 65 long, the
  EC-i 52 to maxon's overall 145.1 with it, the brake 52 across in what is left of the specification's 176.
- The gearhead's shaft 12 across from the flange's face 29.5 forward, its keyseat 2.5 deep with maxon's 4 x 4 x 20
  key 2 mm from its end, its centre hole for an M4; the pinion with a hub 18 across forward to the shaft's end and a
  keyway 4 wide, 1.8 over the bore, through hub and teeth; nothing behind the pinion to clamp it against, so its hub
  starts 0.5 mm behind the shaft's end at maxon's shortest shaft, and an end washer 18 across and 2 thick, clamped on
  the shaft's end by an M4 x 10 cap screw in the shaft's centre hole, overlaps the hub and keeps the pinion on.
- The hinge pins' and the horn pin's cylinders split across at the edges of the ring that sits on them (16 wide) and
  the faces between marked as hardened. The horn pin's seat h6 Rz 10, the pinion's bore K7 and its keyway JS9, by
  hand, the census not reaching them; every other class as design_office/lib/materials_fits.py decides it.
- Refuse a ratio maxon does not make, and one at which the motor would exceed its continuous torque or the speed it
  reaches at 28 V.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- FlapSystem's own build() makes the new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType) and its groups, with the flap's
  As-built revolute joint and the actuator's own joints and motion links; nothing moved; lengths in mm, the API's cm
  inside.
- The companion's check, projects/flap_actuation/checks/flap_detail_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(i_gh), the dialog's input ids the same; at
  module level BESIDE, HARD, KEYED, KEYWAY, SEAT, pose(); refusals that say 'falls short'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the unit's length and the brake's, the key in the hub, the motor's point and current, the brake,
  the pinion's force against the gearhead's rating, the rim factor, the zones and the faces given, and a version tag.
- The motor unit's bodies: the flange 6, the gearhead 65, the motor 80.1, the brake 24.9 long, all 52 across but the
  flange, ending 176 from the flange's face.
- The gearhead's shaft 12 across from 10.5 to 46 mm, the key 20 long in it, the pinion's hub 18 across from 12.0,
  the end washer on the shaft's end at 10.5, the M4 x 10 through it, 8 mm of it in the shaft.
- Three zones hardened, each 16 long and centred on its ring; the faces' classes as above.
- No interference among the system's parts and the actuator's outside at 0, 10, 20, 30 and 40 deg.
- No interference inside the actuator, the flap up: the key clear of the housing's back wall.
- Refused: the 21:1 gearhead, at which the motor would give 478 mN m over its 438 continuous.
