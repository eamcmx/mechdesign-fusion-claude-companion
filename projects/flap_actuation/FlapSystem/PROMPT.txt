Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one flap's actuation system as one machine: the actuator of
projects/flap_actuation/FlapActuator/FlapActuator.py hung on its trunnions in a fitting on the rear spar, its rod end
pinned in the fork of a horn on the flap's middle, and the flap on two hinge brackets, with the wing's spar and skins
drawn from the brief's section, so that the flap can be posed anywhere from 0 to 40 deg with the actuator following
it.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the screw's operating factor f_p, 3.0, passed to the actuator

DERIVED (from projects/flap_actuation/lib/flap_design.py, never typed):
- system_design(): the flap's forces at each angle, the hinges' reaction, the horn's root, the pins as beams, the
  cheeks, eyes and lugs (Shigley Section 8-12), the brackets, their bolts and the ribs, the fitting and its bolts
- linkage(): the actuator's angle about its gimbal and its rod's travel for each flap angle; SYS: the positions

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build the actuator with FlapActuator.py's own functions, loaded, not copied: its housing(), screw(), rod() and
  train() each build their subassembly into the component they are given (here the Actuator), and its revolute_y(),
  slider_y() and link() make the joints and Motion Links in the root component between the Actuator occurrence's
  childOccurrences. Its build() opens a document of its own and is not called. Draw nothing of it twice.
- Refuse what the actuator refuses, and the horn's root or cheeks, a pin, a bracket or its eye, or a lug of the
  fitting under 2 against yield at the ultimate load, saying which and by how much.
- The hinge line on X, along the span, the flap's middle at x = 0; Y aft, Z up; the gimbal at (y, z) = (-420, 114).
- Two hinges, 1100 mm either side of the middle, each a bracket bolted to a trailing-edge rib inside the wing and out
  through the cove to an SKF GE 20 ES on the hinge line; the rib 6 mm thick at the bolts.
- The spar's fitting: a base plate on the spar's aft face (455 mm forward of the hinge) and two lugs with SKF GE 20 ES
  bearings on the trunnions, 64 to 76 mm from the actuator's axis.
- The horn: a fork whose root fills a slot in the flap's nose, its cheeks 16.2 to 26.2 mm from the middle, a spacer
  each side of the rod end so that the horn's cheeks stand clear of its shank; the pin 20 mm.
- The pins a clearance fit in their 20 mm bores. Nothing touches anything else at 0, 10, 20, 30 or 40 deg.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Three subassemblies: Wing (grounded), Actuator (with its own
  six), Flap.
- The flap turns on an As-built revolute joint about X. The actuator is placed by its occurrence's transform, turned
  about the gimbal's axis by the exact angle: Fusion holds a joint's angle to 0.1 deg and the actuator swings less
  than 1 deg. Its rod slides on its own joint, and its Motion Links turn the screw and the gears.
- pose(delta) sets the flap, the actuator and the rod from the linkage; the loop is closed by the numbers, and the
  check measures it.
- The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the hinges' load, the horn's stress and factor, the pin's, the brackets' and the lugs' stresses, the
  parts list and a version tag. Defaults: hinges 13 956 N each, horn 144.1 MPa (3.76), pin 228.3 MPa.
- 85 components in 51 kinds, by component name and count: Actuator housing 1, Ball nut R32-10T4-RSI 1, Ball screw
  32x10 1, Bearing 6201 2, Bearing BSD 2562 CGA 2, Bearing GE 20 ES fitting 2, Bearing GE 20 ES hinge 2, Bracket bolt
  M8x25 4, Bracket nut M8 4, Cover screw M5x16 6, Encoder 1, Encoder pinion z26 1, Encoder shaft 1, End bolt M8x20 1,
  End stop 1, Fitting bolt M8x35 4, Fitting nut M8 4, Flap panel 1, Gear cover 1, Gearhead shaft 1, Guide key 1, Hinge
  bracket 2, Hinge fitting 4, Hinge pin 2, Hinge pin nut 2, Hinge rib 2, Horn 1, Horn pin 1, Horn pin nut 1, Idler
  gear z30 1, Idler pin 1, Jam nut M20x1.5 1, Key 8x7x16 1, Lower skin 1, Motor screw M5x12 4, Motor unit 1, Nut key
  6x6.5x32 1, Nut retaining ring 1, Pinion z18 1, Piston rod 1, Rear spar web 1, Ring nut M66x1.5 1, Rod end SI 20 ES
  1, Rod seal 1, Screw gear z54 1, Spacer 2, Spar fitting 1, Stop bolt M8x16 1, Trunnion 2, Upper skin 1, Washer M8 2.
- At 0, 10, 20, 30 and 40 deg: the rod end's centre within 0.01 mm of the horn's pin; the transmission angle
  measured in Fusion 69.81 deg flap up and 70.15 deg at 40; no interference among the system's parts and the
  actuator's outer ones.
- Both hinges' bearings on the hinge line.
- Refused: f_p 5.0.
