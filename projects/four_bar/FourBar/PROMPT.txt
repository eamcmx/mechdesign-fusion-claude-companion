Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a four-bar crank-rocker linkage on a base plate, assembled as one
closed loop of revolute joints so that Drive Joints on the crank swings the rocker.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- crank length a (mm), 15
- coupler length b (mm), 55
- rocker length c (mm), 50
- ground length d (mm), 55

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- pivots A = (0, 0) for the crank and D = (d, 0) for the rocker; at crank angle theta2 the crank's end
  B = a (cos theta2, sin theta2); the rocker's end C where the circle of radius b round B meets the circle of
  radius c round D, on the upper branch (the open linkage)
- the rocker angle theta4 = atan2(C_y, C_x - d); its swing over a full turn of the crank
- the transmission angle mu, between coupler and rocker: cos(mu) = (b^2 + c^2 - BD^2) / (2 b c) with
  BD^2 = a^2 + d^2 - 2 a d cos(theta2); its smallest and largest values (at theta2 = 0 and 180 deg)

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Grashof: s + l <= p + q for the shortest s, longest l and the other two p, q, and the crank the shortest link,
  so that the crank turns fully, or refuse.
- The transmission angle, the acute angle between coupler and rocker, never below 40 deg: mu(0) and
  180 deg - mu(180 deg) both at least 40 deg, or refuse giving it.
- Built at theta2 = 0, on the upper branch.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created at the origin, drawn in place: Ground (grounded), a plate from z = -6 to 0;
  Crank from A to B, z 0 to 4; Coupler from B to C, z 4 to 8; Rocker from D to C, z 8 to 12. Each link a bar 8 mm
  wide with round ends (two arcs and two lines sharing their sketch points), a 3 mm hole at each end. The pins are
  not drawn.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints, all revolute about z: Crank on Ground at A, Rocker on Ground at D, Coupler on Crank at B, Coupler
  on Rocker at C (the last one closes the loop).
- The companion's check, projects/four_bar/checks/four_bar_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md says
  (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(a, b, c, d), the dialog's input ids the same;
  the joint 'Crank turns'; the component 'Rocker'; at module level linkage(); refusals that say 'Not a crank-rocker',
  'falls to 38.57 deg'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives theta4 at the start, the rocker's swing, the transmission angle's range and a version tag.
- Defaults: theta4 = 105.590 deg at the start; the rocker swings 39.007 deg, from 96.525 to 135.532 deg; the
  transmission angle from 44.47 to 83.48 deg.
- The crank stepped through one turn, 30 deg at a time: at every step the rocker's body at theta4(theta2), as
  Freudenstein's equation gives it (worked independently of the circle intersection); no interference.
- Refused: a = 60 (the crank is not the shortest link); a = 20 (transmission angle 38.57 deg); a, b, c, d = 20, 38,
  40, 55 (31.90 deg at theta2 = 180 deg).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
