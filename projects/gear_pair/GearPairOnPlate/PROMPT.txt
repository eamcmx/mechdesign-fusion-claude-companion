Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a parametric spur gear pair on a base plate, assembled and ready to turn with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- module m (mm), 2
- driver teeth z1, 20
- driven teeth z2, 40
- pressure angle α (deg), 20
- face width b (mm), 10
- backlash on the pitch circle (mm), 0.2
- shaft diameter d_s (mm), 8
- base plate thickness (mm), 6

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- pitch radius r = m·z/2, base radius r_b = r·cos α, tip radius r + m, root radius r − 1.25·m
- centre distance a = m·(z1 + z2)/2
- tooth thickness on the pitch circle = π·m/2 − backlash/2 for each gear
- the fewest teeth without undercut z_min = 2/sin²α, rounded up

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- True involute flanks starting at the base circle. Where the base circle is above the root circle, continue
  each flank with a radial line down to the root circle.
- z1 and z2 at least z_min, 18 at 20° (fewer teeth undercut): refuse with a message.
- The driven gear is turned so that a tooth of the driver sits in a gap of the driven gear on the line of centres.
- Ratio z2/z1; the driven gear turns the opposite way to the driver.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components: Base plate (grounded), Shaft 1, Shaft 2, Gear 1, Gear 2. One body each. Gear 1 at the origin,
  gear 2 at (a, 0); the shafts stand on the plate, the gears sit on the shafts above the plate.
- Each tooth outline is ONE closed polyline of sketch lines that share their SketchPoint objects (no splines).
- The API works in cm and radians: convert every mm and degree input.
- Place every part where it belongs, then join with AsBuiltJoints (not Joints): rigid between the plate and each
  shaft, revolute about the shaft axis between each gear and its shaft.
- A Motion Link between the two revolute joints, so that driving gear 1 turns gear 2 by z1/z2 of the angle,
  the opposite way.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message box at the end gives the centre distance, the ratio and a version tag.
- With the defaults, driving gear 1's joint 90° turns gear 2 by 45° the other way.
- At rest the two gears do not overlap anywhere (Interference finds nothing).
- Changing z2 to 50 in the dialog gives a centre distance of 70 mm with nothing else edited.
- z1 = 12 is refused (fewer than 18 teeth undercut).

A WORKED EXAMPLE of the polyline I mean (it works in my Fusion; P3 = adsk.core.Point3D.create, points in cm):

def _polyline(sk, pts):
    lines = sk.sketchCurves.sketchLines
    first = lines.addByTwoPoints(P3(pts[0][0], pts[0][1], 0), P3(pts[1][0], pts[1][1], 0))
    prev = first.endSketchPoint
    for q in pts[2:]:
        prev = lines.addByTwoPoints(prev, P3(q[0], q[1], 0)).endSketchPoint
    lines.addByTwoPoints(prev, first.startSketchPoint)
