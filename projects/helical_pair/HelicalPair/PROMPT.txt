Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a parametric pair of helical (or herringbone) gears on a base plate,
assembled and ready to turn with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- normal module m_n (mm), 2
- driver teeth z1, 20
- driven teeth z2, 40
- helix angle beta (deg), 20
- normal pressure angle alpha_n (deg), 20
- face width b (mm), 12
- herringbone (one of no, yes), no

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- transverse module m_t = m_n / cos(beta); transverse pressure angle alpha_t = atan(tan(alpha_n) / cos(beta))
- pitch radius r = m_t z / 2, base radius r cos(alpha_t), tip radius r + m_n, root radius r - 1.25 m_n
- centre distance a = m_t (z1 + z2) / 2
- turn of a tooth over the face width, at the pitch circle: theta = b tan(beta) / r (gear 1 one way, gear 2 the other)
- smallest number of teeth without undercut: z_min = 2 cos(beta) / sin^2(alpha_t)
- transverse contact ratio (the length of action over the base pitch, with m_t and alpha_t) and overlap ratio b
  sin(beta) / (pi m_n)
- tooth thickness on the pitch circle, transverse: pi m_t / 2 - 0.1 mm (backlash 0.2 mm)
- Fusion keeps a sweep's twist in tenths of a degree. So the face width is adjusted to b', the nearest width that
  makes gear 1's twist a whole number of tenths (two tenths for a herringbone, each half then a whole number):
  b' = theta1' r1 / tan(beta); every value above is then worked out with b'. Read back the twist Fusion stored.

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Gear 1 right-handed, gear 2 left-handed: two external helical gears mesh only with opposite hands.
- True involute flanks in the transverse plane, starting at the base circle, with a radial line below it.
- z1 and z2 at least z_min, or refuse giving z_min.
- Helix angle from 5 to 45 degrees, or refuse.
- The driven gear is turned so that a tooth of the driver sits in a gap of the driven gear on the line of centres.
- Herringbone: the face width in two halves of opposite hand, the teeth meeting at the middle (draw the upper half
  from the twist Fusion stored for the lower one).
- Gear 2's twist per sweep, kept in tenths of a degree, must not move its tips by more than a quarter of the
  backlash, or refuse.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components: Base plate (grounded), Shaft 1, Shaft 2, Gear 1, Gear 2, one body each; gear 1 at the origin, gear 2 at
  (a, 0). The plate from z = -6 to 0; each gear 1 mm above it, from z = 1 to z = 1 + b.
- Each tooth: the transverse outline as ONE closed polyline of sketch lines sharing their points, swept along the
  gear's axis with a twist angle (right hand: the teeth turn anticlockwise, seen from above, as z rises); then a
  circular pattern of the swept tooth.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: rigid between the plate and each shaft, revolute about z between each gear and its shaft; a Motion
  Link between the revolute joints, the opposite way, at the ratio of the teeth.
- The companion's check, projects/helical_pair/checks/helical_pair_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(mn, z1, z2, beta, alpha_n, b,
  herringbone), the dialog's input ids the same; the joints 'Gear 1 turns', 'Gear 2 turns'; the components 'Gear 1',
  'Gear 2'; at module level helical(); refusals that say 'z_min = 2 cos(beta) / sin^2(alpha_t) = 14.41', 'from 5 to
  45'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives m_t, alpha_t, a, b', theta for each gear, z_min, both contact ratios and a version tag.
- Defaults: m_t = 2.12836 mm, alpha_t = 21.173 deg, a = 63.851 mm, b' = 12.043 mm, theta1 = 11.800 deg,
  theta2 = 5.900 deg, z_min = 14.41, transverse contact ratio 1.504, overlap ratio 0.656.
- The tips of gear 1's teeth at the top face are turned +11.800 deg from those at the bottom, gear 2's -5.900 deg.
- At rest no interference; gear 1 driven 90 deg turns gear 2 -45 deg; no tooth collision through one tooth pitch.
- The same with herringbone = yes.
- Refused: z1 = 14 (z_min = 14.41); beta = 50.

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
