Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a shaft turning in a bearing block, with a hub fixed to the shaft
by a DIN 6885 parallel key, assembled so that Drive Joints on the shaft turns shaft, key and hub together.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- shaft diameter D (mm), 20
- shaft length L_shaft (mm), 80
- hub length L_hub (mm), 25
- hub wall t_wall (mm), 8
- key length L_key (mm), 18
- torque to transmit T (N.m), 30
- allowable shear stress in the key tau_allow (MPa), 60
- allowable crushing stress on the key's sides sigma_allow (MPa), 100

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- key width b, key height h, shaft slot depth t1, hub slot depth t2 from DIN 6885 for D:
    D above 10 up to 12: b 4, h 4, t1 2.5, t2 1.8
    D above 12 up to 17: b 5, h 5, t1 3.0, t2 2.3
    D above 17 up to 22: b 6, h 6, t1 3.5, t2 2.8
    D above 22 up to 30: b 8, h 7, t1 4.0, t2 3.3
    D above 30 up to 38: b 10, h 8, t1 5.0, t2 3.3
    D above 38 up to 44: b 12, h 8, t1 5.0, t2 3.3
    D above 44 up to 50: b 14, h 9, t1 5.5, t2 3.8
    D above 50 up to 58: b 16, h 10, t1 6.0, t2 4.3
- hub bore D + 0.1 (0.05 mm clearance on the radius); hub outside diameter D + 2 * t_wall
- force on the key at the shaft's surface F = 2 * T / D, with T in N.mm (1000 x T in N.m) and D in mm
- key shear stress tau = F / (b * L_key); crushing stress on the hub side sigma = F / ((h - t1) * L_key)
- shaft torsion stress tau_shaft = 16 * T / (pi * D^3), with T in N.mm

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- D outside the table (10 mm or less, more than 58 mm): refuse.
- L_key > L_hub: refuse (the key must not stick out of the hub).
- tau > tau_allow or sigma > sigma_allow: refuse, and say which stress and by how much.
- The key sits with t1 of its height in the shaft's slot and the rest in the hub's slot; it straddles the joint between
  shaft and hub, which is how it carries the torque.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Four components, one body each: Bearing block (grounded), Shaft, Key, Hub. The shaft is along z, its axis at the
  origin, from z = 0 to z = L_shaft. The bearing block is square round the shaft, max(40, D + 20) mm a side, from z = 0 to
  20 mm, with a bore of D + 0.2. The hub's top face is 5 mm below the shaft's end; the key is centred on the hub's length.
- The shaft's slot: b wide, t1 deep, L_key + 1 long, on the +y side, centred on the hub. The hub's slot: b wide,
  reaching t2 above the shaft's surface, through the hub's whole length.
- Create every component where it belongs; never move one afterwards. The API works in cm: convert every mm input.
- Joints, As-built only: the Shaft turns on the Bearing block about z; the Key and the Hub are rigid to the Shaft
  (the key locks the hub to the shaft, so they turn together).
- The companion's check, projects/keyed_shaft/checks/keyed_shaft_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(D, L_shaft, L_hub, t_wall, L_key, T,
  tau_allow, sigma_allow), the dialog's input ids the same; the joint 'Shaft turns'; the components 'Bearing block',
  'Hub', 'Key'; refusals that say 'longer than the hub', 'outside the DIN 6885 table', 'crushing stress 222.2 MPa'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives b, h, t1, t2, the hub's outside diameter, the three stresses and a version tag.
- With the defaults: key 6 x 6, t1 3.5, t2 2.8, hub outside diameter 36 mm; F = 3000 N; tau = 27.78 MPa,
  sigma = 66.67 MPa, tau_shaft = 19.10 MPa.
- With D = 30: key 8 x 7, t1 4.0, t2 3.3, hub outside diameter 46 mm, nothing else edited.
- No two parts overlap, at rest and with the shaft's joint at 90 degrees.
- Shaft joint at 90 degrees: shaft, key and hub all turn 90 degrees about z; the bearing block does not move.
- Refused: L_key = 30 (longer than the hub); D = 70 (outside the table); T = 100 N.m (sigma = 222.2 MPa > 100).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
