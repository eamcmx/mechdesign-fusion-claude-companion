Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the palletizer's compliance unit: a vertical float between the
tool changer's ISO face and the vacuum or bag gripper. The gripper hangs on a carriage that slides up on two rails
when its load touches the stack, held down onto stops by springs, and a sensor sees it rise. The gripper's air crosses
the float through a tube that slides in a sealed receiver.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the gripper (bag, vacuum or clamp), bag
- the stroke (mm), 30
- the springs' preload (N), 853.4

DERIVED (from projects/palletizer/lib/compliance_unit.py, never typed):
- LAY: the layout below; GUIDE: HIWIN HGR25 rails and HGH25CA blocks (C0 36 490 N, 84 long, 40 high, 48 wide)
- AIR: the passages, galleries, tube and receiver; ISO_B and PIN_B: the ISO pattern in the body's angles
- the springs chosen to Shigley's chapter 10 (music wire 4 x 40, 100 installed), the ring factor with each gripper

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse the clamp gripper (the tool changer's locking ring, projects/palletizer/lib/tool_changer.py, would open in
  the worst stop with the unit in the stack), a stroke over the rails' 30 mm, and a preload under what keeps the
  carriage down in the stops that throw the load up.
- The stroke and the preload asked for are checked and printed only: the unit is always built with the layout's
  30 mm stroke and the design's four springs of 213.3 N each.
- The body is turned 90 deg to the changer: the ISO pattern's +X, with its pin and the changer's docking frame, lies
  along the body's +y. In the body's angles the six M10 are at 0, 60, ... 300 deg on the 160 circle, the pin at 90,
  and the tool plate's four passages at 30, 150, 210 and 330.
- The housing machined from one block of 2024-T3: a top plate 274 x 200 x 20 with the spigot 100 g6, six M10 x 25
  from below and the 10 pin, and two cheeks 20 thick at x = +-112 to +-132, 120 wide, down to the rails' foot.
- An HGR25 rail on each cheek's inner face, 218 long under the top plate, on four M6 x 20 in counterbores; two
  HGH25CA blocks on each rail, their centres 100 apart, the upper one 2 mm under the wings' tops.
- The carriage: a plate 176 x 200 x 32, 2 mm clear of the rails' running faces, whose underside repeats the tool
  plate's (the 100 H7 recess 10 deep, six M10 tapped 15 deep, the 10 pin hole), and two wings 15 thick at x = +-57
  to +-72 carrying the blocks; the wings' tops the stroke under the top plate.
- The stops: two lugs on each wing, 25 wide (y = +-30 to +-55), 15 thick, from the wing's face out to x = +-100, their
  tops the wings' tops; under each a stop block on the cheek's inner face from x = +-76 to the cheek, 35 tall, held by
  two M8 x 30 from the cheek's outside; the carriage rests by its lugs on the blocks. Nothing below the gripper's face.
- Four springs at x = +-25, y = +-60 between round seats, 46 across, screwed into the top plate by an M10 stud and
  reaching down to -138, and the carriage; a spigot 35 across and 5 long on each seat and on the carriage centres
  each spring's ends.
- The air: four passages 10 across from the housing's face, galleries 8 across along x at y = +-40 and along y at
  x = 0 (drilled from the -y edge), plugged at the edges, 10 under the face; a steel tube 20 x 16 pressed into a boss
  32 across and 20 deep under the top plate, its foot 15 mm into a receiver 36 across and 50 tall on the carriage,
  sealed by an O-ring in a groove 7 mm under the receiver's top; the carriage's galleries the same, 21 over its face.
- An M12 proximity sensor 40 long looking along -y at the +x wing's side, 2 mm off it, its face's lower edge 3 mm
  over the wing's top; held by a bracket under the top plate.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), parametric; millimetres in the world, z = 0 the unit's top face
  on the tool plate, z up.
- Top-level groups: Body (grounded) and Carriage. One function, set_state(G, lift=0), G the groups by name
  (_last['groups']), raises the carriage by its transform. No joints, as-built or otherwise.
- The API works in cm and radians. A spring is swept once along a helix and copied as a temporary body.
- The companion's check, projects/palletizer/checks/compliance_unit_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(gripper, stroke, F_pre), the dialog's
  input ids the same; at module level ISO, L, Z_CAR, parts_list(), set_state(); _last holding 'coil', 'groups';
  refusals that say 'ring would open', 'rails give', 'preload'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the gripper, the unit's height and stroke, the guides' static factor, the springs, the ring factor,
  the parts list and a version tag.
- At rest: no interference; the carriage's lugs on the four stop blocks (gap under 0.001 mm); nothing below the
  gripper's face.
- The springs 100 mm long installed and 44 mm across.
- The sensor 3.606 mm from the carriage at rest and 2.000 mm once it has risen 3 mm.
- Raised the whole stroke: no interference but the springs; the wings on the top plate; the blocks still on the
  rails; the tube's foot still in the receiver, below its O-ring, and clear of its floor.
- The ISO pattern measured on the housing's face and on the carriage's, and the passages of the two lined up.
- The body and the carriage weighed and written.
- Refused: the clamp gripper; a stroke of 40 mm; a preload of 500 N.
