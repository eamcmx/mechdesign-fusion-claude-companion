Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the palletizer's drives: the base with an SKF
slewing ring RKS.23 0841 on its flange; the turret on the ring's inner ring, turned by a Nabtesco RV-500N on the axis
through a disc coupling; the shoulder's two cheeks with J2's RV-500N and motor on one side and, on the other, the
crank with its counterweight turned by J3's RV-500N on an outboard bracket; the root of the lower arm, a fork, with
the gas balancer's two cylinders in the fork's gap. The arms themselves are
projects/palletizer/PalletizerRobot/PalletizerRobot.py's.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the counterweight added to the crank (kg), 100
- its centre's distance behind the shoulder (mm), 600

DERIVED (from projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_drives.py and projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_design.py, never typed):
- the shoulder A at (300, 1000) mm; the ring's plane at 520 mm
- GAS: the balancer's pin a = 350 mm below the shoulder, the arm's lug b = 550 mm up the arm, the cylinders' bore
  63 mm; the pins' distance L = sqrt(a^2 + b^2 + 2 a b cos q2)
- CW_BLOCK: the counterweight a steel block 460 x 460 mm, as thick as its mass needs, on the crank's outer face

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a counterweight over 200 kg (J1's reducer then falls short of its rated life,
  projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_drives.py's rv_life()) or one whose block, with the crank turned down to q3 = 60
  deg, would come within 20 mm of the floor.
- The ring, its sizes typed here from SKF's table (pp. 66-67): the outer ring on the base's flange, bored 842.5 mm,
  its top 46 mm above the ring's plane; the inner ring under the turret, 839.5 mm outside and 734 mm at its lip,
  its top at 56 mm; one row of 20 mm balls on dm = 841 mm, their centres 28 mm above the ring's plane, between
  them in grooves cut in both rings 0.4 mm clear of the balls.
- The turret's plate 60 mm thick on the inner ring, with a pocket 30 mm deep, 200 mm along X and 260 mm across,
  round the balancer's pin P; the pin on three lugs 60 mm along X standing on the pocket's floor, at y = -125 to
  -100, -15 to 15 and 100 to 125 mm.
- The reducers drawn 325 mm across (the product page) and 170 mm long (assumed), typed here.
- J1's reducer under the turret's middle: its case on a plate inside the base's column, its output through a disc
  coupling to the turret's plate; its motor under it. The reducer carries no load but the torque.
- The crank and its block outboard of the J3 cheek and of the base's flange: the crank's plane at y = 640 mm.
- The balancer's two cylinders side by side at y = +60 and -60 mm, between the fork's plates; each from a pin on the
  turret a below the shoulder to a cross pin in the fork b up the arm; the barrel turns about the turret's pin, the
  rod with its piston slides in it.
- Nothing touches anything else at rest, with J1 turned through the cycle's 30 deg either way, with J2 anywhere in
  its range (the balancer's parts set to each pose) or with J3 anywhere in its range.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType); millimetres in the world, J1's axis along Z through the
  origin, the arm in the XZ plane facing +X, J2 and J3 about the line through A along Y.
- Subassemblies: Base (grounded), Turret (on J1), Lower arm and Crank (each on its own joint in the turret), and one
  occurrence each for the balancer's barrels and rods, set by the script to each pose.
- As-built revolute joints: J1 about Z, J2 and J3 about the line through A along Y.
- A part inside a subassembly is moved by setting transform2 on its proxy from the root
  (occurrence.createForAssemblyContext(the parent's occurrence)), in world coordinates: set_balancer() moves the
  barrels and rods so, the turret's own turn included. Fusion refuses transform2 on the occurrence itself.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- The companion's check, projects/palletizer/checks/palletizer_drives_check.py, drives the script as
  CHECK_INTERFACE.md says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(cw, r_cw), the dialog's
  input ids the same; at module level AX, AZ, A_MM, BARREL, B_MM, ROD_LEN, parts_list(), set_balancer(); refusals that
  say 'over', 'floor'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the ring, the three reducers with their lives against what each needs, the balancer's forces and
  pressures, the counterweight, the parts list and a version tag. Defaults: RKS.23 0841; J1 12 505 h of 11 667,
  J2 120 984 h of 10 667, J3 13 074 h of 10 667; the pair 54.0 to 69.5 kN, 8.66 to 11.14 MPa; 100 kg at 600 mm.
- 48 components in 40 kinds, by component name and count: Balancer barrel 2, Balancer cross pin Q 1, Balancer lug 3,
  Balancer pin P 1, Balancer rod 2, Base flange 1, Cheek J2 side 1, Cheek J3 side 1, Column 1, Counterweight block 1,
  Coupling disc 2, Coupling spacer 1, Crank shaft 1, Crank web 1, Floor plate 1, Fork plate 2, Gas bottle 2, Hub boss
  J2 side 1, Hub boss J3 side 1, J1 reducer plate 1, J1 stop 1, J1 stop dog 1, J2 support bearing 1, J3 bracket 1, J3
  bracket post 2, J3 support bearing 1, Lower arm root box 1, RV-500N case J1 1, RV-500N case J2 1, RV-500N case J3 1,
  RV-500N output J1 1, RV-500N output J2 1, RV-500N output J3 1, Servo motor J1 1, Servo motor J2 1, Servo motor J3 1,
  Slewing ring balls 1, Slewing ring inner ring 1, Slewing ring outer ring 1, Turret plate 1.
- No interference at rest, at J1 -30 and +30 deg, at J2 -42, 0, 13, 26, 60 and 85 deg, at J3 -75, -51, 0, 30 and
  60 deg.
- At each J2 pose the pins' distance in the model equals sqrt(a^2 + b^2 + 2 a b cos q2) to 0.01 mm, and each piston
  stays inside its barrel's travel.
- Refused: a 300 kg counterweight; a counterweight 1100 mm behind the shoulder.
