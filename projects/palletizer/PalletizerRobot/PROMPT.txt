Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the whole palletizer:
projects/palletizer/PalletizerDrives/PalletizerDrives.py's base, slewing ring, turret, drives, balancer and crank,
with J1 now on a Nabtesco RV-700N; and the links: the lower arm's box up to the elbow, the upper arm with its yoke,
its front fork and its rear lever on a torque tube, the parallel rod, the levelling loop's two rods and the elbow's
triangle, and the wrist housing with J4's RV-80N, its motor and the tool flange. Every pin turns on its bearing.

INPUTS (a command dialog; the pose, by default the cycle's low point):
- J1, q1 (deg), 0
- J2, q2 (deg), 25.988
- J3, q3 (deg), -51.036

DERIVED (from projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_design.py, projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_drives.py and projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_links.py, never typed):
- the layout: the shoulder A at (300, 1000) mm, the lower arm 1300, the upper arm 1400, the crank and the rear lever
  450, the levelling offsets (-390, 150) and (0, 250), J4's axis 250 ahead of the wrist pin, the flange 380 below it;
  the working range
- SECTIONS: the lower arm a box 220 x 300 x 12 mm, the upper arm 200 x 260 x 10, the torque tube 300 x 12, the
  parallel rod a flat bar 40 x 70, the levelling rods tubes 60 x 5
- Y: each part's layer across the arm's plane; STANDOFFS: where the counterweight's standoffs stand on the crank
- the bearings' sizes from SKF's product data; the balancer (GAS) and the block's thickness as
  projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_drives.py gives them

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a pose outside the working range: the joints' limits, and psi = 90 - q2 - q3 between 25 and 140 deg.
- Build each group at q = 0 and place every group by one function, set_pose(G, q1, q2, q3), G the dict of top-level
  groups by name that build() keeps in _last['groups']: the lower arm turned by q2 about the shoulder; the crank and
  the upper arm turned by q3 about the shoulder and the elbow; the parallel rod turned by q2 about its pin C; the
  levelling rods turned by q2 about E and by q3 about G; the triangle and the wrist moved with the elbow and the wrist
  pin; the balancer's barrels and rods as PalletizerDrives.py draws them; then all turned by q1. Fusion's joints do
  not close a parallelogram's loop; its geometry does.
- The layers across the arm's plane (y, mm): the crank's web 610 to 670 and the parallel rod 675 to 745 on its
  outer face; the upper arm's rear lever 610 to 670 on a torque tube round the elbow's axis from 200; the
  counterweight block 750 to 810 on four standoffs where the rod never swings, each standing on the web (whose box
  reaches 150 either side of the crank's line); the levelling rods -310 to -250; the
  triangle and the wrist's plate up to H -245 to -215.
- The parallel rod 40 mm deep in the arm's plane: at full reach it passes 190.2 mm from J3's axis, beside the
  reducer's case of 162.5 mm.
- The bearings: SKF 32213 pairs in the upper arm's cheeks at y = -180 and +180 (the elbow) and at -150 and +150 (the
  wrist); 22213 E in the parallel rod's eyes; 22207 E in the levelling rods' eyes and under the triangle; 61856 MA for
  the lower arm on the J3 side; 61836 for the crank in the J3 cheek.
- The wrist's plate up to H leaves the housing 230 mm ahead of the wrist pin, outside the circle the front fork's
  cheeks sweep round the pin.
- Nothing touches anything else at the cycle's low and high points, with J1 at -30 deg, at the working range's six
  corners and at full reach.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType) in mm; J1's axis along Z through the origin, the arm in
  the XZ plane facing +X, J2 and J3 about A's line along Y.
- No joints, as-built or otherwise; set_pose() closes the loops.
- Top-level groups: Base (grounded), Turret, Lower arm, Crank, Upper arm, Parallel rod, Levelling rod 1 and 2,
  Triangle, Wrist, and one each for the balancer's barrels and rods; each group's parts grounded inside it.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- The companion's check, projects/palletizer/checks/palletizer_robot_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(q1, q2, q3), the dialog's input ids the
  same; at module level AX, AZ, HOME, Y, parts_list(), set_pose(); _last holding 'groups'; refusals that say
  'outside'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the pose, the reducers, the sections, the bearings, the parts list and a version tag.
- At every pose the check visits, each pin's two or three parts meet on one axis to 0.001 mm, and the flange's face is
  where the kinematics of projects/palletizer/lib/palletizer_design.py put it to 0.001 mm.
- No interference at those poses; the parallel rod at least 5 mm from J3's reducer at every one of them.
- Refused: q2 = 85 and q3 = 60 deg (psi -55).
- Each moving group weighed, its mass, centre and inertia about its centre, for the cycle worked again.
