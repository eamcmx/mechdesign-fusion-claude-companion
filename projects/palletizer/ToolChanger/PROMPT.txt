Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the palletizer's mechanical interface of Chapter 44: the robot's
plate under J4's RV-80N to ISO 9409-1-160-6-M10, and on it a tool changer: a master with its cover held by six M10
cap screws, a double-acting piston whose rod passes a gland down to a cam, six segments the cam drives out into a
ring of the tool plate, and the tool plate with the same ISO pattern underneath for the gripper. The script shows the
changer locked or free.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the cam's locking angle (deg), 5
- the supply's least pressure (bar), 5.5
- the state, locked or free, locked

DERIVED (from lib/tool_changer.py, never typed):
- ISO9409: Table 1 of ISO 9409-1 for the pitch circle 160: d2 200 h8, d3 100 H7, six M10 at 30 + 60 k deg, the pin
  hole d5 10 H7 on +Xm, t1 8 and t2 10
- CHANGER: the piston 100 mm, the cam's 5 deg locking zone and 30 deg lead, the ring's 15 deg face, six segments 36 mm
  wide and 16 mm high travelling 3 mm, the coupling face's ring R 90 to 100
- GEOM: every radius and height of the lock, the gland, the cover, the piston's place and its 20 mm stroke, the two
  air ports

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a locking angle that friction as low as 0.10 does not hold (5.71 deg or more): the lock must not let go when
  the air fails.
- Refuse a pressure whose clamp, F_c = F_p / (tan(alpha + phi) tan(beta + phi)) with mu 0.15, falls under 1.5 times
  the clamp that keeps the coupling face closed over the standard cycle.
- The cover from 0 to -7 mm with the spigot into the robot's recess; the master's body from -7 to -70 with the lock's
  spigot (R 65) down to -95; the piston's bore (R 50) from -51 up to the cover over a gland from -55 to -51 that the
  rod (R 20) passes; the cam's bore under the gland.
- The piston's face at -47 when locked, 20 mm higher when free; its rod's end, with the cam on it, at -76 locked
  and -56 free, so in both states the rod spans the gland (-55 to -51) and the gland always seals on it.
- Four 10 mm service passages at 60, 120, 240 and 300 deg on the 160 mm circle, through the cover, the master and
  the tool plate; two 12 mm pins at 0 and 180 deg, standing 12 mm out of the master's face into the tool plate.
- The release port (3 mm, at 15 deg, z -49) opens between the gland and the piston, and the lock port (195 deg,
  z -10) over the piston, locked and free.
- The cam is a separate part on the rod's end, so the piston goes in from above and the cam from below.
- Segments with parallel sides in straight windows, so they slide in and out without jamming.
- The tool plate's ring face only touches the master on R 90 to 100.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), parametric; millimetres in the world, the interface's axis along Z with the robot's face at z = 0, +Xm
  along X.
- Top-level groups: Robot plate (grounded), Master (grounded, with its body, cover, screws and pins), Piston (the
  piston with its rod, and the cam), Segment 1 to 6, Tool plate. One function, set_state(locked, tool_down), places
  the moving groups by their transforms; tool_down lowers the tool plate to test the lock. No joints, as-built or
  otherwise: the lock's states are poses, not motions.
- The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the ISO designation, the screws, the piston, the angles, the clamp against the need, the parts
  list and a version tag.
- No interference locked and free.
- Locked, each segment's 15 deg face touches the ring's (least distance under 0.001 mm), and the tool plate lowered
  0.5 mm runs into all six segments.
- Free, the segments clear the tool plate by more than 0.2 mm, and the tool plate comes off through 45 mm with
  nothing in the way.
- The rod spans the gland and the two ports open on their sides of the piston, locked and free, measured.
- The robot plate's six M10 holes and the pin hole measured on the 160 circle at the ISO angles, and the recess on
  the axis.
- Each group weighed.
- Refused: a 6 deg locking angle; 1.5 bar.
