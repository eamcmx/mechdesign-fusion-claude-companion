Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the palletizer's vacuum gripper: the adapter to ISO
9409-1-160-6-M10 of the parallel-jaw gripper (projects/palletizer/ClampGripper/ClampGripper.py), two aluminium beams and a plate under them, and over each of the six cartons of a
layer a zone of six bellows suction cups on spring level compensators, with a manifold and two compact ejectors. The
script also builds the layer of cartons it holds.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the cartons under the gripper (count), 6
- a carton's mass (kg), 6.5
- the vacuum the system holds, one of 0.6 bar or more, 0.6

DERIVED (from projects/palletizer/lib/vacuum_gripper.py, never typed):
- CUP: Schmalz SPB1 80 ED-65 (166 N at -0.6 bar, 59 N lateral, 98.358 cm3, 45.9 mm high, lip 82.7, 88 pressed)
- EJECTOR: Schmalz SCPSi 2-14 (82 l/min of air, 0.65 s per litre to 600 mbar), lying 93 x 99 x 18 on its manifold
- LAY: the layout below; SPRING: music wire 1.5 x 20, 10 N hanging, 25 N at the end of a 25 mm stroke
- holding(): the cups' use, S m (a_v/(n F_s) + a_h/(n F_l)), with the stops' accelerations for the payload

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse more than six cartons, a carton the cups do not hold (use over 1 at S = 2.0 through the cycle and the
  worst brake stop for its payload), and a vacuum under 0.6 bar.
- The beams 1240 x 60 x 100, walls 5, at y = +-70, from z -16 to -116; the plate 1240 x 840 x 10 under them; the
  adapter's four M8 from inside the beams, reached through holes in the beams' and the plate's bottom.
- The plate held to each beam's bottom wall by eight M6 x 14 cap screws on the beam's middle line at x = +-140,
  +-280, +-420 and +-560, put in from inside the beam through holes in its top wall and tapped 9 mm into the plate.
- The zones' centres at x = -400, 0, +400 and y = -200, +200; each zone's six cups at x +-130 and 0, y +-70 from its
  centre; each cup's sleeve 30 x 16.2 x 50 on the plate, its plunger 16 x 2 from the collar at z -60 to its foot at
  -204, the collar 24 resting on the sleeve, the seat 24 x 4 at -180, the spring between the plate's underside and
  the seat, 50 mm installed.
- Each zone's manifold 150 x 60 x 40 between the zone's two rows of cups, its two ejectors on it, clear of the
  sleeves.
- The cups' lips at z -249.9, on the cartons' tops; every zone free to rise its 25 mm stroke with nothing in the way
  but its springs.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), parametric; millimetres in the world, z = 0 the gripper's
  mounting face, z up; x along the pallet's 1200 mm.
- Top-level groups: Adapter and Frame (grounded), Zone 1 to 6 (each zone's plungers and cups), Pack. One function,
  set_state(lift), raises the zones (and the pack) by their transforms. No joints, as-built or otherwise.
- The API works in cm and radians. A spring is swept once along a helix and copied as a temporary body.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the cartons, the cups' use in the worst stop, the parts list and a version tag.
- On the layer: no interference, and all 36 lips on their cartons (gap under 0.001 mm).
- Every zone pushed up 25 mm with its cartons: no interference but the springs, the lips still on.
- The springs 50 mm long and 21.5 mm across.
- The plate's 16 M6 with their heads on the beams' bottom walls, 9 mm into the plate.
- The adapter's ISO pattern measured.
- Each group weighed, its centre and its inertia about the gripper's axis, written for the envelope.
- Refused: 7 cartons; one carton of 7.5 kg; 0.5 bar.
