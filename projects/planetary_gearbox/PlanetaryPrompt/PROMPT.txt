Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a parametric planetary gearbox, assembled so that
ASSEMBLE > Drive Joints on the sun turns the planets round the ring and turns the carrier.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- module m (mm), 1.5
- sun teeth Zs, 16
- planet teeth Zp, 20
- number of planets N, 3
- pressure angle α (deg), 20
- face width b (mm), 12
- backlash on the pitch circle (mm), 0.3
- planet pin diameter (mm), 6
- sun shaft diameter, input (mm), 8
- carrier shaft diameter, output (mm), 12
- carrier plate thickness (mm), 6
- ring wall outside the tooth spaces (mm), 6

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- ring teeth Zr = Zs + 2·Zp
- centre distance a = m·(Zs + Zp)/2; planet i (i = 0 .. N−1) sits at angle θ_i = 2π·i/N, at (a·cos θ_i, a·sin θ_i)
- pitch radius r = m·z/2, base radius r·cos α; outside gears: tip r + m, root r − 1.25·m
- planet phase ψ_i = [θ_i·(Zs + Zp) + π·(Zp − 1)]/Zp (mod 2π/Zp): planet i's tooth 0 points along ψ_i
- ring phase: the ring's tooth SPACES are centred at k·2π/Zr + φ_r with φ_r = 0 when Zp is odd, π/Zr when Zp is even
- ratios: ring held 1 + Zr/Zs; carrier held −Zr/Zs; sun held 1 + Zs/Zr

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- (Zs + Zr)/N must be a whole number, or the planets cannot be spaced evenly: refuse with a message.
- Neighbouring planets must not touch: 2·a·sin(π/N) > m·(Zp + 2), or refuse with a message.
- True involute flanks from the base circle. Where the base circle is above the root circle, continue the flank
  with a radial line down to the root circle.
- Each outside gear: a root disc (the planets' with their bores, the sun's solid, its shaft joined to it), ONE tooth
  joined to it, then a circular pattern of that tooth z times. The sun's tooth 0 points along +x; planet i's tooth 0 along ψ_i.
- The ring: an annulus from radius Rr − m (the tips of its inner teeth) to Rr + 1.25·m + wall (Rr its pitch
  radius); cut ONE tooth space and circular-pattern the cut Zr times. The tooth space has the outline of an
  outside tooth of a Zr-tooth gear with tip radius Rr + 1.25·m, root radius Rr − m and thickness π·m/2 + backlash/2
  on the pitch circle; space 0 centred at φ_r.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- One component per part, one body each: Ring, Sun, Planet 1 .. Planet N, Carrier, Sun bearing.
- Create each component WHERE IT BELONGS (the translation in the matrix of addNewComponent) and never move it
  afterwards: in a parametric design a later feature undoes the move.
- Gears from z = 0 to z = b. Planet bores: pin diameter + 0.2 mm.
- Sun shaft (input), joined to the sun: from z = −20 mm (it leaves through the BOTTOM) up into the carrier's bore,
  ending 0.5 mm short of the bore's end (z = b + 1 + half the plate thickness − 0.5).
- Carrier (output): a disc of radius a + pin diameter, from z = b + 1 to z = b + 1 + plate thickness; a pin down from
  it into each planet's bore, to z = 0; a blind bore from its bottom face, half the plate thick, of diameter
  sun shaft + 4.4 mm; the output shaft up from its top face, 20 mm long (it leaves through the TOP).
- Sun bearing: a sleeve in the carrier's bore round the sun shaft, inner diameter sun shaft + 0.2 mm, outer
  diameter sun shaft + 4 mm, as deep as the bore. A shaft that passes a turning frame needs a bore and a bearing.
- Each tooth outline is ONE closed polyline of sketch lines that share their SketchPoint objects (no splines).
- The API works in cm and radians: convert every mm and degree input.
- Joints: AsBuiltJoints only (Joints.add would move the parts). The ring is grounded. The sun turns on the ring
  about z; the carrier turns on the ring about z; each planet turns on the CARRIER about its own centre; the sun
  bearing is rigid to the carrier.
- Fusion has no tooth contact: the meshes are Motion Links. Sun to carrier: (Zs + Zr)·360 deg of the sun for
  Zs·360 deg of the carrier, same direction. Carrier to each planet: Zp·360 deg of the carrier for Zr·360 deg of
  the planet, reversed (the planet turns backwards relative to the carrier).
- The companion's check, projects/planetary_gearbox/checks/planetary_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(m, zs, zp, n, alpha, b, backlash, d_pin,
  d_in, d_out, ct, wall), the dialog's input ids the same.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message at the end gives Zr, the three ratios and a version tag.
- With the defaults: driving the sun one full turn turns the carrier 80° the same way, and each planet −224°
  relative to the carrier.
- No two parts overlap at rest, nor at any sun angle in steps of 45° through one turn (Interference).
- Zs = 24, Zp = 30, N = 4 builds; Zs = 20, Zp = 25, N = 4 is refused, because 90/4 is not whole.

A WORKED EXAMPLE of the polyline I mean (it works in my Fusion; P3 = adsk.core.Point3D.create, points in cm):

def _polyline(sk, pts):
    lines = sk.sketchCurves.sketchLines
    first = lines.addByTwoPoints(P3(pts[0][0], pts[0][1], 0), P3(pts[1][0], pts[1][1], 0))
    prev = first.endSketchPoint
    for q in pts[2:]:
        prev = lines.addByTwoPoints(prev, P3(q[0], q[1], 0)).endSketchPoint
    lines.addByTwoPoints(prev, first.startSketchPoint)
