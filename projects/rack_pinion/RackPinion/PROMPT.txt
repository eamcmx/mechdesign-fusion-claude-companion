Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a rack and pinion on a base plate: a spur pinion turning on a fixed
shaft and a straight rack sliding under it, assembled so that Drive Joints on the pinion moves the rack.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- module m (mm), 2
- pinion teeth z, 20
- rack teeth N_r (count), 16
- pressure angle alpha (deg), 20
- face width b (mm), 10

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- pinion: pitch radius r = m z / 2, base radius r cos(alpha), tip r + m, root r - 1.25 m
- rack: pitch p = pi m; teeth with straight flanks at alpha to the vertical, addendum m above the pitch line,
  dedendum 1.25 m below it, thickness on the pitch line p / 2 - 0.1 mm (backlash 0.2 mm); a body 8 mm deep below
  the roots
- rack travel per pinion turn = pi m z; per degree r * pi / 180
- contact ratio of a rack and pinion: (sqrt(r_a^2 - r_b^2) - r sin(alpha) + m / sin(alpha)) / (pi m cos(alpha))
- travel limits of the rack: plus and minus (N_r / 2 - 2) p, so that the pinion always meshes with whole teeth

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- z at least 18 (fewer undercut at 20 degrees), or refuse.
- N_r at least 8, or refuse: the rack must be long enough to travel.
- A gap of the rack is centred under the pinion's tooth 0 at x = 0, which points straight down at the rack.
- The rack's pitch line is tangent to the pinion's pitch circle.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created where they belong: Base plate (grounded), top face at z = 0; Shaft at (0, r), 8
  mm, with a collar under the pinion; Pinion on it from z = 1 to 1 + b, centre (0, r); Rack from z = 1 to 1 + b, its
  pitch line on y = 0, teeth pointing up (+y), tooth k (from 0) centred at x = (k + 1/2) p - floor(N_r / 2) p, so
  that a gap sits at x = 0 (an odd N_r puts one tooth more on the +x side). The rack's guide is not drawn.
- The pinion tooth outline is one closed polyline of sketch lines that share their SketchPoints (no splines); the
  rack's whole outline, teeth and body, is one closed polyline of sketch lines sharing their points.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: rigid between the plate and the shaft; revolute about z between the pinion and the shaft; a slider
  along x between the rack and the plate, limited to the travel (switch each limit on before setting its value: a
  value set while its limit is off is rounded to 0.001 mm). A Motion Link: 360 deg of the pinion for pi m z of the
  rack; the pinion turning anticlockwise (seen from above) moves the rack towards +x.
- The companion's check, projects/rack_pinion/checks/rack_pinion_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(m, z, n_r, alpha, b), the dialog's input
  ids the same; the joints 'Pinion turns', 'Rack slides'; the components 'Pinion', 'Rack'; at module level
  rack_pinion(); refusals that say 'undercut', 'cannot travel'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives r, the travel per turn, the contact ratio, the travel limits and a version tag.
- Defaults: r = 20 mm, travel per turn 125.664 mm, contact ratio 1.769, limits plus and minus 37.699 mm.
- The pinion driven 90 deg moves the rack +31.416 mm, by the joint and by the body.
- No interference at rest; no tooth collision while the pinion turns through one tooth pitch (18 deg).
- Refused: z = 12; N_r = 6.

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
