Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the rear axle of projects/rear_axle/RearAxle/RearAxle.py in
detail: its tapered bearings' shoulders and spacer brought inside SKF's abutment dimensions, the cups' bores closed to
the cups, the half shafts' splines to ISO 14, the case bearings' adjusters threaded, and every made part's material
and every fitting face's class and roughness as design_office/lib/materials_fits.py decides them.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the adjusters' thread pitch (mm), 2

DERIVED (from projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_detail.py, projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.py and design_office/MaterialsFits/MaterialsFits.py, never typed):
- SKF_ABUT, ABUTMENTS, abutments(): SKF's abutment dimensions, each shoulder's diameter in the concept
  (projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.py) and the detail's
- carrier_layout(), spacer(): the pinion's bearings along its axis, the spacer from cone face to cone face
- load_classes(): each tapered bearing's load at cruise and at its peaks against C, and SKF's class for its seat
- splines(), SHAFT, PINION: ISO 14's 8 x 36 x 40 x 7 for the half shafts, the pinion's 8 x 32 x 38 x 6
- adjuster(), ADJ: the thread M120 x 2, its minor and pitch diameters, the stresses on it
- MaterialsFits.apply(): the materials and the fits on the open design

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build with RearAxle.py's own build() and apply MaterialsFits.py's apply(); copy neither, nor
  projects/rear_axle/RearAxleCarrier/RearAxleCarrier.py or projects/rear_axle/AxleHousing/AxleHousing.py. Change the
  parts by replacing, before the build, the functions that draw them, each replacement recognising only the calls it
  means and passing every other through: the cylinder for the cups' bores and the nose's middle bore; the tube for the
  spacer, the companion flange's hub and the adjusters; the splines' constants and their engagement's defaults.
  Restore every replaced function and constant after the build. The case's shoulders are not a call to replace: their
  cylinder lies inside the case's sphere, so a smaller one changes nothing.
- The cups' bores 100, 90 and 120, the cups' own diameters; the nose's middle bore 88 from cup A to its middle and 80
  from there to cup B; the spacer from cone A's front face to cone B's, 67.8 long; the companion flange's hub 50 across
  for 3 mm at cone B; the case's shoulders 75 across; the adjusters 106 across for 6 mm at the cups, 72 beyond.
- The half shafts' and the side gears' splines 8 x 36 x 40 x 7.
- After the build, cut a ring 2 mm deep from 75 to 82 across at each end of the differential case, where the cones
  sit, so that each cone sits on 75; split the carrier's and the caps' bores where the adjusters begin; and give by
  hand: M120 x 2 6g on
  the adjusters and 6H in the bores beyond the cups; ISO 14's sliding fit on the half shafts and the side gears (shaft
  f7 a11 d10, hub H7 H10 H9), its fixed fit on the pinion (h7 a11 h10) in the companion flange (H7 H10 H9).
- Refuse a pitch at which one thread would not carry the case bearing's axial load at the peak with a factor of 2 in
  shear on the adjusters' 1045.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- RearAxle's own build() makes the new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType) and its six subassemblies, with
  its As-built joints and motion links; nothing moved; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.
- The companion's check, projects/rear_axle/checks/axle_detail_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md says
  (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(pitch), the dialog's input ids the same; at
  module level DIA; _last holding 'changed', 'mf', 'new'; refusals that say 'falls short'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the abutments' diameters, the spacer, the cups' bores, the splines and their torsion factor, the
  thread, the faces given, and a version tag.
- The cups' bores 100, 90 and 120 in the casting and the caps, none 0.1 over; the census's six cup seats touching, H7.
- The nose's middle bore 88 and 80, none 93; the spacer touching both cones, 67.8 long.
- The flange's hub 50 at cone B, the case's shoulders 75, the adjusters 106 at the cups.
- The half shafts' and the side gears' splines 36 across their minor diameter.
- The threads and the splines' classes as above; every other decided face what the rules decide.
- No interference at rest, driving straight, nor in a turn.
- Refused: a pitch of 1 mm, at which one thread carries the case bearing's load at 174.0 MPa in shear.
