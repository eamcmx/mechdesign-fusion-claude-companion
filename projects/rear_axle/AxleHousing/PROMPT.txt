Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the housing of a light truck's live rear axle and its two
semi-floating wheel ends, as real parts: a banjo housing that holds the carrier of
projects/rear_axle/RearAxleCarrier/RearAxleCarrier.py and carries the truck's rear from its spring seats to its wheel
bearings, and in each tube's end a half shaft on a deep-groove bearing, the shaft's flange carrying the wheel's studs;
the half shafts turning in their bearings.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the half shaft's body d_body (mm), 48
- the tube's outside diameter D (mm), 80
- the tube's wall t (mm), 8
- the wheel bearing's bore (mm), 65

DERIVED (computed from the inputs by projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.py, never typed; Budynas and Nisbett 2015):
- the six load cases at one wheel (projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.py): the moment about the bearing b V -/+ r S
  with b = 65 mm and r = 365 mm, b X; the shaft on two supports 615 mm apart; the bearing's load
- the shaft, 4140 Q&T 425 C (Table A-21): yield in the hardest turn with the design torque; DE-Goodman fatigue under
  the static wheel load and the running torque (Eqs. 6-8, 6-19, 6-20, Table 7-1); torsion at the splines' minor
  diameter
- the wheel bearing from Table 11-2: life (C10 / F)^3 against 127.32 million turns; Eq. 11-12 with Table 11-1 in a turn
- the tube, 1035 HR (Table A-20): the pothole's bearing load on the 140 mm from the bearing to the spring seat

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a shaft that keeps under 1.5 against yield in the hardest turn or in fatigue, a bearing that does not last
  127.32 million turns, a tube under 1.5 in the pothole, saying which.
- The axle along Y, forward +X, up +Z, the axle's middle at the origin.
- The housing: a drum round the axle whose outside is 168.07 mm from the axis (196.93 mm of ground clearance with the
  365 mm static radius, at least the 195 of Change 1); a front neck whose flange takes the carrier's plate at x = 165,
  both cut flat 166 mm below the axle, with eight studs and nuts M12 on 370 mm
  (projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.joint_bolts); a rear neck past the drum's back with a cover, ten M8 screws,
  filler and drain plugs; a breather; tubes to each wheel; at each tube's end the bearing's housing with the seal's
  bore and the bearing's shoulder, and the brake's flange; a spring seat on each tube 575 mm from the middle.
- Each wheel end: a half shaft with eight straight-sided splines 35 x 40 at its inner end, held by the side gear at
  100 mm from the middle, a body of d_body, the bearing's seat, a shoulder of d_S against which the bearing is
  pressed, and a flange of 190 mm with six M14 studs on 139.7 mm, the flange part of the shaft; a retaining ring
  pressed on inboard of the bearing, the seal running on it. Nothing inboard of the shoulder wider than the
  bearing's bore. The bearing's outer ring, a retainer plate and an oil seal 74 x 100 x 10 stay with the housing.
- Nothing touches anything else, at rest or turning.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Three subassemblies: Housing (grounded), Wheel end L, Wheel end R;
  each part a component, grounded to its parent, one body each; deep-groove bearings drawn by
  common/lib/mdfc_parts.py's bearing_bodies() (inner ring, outer ring, balls).
- Each wheel end turns on an As-built revolute joint about Y on the housing.
- The API works in cm and radians. Materials: Steel; the breather Brass; the seals Rubber, Silicone.
- The companion's check, projects/rear_axle/checks/axle_housing_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md says
  (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(d_body, D, t, d_seat), the dialog's input ids
  the same; the joint 'Half shaft <side> turns'; at module level design(), parts_list(); refusals that say 'against
  yield', 'million turns', 'in the pothole'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the shaft's three factors, the bearing's life, the tube's stress and factor, the parts list and a
  version tag. Defaults: 2.49, 3.17, 1.47; 172.9 million turns; 142.0 MPa, 1.90.
- 57 components in 16 kinds, by component name and count: Banjo housing 1, Bearing 6213 balls 2, Bearing 6213 inner
  ring 2, Bearing 6213 outer ring 2, Breather 1, Carrier nut M12 8, Carrier stud M12 8, Cover screw M8x20 10, Drain
  plug M22 1, Filler plug M22 1, Half shaft 2, Oil seal 74x100x10 2, Rear cover 1, Retainer plate 2, Retaining ring 2,
  Wheel stud M14 12.
- No interference at rest or with each half shaft turned 30 and 75 deg; each bearing's
  inner ring on the 65 mm seat and its outer ring in the 120 mm bore; each wheel end able to go together, the shaft
  inboard of the bearing no wider than its bore, the retaining ring inboard of it, a shoulder of d_S at its outboard
  face; the housing no lower than 168.07 mm below the axle.
- Refused: a body of 38 mm, a bearing of 60 mm bore, a tube 70 x 5.
