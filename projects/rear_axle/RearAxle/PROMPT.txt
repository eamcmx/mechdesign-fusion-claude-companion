Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the rear axle of a light truck from its two subsystems: the
carrier of projects/rear_axle/RearAxleCarrier/RearAxleCarrier.py bolted into the housing of
projects/rear_axle/AxleHousing/AxleHousing.py, each half shaft splined into its side gear, so that the axle turns as
one machine: the pinion drives the differential's case through the spiral bevel pair, the case drives both wheels, and
in a turn the side gears let the outer wheel run faster than the inner one.

INPUTS (no dialog: each subsystem keeps its own defaults and its own refusals; the one value read here, which the
check changes to test the refusal, with its default):
- where each half shaft's splines start from the axle's middle, Y_TIP of AxleHousing.py (mm), 35

DERIVED (from the two scripts and projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.py, never typed):
- the carrier: RearAxleCarrier.design() and layout(); the housing and wheel ends: AxleHousing.design()
- the splines in the side gears (projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.spline_engagement): engaged from the shaft's tip
  to the end of the side gear's hub (74 + 32.75 mm); the flanks carrying one half shaft's torque, 3777.4 N m (half
  the design torque: duty(brief())['T_shaft'], passed in N mm), at their mean radius on their height with half the
  teeth taken to carry it; at least 2 against the shaft's yield (1140 MPa)

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Use the two subsystems' own builders, each of which builds into the component it is given: AxleHousing.py's
  housing() and wheel_end_asm(), RearAxleCarrier.py's carrier(), pinion_assembly() and differential_assembly(), and
  RearAxleCarrier.py's revolute_about() for the joints. Their build() functions open documents of their own and are
  not called; the final drive's Motion Link is made here. Draw nothing twice. Refuse what either refuses, and refuse
  splines that reach less than the length needed into the side gears, saying how far they reach.
- The axle along Y, forward +X, up +Z, Y to the left (ISO 8855), the axle's middle at the origin: L at +Y, R at -Y in
  both subsystems.
- The carrier's plate on the housing's neck flange at x = 165, on the eight studs.
- Each half shaft's splines from 35 mm, deep in its side gear, to 115 mm.
- The drum's cavity clear of the necks' walls: the carrier's caps and the crown wheel turn inside it.
- Nothing touches anything else, at rest, driving straight or in a turn.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Six subassemblies: Housing (grounded), Carrier, Pinion
  assembly, Differential assembly, Wheel end L, Wheel end R.
- AsBuiltJoints: the carrier rigid on the housing; the pinion revolute about X and the case about Y on the carrier,
  with the Motion Link of the final drive (47 turns of the pinion to 12 of the case, the case turning about +Y when the
  pinion turns about +X); the spiders and side gears revolute in the case, made in the root between proxies; each half
  shaft rigid in its side gear. Motion Links for the differential: each spider turns 22 for 15 of a side gear, the
  other way as seen along their own axes; side gear L to every spider, spider 1 to side gear R.
- The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the final drive, the differential, the splines' engagement with their pressure and factor, the
  parts list and a version tag. Defaults: 71.75 mm engaged, 35.3 mm needed, 280.8 MPa, factor 4.06.
- 106 components in 43 kinds, by component name and count: Adjuster 2, Banjo housing 1, Bearing 6213 balls 2, Bearing
  6213 inner ring 2, Bearing 6213 outer ring 2, Bearing A 32309 cone 1, Bearing A 32309 cup 1, Bearing B 32308 cone 1,
  Bearing B 32308 cup 1, Bearing C1 32213 cone 1, Bearing C1 32213 cup 1, Bearing C2 32213 cone 1, Bearing C2 32213
  cup 1, Bearing cap 2, Breather 1, Cap bolt M12x90 4, Carrier casting 1, Carrier nut M12 8, Carrier stud M12 8,
  Companion flange 1, Cover screw M8x20 10, Cross 1, Crown wheel bolt M12x35 10, Crown wheel z47 1, Differential case
  1, Drain plug M22 1, Filler plug M22 1, Half shaft 2, Oil seal 74x100x10 2, Pinion depth shim 1, Pinion nut M30 1,
  Pinion seal 60x85x10 1, Pinion shaft z12 1, Rear cover 1, Retainer plate 2, Retaining ring 2, Side gear 2, Side gear
  washer 2, Spacer 1, Spider 4, Spider washer 4, Washer 1, Wheel stud M14 12.
- No interference at rest; each half shaft's splines 71.75 mm into its side gear.
- Driving straight, the pinion turned 30 deg: the case and both half shafts 7.6596 deg about +Y, no interference.
- In a turn, the pinion 47 deg and spider 1 2.5 deg: the case 12 deg, the half shafts 12 -/+ 1.7045 deg, no
  interference.
- Refused: splines from 100 mm.
