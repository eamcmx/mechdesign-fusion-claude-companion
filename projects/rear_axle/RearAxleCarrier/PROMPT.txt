Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the carrier of a light truck's rear axle as a subsystem of real
parts: a spiral bevel pinion on two tapered roller bearings in the carrier's nose, a crown wheel bolted to the
differential's case, the case on two tapered roller bearings in the carrier's saddles under bearing caps, and an open
differential of four spiders on a cross and two side gears; the parts turning as they do in the axle.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- final-drive module at the heel m (mm), 6.5
- final-drive face width F (mm), 52
- differential module m_d (mm), 5.5
- spiders N (a count), 4

DERIVED (computed from the inputs by projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.py, never typed; Childs 2014 and Budynas and Nisbett 2015):
- the duty: 2009.25 N m on the pinion for 9528 turns (5000 hard starts), 283.49 N m at 4800 rpm for 498.69 million
- the spiral bevel pair 12:47, 20 deg normal, 35 deg spiral: cones, A_e = m/2 sqrt(12^2 + 47^2), mean point at
  A_e - F/2; rated by ANSI/AGMA 2003-B97 in SI (Childs Eqs. 10.9, 10.12, 10.16-10.23; Z_I 0.1275, Y_J 0.23;
  carburized grade 2, 1550 and 240 MPa; Z_NT, Y_NT for the turns)
- the forces of the mesh (Childs Eqs. 10.6, 10.7, the upper signs driving forward), the pinion's bearings SKF 32309
  and 32308 back to back, the case's SKF 32213 face to face (Shigley Eqs. 11-18 to 11-20)
- the differential: side gears 22, spiders 15, straight bevel, rated in bending at the design torque
- the layout: the pinion's bearings' pressure points from SKF's a; the case's bearings at y = -74 and +74: the case
  ends at shoulders there, and each 32213 cone sits on a seat running outwards from its shoulder, its large (back)
  face against the shoulder, so that the pressure points lie T - a further out

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a module whose pinion keeps a bending factor under 1 at the design torque, a crown wheel that leaves less
  than 195 mm of ground clearance (365 mm less half its outside diameter, 8 mm of wall and 6 mm of gap), a face
  over a third of the cone distance, and spiders for which 2 z_s / N is not a whole number, saying which.
- The pair's apex at the origin, the pinion along +X (forward), the axle along Y, the crown wheel on the -Y side.
- Spiral teeth lofted through seven transverse sections from the toe to the heel, each Tredgold's virtual tooth with
  the transverse module and pressure angle, drawn in its gear's own frame (the apex at the origin, the gear's axis
  +z, the heel at the outer cone distance) and turned about that +z by phi(R) = -/+ tan(35 deg) / sin(delta)
  ln(R/A_m): minus for the pinion, which is then left-handed, plus for the crown wheel, right-handed, so the crown
  wheel's teeth of the opposite hand to the pinion's (projects/rear_axle/lib/spiral_bevel.py's member(), section()
  and to_world(), loaded, not copied, draw them so), its tooth 0 half a pitch round so that a gap meets the
  pinion's tooth.
- Tapered roller bearings drawn from their catalogue sizes: the cone with its rollers as one body, the cup apart.
- The pinion's shaft: a collar, a depth shim, bearing A's seat (45), a spacer, bearing B's seat (40), splines
  8 x 32 x 38 for the companion flange, an M30 thread, washer and nut.
- The case: one casting, a spherical cavity of radius 76 mm, two windows, a flange for ten M12 bolts on 186 mm, a
  spigot; the crown wheel's bore 170.4 mm passes over the case. The gears' backs spherical, of radius 74 mm, on
  spherical washers; the side gears' hubs splined 8 x 35 x 40 for the half shafts.
- The carrier: a mounting plate 400 mm across, cut flat 166 mm below the axle so that it stays above the housing's
  bowl, with eight holes on 370 mm over its upper three quarters (projects/rear_axle/lib/axle_design.joint_bolts); the
  nose with the cups' bores and the seal's, two legs with the saddles, bearing caps with two M12 bolts each, threaded
  adjusters behind the case's cups. Every cup's bore in the casting, in the nose and in the saddles, is 0.1 mm over
  the cup's outside diameter (0.05 mm a side); every cone sits on a seat of its own bore.
- Each bearing cap 75 mm deep below the legs' face at x = 0, the width of its leg, held by two M12 x 90 at z = -68
  and +68 mm (clear of the cups), each through a 13.2 mm hole in the cap into a hole tapped 16 mm into the leg.
- Nothing touches anything else, at rest or turning.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Three subassemblies, Carrier (grounded), Pinion assembly,
  Differential assembly; each part a component, one body each, grounded to its parent except the spiders and the
  side gears, which turn on their own joints.
- AsBuiltJoints in the root: the pinion assembly revolute about X, the differential revolute about Y; a Motion Link of
  47 turns of the pinion to 12 of the case, the case turning about +Y when the pinion turns about +X. The spiders and
  the side gears revolute on the case, each joint made in the root between the parts' proxies.
- The API works in cm and radians. Materials: Steel; Iron, Cast for the casting, the caps and the case.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the ratio, the module, the crown wheel's size and the clearance, the pinion's factors at both duty
  points, the bearings and their spacing, the differential's factors, the parts list and a version tag.
- Defaults: bending factor 1.078 and contact 1.718 at the design torque; 1.861 and 1.809 at full power; crown wheel
  308.1 mm, clearance 196.9 mm; the differential's side gear 1.114 and spider 1.377.
- 49 components in 27 kinds, by component name and count: Adjuster 2, Bearing A 32309 cone 1, Bearing A 32309 cup 1,
  Bearing B 32308 cone 1, Bearing B 32308 cup 1, Bearing C1 32213 cone 1, Bearing C1 32213 cup 1, Bearing C2 32213
  cone 1, Bearing C2 32213 cup 1, Bearing cap 2, Cap bolt M12x90 4, Carrier casting 1, Companion flange 1, Cross 1,
  Crown wheel bolt M12x35 10, Crown wheel z47 1, Differential case 1, Pinion depth shim 1, Pinion nut M30 1, Pinion
  seal 60x85x10 1, Pinion shaft z12 1, Side gear 2, Side gear washer 2, Spacer 1, Spider 4, Spider washer 4, Washer 1.
- No interference at rest; every bearing's cone on its seat and its cup in its bore; the
  plate no lower than 166 mm below the axle.
- The pinion turned 30 deg about +X: the case 7.6596 deg about +Y. No tooth collision while the pinion turns one
  pitch, nor with the side gears turned 4, 8.18 and 12 deg in the case.
- Refused: module 5.9 with a face of 47 mm (bending), module 6.75 with a face of 54 mm (clearance), a face of 56 mm,
  three spiders.
