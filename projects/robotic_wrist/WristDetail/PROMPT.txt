Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the robotic wrist of
projects/robotic_wrist/WristIntegration/WristIntegration.py in detail: its bought products drawn to their makers'
figures, its bearings' abutments inside SKF's bands, the stop disc's key the length that is made, and every made
part's material and every fitting face's class and roughness as design_office/lib/materials_fits.py decides them.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the hard stop's contact angle (deg), 128

DERIVED (from projects/robotic_wrist/lib/wrist_detail.py, projects/robotic_wrist/lib/wrist_design.py and design_office/MaterialsFits/MaterialsFits.py, never typed):
- products(): the motor's, the gear unit's and the slip ring's figures in place of the assumptions of
  projects/robotic_wrist/lib/wrist_design.py, for the build and for the numbers
- numbers(): the motors' duty, the stop, the play at the tool, the wrist's mass, each motor's end
- abutments(): the shoulders against SKF's da and Da; DETAIL_ABUT, the diameter the detail gives those outside
- load_classes(): each bearing position's load against SKF's C and its class
- housing_classes(): SKF's table 4 on those classes, the front side gears' housings and the floating roll bearing's
  bore
- detail_numbers(): the detail's new names and their part numbers
- MaterialsFits.apply(): the materials and the fits on the open design

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build with WristIntegration.py's own build() and apply MaterialsFits.py's apply(); copy neither. Change the wrist's
  parts by replacing, before the build, the functions that draw them, each replacement recognising only the calls it
  means and passing every other through: the gear motor, the motor's output shaft and pulley, the motor bracket, the
  slip ring's parts, the ring that draws spacers S4 and S5, the cylinder that draws the roll shaft's collar, the
  frame and the screw that place the slip ring bracket's screw. Restore every replaced function after the build.
- The gear motor: the PLE040's square 40 flange 5 thick at the gearhead's face with its 26 h7 spigot, its round body
  40 across to the adapter, the adapter square 40 and 28.5 long; the AM8111 square 40 and 129 long with its brake.
- The output: the pulley and two M4 set screws on the unit's smooth 12 mm shaft, 26 long.
- The motor bracket's middle slot 27 mm across, so that the 26 mm spigot passes it over the slot's travel.
- The slip ring: its stator 22 across and 19.5 long, its 44.5 mm flange at the top against the bracket's tab; the
  bracket's bar and the stator's pin gone; the bracket's screw M3 x 10 through the flange and the tab.
- The roll shaft's collar and spacers S4 and S5 18 mm across; the stop disc 71 to 82 mm, its key 5 x 5 x 10 from 72
  to 82 and renamed so, the trunnion's end at 82.
- The front side-gear bearings' housings M7, their load being light; the carrier's bore for the roll shaft's lower,
  floating 6002 J7, so that its outer ring can slide; every other class as design_office/lib/materials_fits.py decides
  it.
- Refuse a contact angle the AM8111's brakes (8 ms delay) would not stop the carrier short of, or one leaving the
  block under 10 deg a side: WristIntegration.py's build() refuses, run with the products' figures.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- WristIntegration's build() makes the design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), its groups and
  projects/robotic_wrist/WristDifferential/WristDifferential.py's As-built joints and motion links; nothing moved;
  lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.
- The companion's check, projects/robotic_wrist/checks/wrist_detail_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(contact), the dialog's input ids the same;
  at module level pose; refusals that say 'falls short'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives each motor's end, the slip ring, the abutments, the key, the motors' duty, the stop, the play, the
  wrist's mass, the front housings' class, the materials and faces given, and a version tag.
- Each gear motor 209.5 mm from the gearhead's face to the motor's end, its spigot 2 mm beyond, 40 mm square; the
  output shaft 12 across.
- The slip ring's stator 22 across, 19.5 long, its flange 44.5 across; no pin; the bracket's screw M3 x 10.
- The collar and S4 and S5 18 across, each 6002's shoulders inside 17 to 18.5.
- The key 10 long, 1 mm inside the stop disc at its inner face.
- The front side gears' two bores carry M7, the floating roll bearing's bore J7 and the fixed one's K7, every other
  decided face what the rules decide.
- No interference at rest, nor at pitch -120, 0 and +120 deg with the roll turned 0, 90 and 180 deg.
- Refused: a contact angle of 123 deg, which the brakes with the AM8111's brake do not stop short of.
