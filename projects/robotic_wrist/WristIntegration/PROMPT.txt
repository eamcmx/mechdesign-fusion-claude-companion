Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the pitch-roll wrist of
projects/robotic_wrist/WristDifferential/WristDifferential.py, loaded and not copied, and makes it ready for its arm:
the tool flange drawn to ISO 9409-1-31.5-4-M5, the tool's wires carried across the roll by a slip ring, and hard stops
for the pitch, so that the wrist can be posed over its whole range and checked for interference, and its stops shown
to engage.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the hard stop's contact angle (deg), 128

DERIVED (from projects/robotic_wrist/lib/wrist_design.py, never typed):
- stops40(): the brakes' stopping angle from the switch at 121 deg, the brakes' delay and torque; the block's half
  angle from the contact angle, the lug's width and the pads' thickness
- FLANGE40, BORE40, SLIPRING, STOP40, KEY40 and LAY40: the flange's interface, the bore, the slip ring's outline,
  the stop's radii and every new axial position

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a contact angle the brakes would not stop the carrier short of, or one that leaves the block less than
  10 deg either side.
- The tool flange: four M5 on 31.5, a 5 H7 pin hole on +X between two of them, the 20 H7 centring recess 3 deep
  carried down to a DIN 471 ring on the roll shaft, which holds the flange; no screw in the shaft's end.
- The roll shaft bored 5 mm through; the slip ring's rotor on its lower end, its stator under the carrier, held
  from turning by a pin in a bracket screwed to the carrier.
- On the left (+y) side only: the trunnion longer, a spacer, a stop disc keyed to it with a lug on +z, an end
  washer and screw, the trunnion's end 0.5 mm inside the disc's face so that the washer clamps the stack; the fixed
  cap replaced by a stop cap with four countersunk screws, a block on -z and two buffer pads.
- Nothing touches anything else at rest, at the eight poses of pitch and roll (30, 0), (0, 45), (-40, 70), (75, -123),
  (-110, 200), (25, 30), (-60, 150) and (120, -90) deg, and over +-127 deg of pitch with the roll turned.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), as WristDifferential.py makes it; the API works in cm and
  radians.
- WristDifferential.py's parts that change are deleted from their groups and the new ones added to the groups they
  move with, with the joints at rest; the As-built joints and motion links are WristDifferential.py's; the pitch's
  limits opened to +-140 deg so that the check can reach the stops.
- Every body keeps the material it had before the deletions.
- The companion's check, projects/robotic_wrist/checks/wrist_integration_check.py, drives the script as
  CHECK_INTERFACE.md says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(contact), the dialog's
  input ids the same; at module level design(), parts_list(), pose; refusals that say 'brakes', 'too short'.

- A message gives the stop's numbers, the bored shaft's frequency, the play, and the parts list with a version tag.
- 97 components in 43 kinds, by component name and count: Base plate 1, Bearing 6002-2RSH 4, Bearing 61902-2RS1 4,
  Bearing cap floating 1, Belt HTD 350-5M-15 2, Buffer pad 2, Cap screw ISO 4762 M3x8 1, Cap screw ISO 4762 M4x10 8,
  Cap screw ISO 4762 M4x12 4, Cap screw ISO 4762 M5x12 6, Cap screw ISO 4762 M5x16 6, Cap screw ISO 4762 M8x20 4,
  Carrier block 1, Countersunk screw ISO 10642 M3x8 8, Countersunk screw ISO 10642 M4x12 4, Dowel ISO 8734 5x16 4, End
  washer 18x5.5x2.5 2, Key DIN 6885 A 5x5x18 1, Key DIN 6885 A 5x5x8 1, Motor bracket 2, Motor output 2, Output bevel
  gear 1, Retaining ring DIN 471-15 1, Retaining ring DIN 471-15, flange 1, Retaining ring DIN 472-32 1, Roll shaft,
  bored 1, Servo motor with brake and 15:1 gearhead 2, Side bevel gear with 36-groove pulley 2, Side cheek 2, Slip

  1, Stop cap 1, Stop disc 1, Tie bar 2, Tool flange ISO 9409-1-31.5-4-M5 1, Trunnion 1, Trunnion with stop end 1.
