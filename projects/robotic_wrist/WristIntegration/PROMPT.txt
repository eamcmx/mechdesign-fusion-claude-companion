Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds Chapter 39's pitch-roll wrist with WristDifferential.py, loaded
and not copied, and makes it ready for its arm: the tool flange drawn to ISO 9409-1-31.5-4-M5, the tool's wires
carried across the roll by a slip ring, and hard stops for the pitch, so that the wrist can be posed over its whole
range and checked for interference, and its stops shown to engage.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the hard stop's contact angle (deg), 128

DERIVED (from lib/wrist_design.py, never typed):
- stops40(): the brakes' stopping angle from the switch at 121 deg, the brakes' delay and torque; the block's half
  angle from the contact angle, the lug's width and the pads' thickness
- FLANGE40, BORE40, SLIPRING, STOP40, KEY40 and LAY40: the flange's interface, the bore, the slip ring's outline,
  the stop's radii and every new axial position

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a contact angle the brakes would not stop the carrier short of, or one that leaves the block less than
  10 deg either side.
- The tool flange: four M5 on 31.5, a 5 H7 pin hole on +X between two of them, the 20 H7 centring recess 3 deep
  carried down to a DIN 471 ring on the roll shaft, which holds the flange; no screw in the shaft's end.
- The roll shaft bored 5 mm through; the slip ring's rotor on its lower end, its stator under the carrier, held
  from turning by a pin in a bracket screwed to the carrier.
- On the left (+y) side only: the trunnion longer, a spacer, a stop disc keyed to it with a lug on +z, an end
  washer and screw; the fixed cap replaced by a stop cap with four countersunk screws, a block on -z and two buffer
  pads.
- Nothing touches anything else at rest, at Chapter 39's poses and over +-127 deg of pitch with the roll turned.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), as WristDifferential.py makes it; the API works in cm and
  radians.
- Chapter 39's parts that change are deleted from their groups and the new ones added to the groups they move with,
  with the joints at rest; the As-built joints and motion links are Chapter 39's; the pitch's limits opened to
  +-140 deg so that the check can reach the stops.
- Every body keeps the material it had before the deletions.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the stop's numbers, the bored shaft's frequency, the play, and the parts list with a version tag.
- 97 components in 43 kinds.
- The links as in Chapter 39.
- No interference at rest, at the eleven poses of the check; at +-129 deg the stop disc inside a pad and nothing
  else.
- The flange's faces include its 20 mm recess, its 5 mm pin hole and its 45 mm outside.
- Refused: a contact at 125 deg; a contact at 155 deg.
