Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the SCARA's two arm joints as two modules side by side: each a
servo motor with its holding brake on a module plate, its shaft in the wave generator of a Harmonic Drive CSG-2UH
housed unit, ratio 50, whose output flange carries the next arm. J1 is a CSG-40-50-2UH, J2 a CSG-20-50-2UH. The output
turns one fiftieth of the motor's shaft, the other way.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the J1 unit's size, one of CSG 20, 32 or 40 (the J2 unit's is 20), 40

DERIVED (from projects/scara_robot/lib/scara_design.py, never typed):
- modules(): each joint's load pattern over the standard cycle (J1 the horizontal leg, J2 the radial move), the
  catalogue's average torque, average and largest input speeds and life (pp. 14-15); the motors' peak and RMS
  torques through the ratio, the efficiency and the inertia; the output bearings' static safety and life
  (pp. 34-35); the case bolts' friction torque; the joints' natural frequencies from K1
- CSG: the catalogue's ratings (Table 3) and outlines (Tables 28 to 30); MOTOR: the motors' specification

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a J1 unit whose repeated peak torque, average torque or largest input speed is over its catalogue limit,
  or whose life is under the brief's 20 000 h, saying which and by how much.
- Draw each unit from its catalogue outline: the housing A across and E high from the input face, the output flange
  O across reaching B, its bore S; the case bolts on the circle b, as many and as large as Table 29 gives, class
  12.9, through the housing into the module plate.
- Inside each unit, at concept level: the housing with the circular spline; one row of cross rollers, their axes
  alternately at +45 and -45 deg; the output flange's hub inside the rollers; the flexspline's cup under the hub;
  the elliptical wave generator on the motor's shaft inside the cup.
- The motor's flange square under the module plate on four bolts; the motor and its brake below; the motor's shaft
  up through the plate into the wave generator, and no further.
- Nothing touches anything else at rest or with an output turned.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Two modules, J1 at the origin and J2 400 mm along X, each along
  +Z from its housing's input face. In each, three subassemblies: stator (the housing, the rollers, the plate, the motor and the bolts),
  output (the flange and the flexspline) and input (the wave generator and the motor's shaft).
- As-built revolute joints about each module's axis for its output and its input; a Motion Link of 50 turns in to
  one out, the output turning the other way (the circular spline is fixed).
- The temporary-body manager makes no ellipse: sketch the wave generator and extrude it in its own component.
- The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives each joint's unit, average torque against its limit, life and largest input speed, the J1 motor's
  peak and RMS torques, the parts list and a version tag. Defaults: J1 CSG-40, 150.8 N m (limit 255), 23 984 h,
  3426 rev/min, motor 9.56 N m peak and 6.04 RMS; J2 CSG-20, 26.4 N m (limit 44), 15 121 h on radial moves.
- 42 components in 16 kinds, by component name and count: CSG-20-50-2UH housing 1, CSG-40-50-2UH housing 1, Case bolt
  M5 8, Case bolt M8 10, Cross rollers 2, Flexspline 2, Module plate J1 1, Module plate J2 1, Motor bolt M5 4, Motor
  bolt M8 4, Motor shaft J1 1, Motor shaft J2 1, Output flange 2, Servo motor J1 1, Servo motor J2 1, Wave generator
  2.
- Each output turned 7.2 and 30 deg: its input turned 50 times as far, the other way; no interference.
- Each housing A across and each output flange's face B from the input face, as the catalogue gives them.
- Refused: a CSG-32 at J1.
