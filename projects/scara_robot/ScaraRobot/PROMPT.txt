Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the SCARA as one robot: a base column with
projects/scara_robot/ScaraJointModules/ScaraJointModules.py's J1 module on its top, a first arm on J1's output flange
carrying its J2 module at its end, and a second arm on J2's output flange whose end is
projects/scara_robot/ScaraToolJoints/ScaraToolJoints.py's ball screw spline with its belts and motors, so that the
robot can be posed anywhere in its workspace and its mass properties measured.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the J1 unit's size, CSG 40 or 45 (the 40 only for comparison), 45

DERIVED (from projects/scara_robot/lib/scara_design.py, never typed):
- LAY38 and heights(): the column's top at 200 mm, the first arm's box section, every height from the units' B
- the J1 and J2 modules from ScaraJointModules.py's module(), the arm's end from ScaraToolJoints.py's design() and
  build_parts(), loaded, not copied

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a J1 unit other than a CSG-40 or a CSG-45.
- The J1 module from ScaraJointModules.py's module() at the CSG-45: its case bolts, twelve M8 in 9 mm holes on a
  164 mm circle (Table 29), added to that script's bolt table, which knows only the sizes 20, 32 and 40.
- The base: a steel foot 300 mm square with four anchor holes, an aluminium tube 220 mm across with 10 mm walls, a
  top flange clear of the J1 motor's flange; the J1 module plate on the flange.
- The first arm: an aluminium box 110 wide, 70 high, 4 mm walls, from 75 mm behind J1 to 50 mm beyond J2, on J1's
  output flange; a window through it at J2 for the J2 motor, which hangs from the J2 module's plate on the arm's top.
- The second arm: in place of ScaraToolJoints.py's plates and walls, 4 mm skins over the whole arm from 45 mm behind
  J2, the full 12 and 10 mm only in bosses round the nuts, the motors' mounts and J2's output flange; 3 mm walls and a
  back wall; the arm's end's other aluminium parts kept aluminium.
- Nothing touches anything else where the links meet, at rest, at both ends of the standard cycle's horizontal leg,
  with J2 at +150 and -150 deg, and at J1's range ends.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Three groups: the base (grounded), the first arm and the
  second arm, the base's and the first arm's frames at J1's unit's input face, the second arm's at J2's axis on its base plate's top face.
- J1 and J2: As-built revolute joints about z. J3: the arm's end's carriage slides; J4: the tool's shaft turns on it.
- Each strain wave unit's output held to the next arm by an As-built rigid joint; the units' inputs and the arm's
  end's nuts and motors held still in their groups.
- pose(q1, q2, j3, j4) in degrees and millimetres.
- The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the units and the parts list with a version tag.
- 93 components in 41 kinds with the CSG-45, by component name and count: Ball screw spline shaft SP20 1, Base column
  1, Base foot 1, Base top flange 1, Belt 3MR-474-09 J3 1, Belt 3MR-489-09 J4 1, CSG-20-50-2UH housing 1,
  CSG-45-50-2UH housing 1, Case bolt M5 8, Case bolt M8 12, Cross rollers 2, First arm beam 1, Flange bolt M4 8,
  Flexspline 2, J3 motor bolt M5 4, Module plate J1 1, Module plate J2 1, Motor bolt M4 4, Motor bolt M5 4, Motor bolt
  M8 4, Motor shaft J1 1, Motor shaft J2 1, Motor shaft J3 1, Motor shaft J4 1, Output flange 2, Pulley 18T motor J4
  1, Pulley 28T motor J3 1, Pulley 56T nut J3 1, Pulley 72T nut J4 1, Pulley screw M5 12, Screw nut SP20 flange 1,
  Screw nut SP20 rotor 1, Second arm structure 1, Servo motor J1 1, Servo motor J2 1, Servo motor J3 1, Servo motor J4
  1, Spline nut SP20 flange 1, Spline nut SP20 rotor 1, Tool flange 1, Wave generator 2.
- No interference where the links meet at the seven poses.
- The robot's mass properties measured part by part; on them, with the motors at their specified masses and the
  units at the catalogue's, J1 lasts 20 000 h or more on the CSG-45 and less on the CSG-40; J2's peak and average
  torques within the CSG-20's limits.
- The tool flange's underside 359 mm above the floor at J3 0.
- Refused: a CSG-32.
