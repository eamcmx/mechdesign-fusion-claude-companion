Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the SCARA's third and fourth joints of Chapter 37 at the end of
its second arm: a HIWIN FB ball screw spline, whose one shaft carries a screw and a spline, with the screw nut on the
arm's top plate and the spline nut under its base plate, each turning in its own support bearing; a toothed pulley on
each nut; two Gates GT3 3 mm belts to the J3 and J4 servo motors inside the arm's end; the shaft sliding (J3) and
turning (J4) with a tool flange at its lower end.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the ball screw spline's size, one of SP 16, 20, 25 or 32, 20

DERIVED (from lib/scara_design.py, never typed):
- tool_joints(): the shaft at the full stroke as a cantilever (its swing and its bending against the endurance
  limit), the depth rule, the screw's cube-mean force and life with the short-stroke operating factor, the spline's
  torque, the motors' peak and RMS torques, the belts by Gates' procedure, the brakes
- SP: the catalogue's dimensions and ratings (HIWIN Tables 9.2 and 9.3); nut_layout(): every height; LAY37: the
  layout; MOTOR34: the motors' specification

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a size whose shaft swings under 20 Hz at the full stroke, whose fatigue factor with the tool 100 mm down
  is under 1.5, or whose mass at the tool costs the first joint its life of 20 000 h, saying which and by how much.
- The screw nut's flange on the top plate, its rotor's pilot up, its pulley above it; the spline nut's flange under
  the base plate, its rotor's pilot down, its pulley below it; each pulley located on its nut's pilot and held by
  six M5 screws in the pilot's tapped holes; each flange held by four M4 bolts.
- Pulleys of 28 and 56 teeth for J3, 18 and 72 for J4; 9 mm belts of the stock lengths Gates' procedure gives, the
  motors placed at the centre distances those lengths give, 34 mm either side of the arm's middle line.
- The shaft long enough to stay 5 mm beyond the screw nut's rotor at the full stroke of 200 mm.
- Nothing touches anything else at J3 0, 100 and 200 mm with J4 at 0, 90 and 180 deg.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Seven groups: the arm's end (grounded), the screw side, the
  spline side, each motor's side, a body-less carriage and the tool's shaft.
- The carriage slides along z on an As-built slider joint; the shaft turns on it on an As-built revolute joint, so
  that J3 and J4 are two joints. The spline nut turns with the shaft (a Motion Link, one to one); each motor turns
  with its nut through its belt's ratio, the same way (Motion Links).
- The screw nut's angle depends on both joints (the shaft's turn less the travel over the lead), which a Motion Link
  cannot hold: pose(j3, j4) sets it by the numbers, and the check measures the result.
- The temporary-body manager makes no belt: sketch each belt's band (the external tangents and arcs) and extrude it.
- The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the shaft's swing, the fatigue factors at 100 and 200 mm and the acceleration's scale at the
  bottom, the screw's cube-mean force and life, the motors' torques, the belts and their centre distances, the parts
  list and a version tag. Defaults: 21.1 Hz, 1.60 and 1.01, 0.632; 66.7 N, 910 708 h; J3 motor 0.699 N m peak,
  0.407 RMS, 3092 rev/min; J4 motor 0.342 and 0.209; 3MR-474 and 3MR-489, 173.48 and 175.10 mm.
- 48 components in 24 kinds.
- J4 turned 90 deg alone: both nuts turn 90, the J4 motor 360, the J3 motor 180. J3 down 7 mm alone: the spline nut
  still, the screw nut -126 deg, the J3 motor -252 deg.
- At the full stroke the shaft's top 5 mm above the screw nut's rotor; the belts' centre distances measured on the
  pulleys' faces as Gates' lengths give them.
- Refused: SP16 and SP25.
