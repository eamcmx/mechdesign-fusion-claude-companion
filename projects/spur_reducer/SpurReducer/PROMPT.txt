Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a single-stage spur reducer, sized by subsystems: a split
cast iron housing with bearing caps, seals and fasteners, an input shaft with its pinion, and an output shaft with
its gear; every part a real machine element at concept level, the shafts turning on their bearings.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- power P (kW), 4
- input speed n1 (rpm), 1440
- application factor KA (a count), 1.25
- bearing life L10 (h), 20000
- design factor nd (a count), 2

DERIVED (the sizes chosen here, and what is computed from them and the inputs, never typed; data from Budynas
and Nisbett, 2015):
- gears m = 3 mm, z1 = 19, z2 = 95, face 30 mm, 20 deg: T1 = P / w1, T2 = T1 x 5 x 0.98; Ft = KA 2 T1 / d1,
  Fr = Ft tan 20 deg, W = Ft / cos 20 deg; Kv = (6.1 + V) / 6.1 (Eq. 14-6b); Lewis Kv Ft / (F m Y) with Y of
  Table 14-2; contact Cp sqrt(Kv Ft / (F cos 20 deg) (1/r1 + 1/r2)), Cp = 191 (Eq. 14-14); steel of 300 HB,
  St = 0.533 HB + 88.3 and Sc = 2.22 HB + 200 MPa (Figs. 14-2, 14-5); for the life's cycles N, YN = 1.6831 N^-0.0323
  and ZN = 2.466 N^-0.056 (Figs. 14-14, 14-15); bending factor St YN / stress, contact factor Sc ZN / stress
- shafts of 1045 CD (Sut 630, Sy 530 MPa): bearings 98 and 100 mm apart, W at mid-span; DE-Goodman (Eq. 7-8) with
  ka = 4.51 Sut^-0.265, kb = 1.24 d^-0.107, Se = ka kb 0.5 Sut, keyseats Kt 2.14 and Kts 3.0 (Table 7-1), at the
  input end (16 mm), the gear seat (35 mm) and the output end (25 mm); slopes W L^2 / (16 E I) at the bearings
  and deflection W L^3 / (48 E I) under the gears, the seat's diameter all along, against Table 7-2
- bearings of Table 11-2 chosen by their seats, 6204 and 6206: C needed = R (60 n L10 / 10^6)^(1/3), L10 in hours
- DIN 6885 keys 5x5x28, 10x8x42, 8x7x52 (form A): shear F / (b (L - b)), crushing F / ((h - t1)(L - b)), with
  F = 2 KA T / d; 60 and 100 MPa allowed

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a duty at which a tooth's bending factor is below nd or its contact factor below 1.1, a shaft's factor is
  below nd, a key is overstressed, a bearing's life is short, a slope exceeds 0.001 rad or the gear deflects more
  than Table 7-2 allows, saying which.
- Shafts along y, the split plane z = 0, the pinion at x = 0, the gear at x = 171 mm. Bearing walls from y = 42 to
  60 mm each side; the bearings against the walls' inner faces; caps 8 mm thick with spigots on the outer rings.
- Input shaft: 16 mm end with its key, 20 mm seats, 25 mm shoulders, the pinion cut on the shaft. Output shaft:
  30 mm seats, a 40 mm shoulder, the gear's 50 mm hub on 35 mm between the shoulder and a spacer to the second
  bearing, a 25 mm end with its key. The gear with a web, six holes and a rim.
- Lip seals 20x35x7 and 30x47x7 in the through caps; 4 M6 screws per cap; 8 M10 screws and nuts in the split
  flange, which stops short of the caps; two dowels; drain and filler plugs; feet with four holes.
- Nothing touches anything else at rest, and the teeth do not collide while turning.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Three subassemblies: Housing (grounded), Input shaft assembly, Output shaft assembly; each part a component of
  its subassembly, one body each; the bearings' inner rings with the shafts, outer rings and balls with the housing.
- Inside a subassembly only the first component is grounded to its parent: set isGroundToParent on every part.
- Bodies from TemporaryBRepManager given in world coordinates and moved into the component; involute teeth (each
  outline one closed polyline of sketch lines), extruded along the sketch's normal and patterned about the component's
  own axis.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: each shaft assembly revolute about y on the housing; a Motion Link of 1800 deg to 360 deg,
  the output turning the other way. Materials: Steel for the shafts, gear and small parts; Iron, Cast for the
  housing and the caps.
- The companion's check, projects/spur_reducer/checks/spur_reducer_check.py, drives the script as CHECK_INTERFACE.md
  says (give it to the model for its names, never for a design fact): build(P, n1, KA, life, nd), the dialog's input
  ids the same; the joint 'Input shaft'; at module level parts_list(), reducer_design imported as rd; refusals that
  say 'the in key bears 101.4 MPa', 'bearing 6204 lasts 99941 h', 'the output end has a factor of safety of 2.29'.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the gears' stresses, the shafts' factors, both slopes and the input shaft's deflection under
  the pinion, the bearings' lives, the keys' stresses, the parts list and a version tag.

  1.

  stack (shoulder, gear, spacer, bearing) without play; every cap on its outer ring; the gear's tip radius.
