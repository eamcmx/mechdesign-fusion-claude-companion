Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds Chapter 31's rear axle in detail: its tapered bearings' shoulders
and spacer brought inside SKF's abutment dimensions, the cups' bores closed to the cups, the half shafts' splines to
ISO 14, the case bearings' adjusters threaded, and every made part's material and every fitting face's class and
roughness as Chapter 51 decided them.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the adjusters' thread pitch (mm), 2

DERIVED (from lib/axle_detail.py, lib/axle_design.py and Chapter 51's MaterialsFits.py, never typed):
- SKF_ABUT, ABUTMENTS, abutments(): SKF's abutment dimensions, each shoulder's diameter in Volume 2 and the detail's
- carrier_layout(), spacer(): the pinion's bearings along its axis, the spacer from cone face to cone face
- load_classes(): each tapered bearing's load at cruise and at its peaks against C, and SKF's class for its seat
- splines(), SHAFT, PINION: ISO 14's 8 x 36 x 40 x 7 for the half shafts, the pinion's 8 x 32 x 38 x 6
- adjuster(), ADJ: the thread M120 x 2, its minor and pitch diameters, the stresses on it
- MaterialsFits.apply(): the materials and the fits on the open design

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build with RearAxle.py's own build() and apply MaterialsFits.py's apply(); copy neither, nor RearAxleCarrier.py or
  AxleHousing.py. Change the parts by replacing, before the build, the functions that draw them, each replacement
  recognising only the calls it means and passing every other through: the cylinder for the cups' bores, the nose's
  middle bore and the case's shoulders; the tube for the spacer, the companion flange's hub and the adjusters; the
  splines' constants and their engagement's defaults. Restore every replaced function and constant after the build.
- The cups' bores 100, 90 and 120, the cups' own diameters; the nose's middle bore 88 from cup A to its middle and 80
  from there to cup B; the spacer from cone A's front face to cone B's, 67.8 long; the companion flange's hub 50 across
  for 3 mm at cone B; the case's shoulders 75 across; the adjusters 106 across for 6 mm at the cups, 72 beyond.
- The half shafts' and the side gears' splines 8 x 36 x 40 x 7.
- After the build, split the carrier's and the caps' bores where the adjusters begin, and give by hand: M120 x 2 6g on
  the adjusters and 6H in the bores beyond the cups; ISO 14's sliding fit on the half shafts and the side gears (shaft
  f7 a11 d10, hub H7 H10 H9), its fixed fit on the pinion (h7 a11 h10) in the companion flange (H7 H10 H9).
- Refuse a pitch at which one thread would not carry the case bearing's axial load at the peak with a factor of 2 in
  shear on the adjusters' 1045.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- RearAxle's own new design document and groups; nothing moved; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the abutments' diameters, the spacer, the cups' bores, the splines and their torsion factor, the
  thread, the faces given, and a version tag.
- The cups' bores 100, 90 and 120 in the casting and the caps, none 0.1 over; the census's six cup seats touching, H7.
- The nose's middle bore 88 and 80, none 93; the spacer touching both cones, 67.8 long.
- The flange's hub 50 at cone B, the case's shoulders 75, the adjusters 106 at the cups.
- The half shafts' and the side gears' splines 36 across their minor diameter.
- The threads and the splines' classes as above; every other decided face what the rules decide.
- No interference at rest, driving straight, nor in a turn.
- Refused: a pitch of 1 mm, at which one thread carries the case bearing's load at 174.0 MPa in shear.
