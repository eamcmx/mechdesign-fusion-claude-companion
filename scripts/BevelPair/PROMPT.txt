Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds a pair of straight bevel gears on shafts at right angles,
assembled and ready to turn with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- module m at the heel (mm), 2
- pinion teeth z1, 20
- gear teeth z2, 40
- pressure angle alpha (deg), 20
- face width b (mm), 12
- shaft diameter d (mm), 8

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- pitch cone angles delta1 = atan(z1 / z2), delta2 = 90 deg - delta1
- pitch radii r = m z / 2; cone distance R = r1 / sin(delta1) (the same for both); k = (R - b) / R
- Tredgold's approximation: each tooth is the tooth of a virtual spur gear of pitch radius r_v = R tan(delta) and
  z_v = z / cos(delta) teeth, drawn at the heel on the back cone; tip r_v + m, root r_v - 1.25 m, base
  r_v cos(alpha), thickness pi m / 2 - 0.1 mm (backlash 0.2 mm) on the virtual pitch circle; below the base circle
  the flank is a radial line
- contact ratio of the virtual spur gears (Chapter 11); ratio z2 / z1
- the blank as in the bevel wrist of Topic 5: root cone from toe to heel, back cone, back face 2 mm behind the heel
  root, a hub of radius d / 2 + 6.2 mm, 8 mm long, and a bore of d + 0.4 mm

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- b at most R / 3, or refuse.
- The pinion's virtual teeth z_v1 at least 2 / sin^2(alpha) = 17.1 (fewer undercut), or refuse.
- Both pitch cones have their apex at the origin; the pinion's axis is world +Z, the gear's world +X.
- The pinion's tooth 0 points at the gear (world +X); the gear is turned half a tooth so that a gap meets it.
- The pinion turning anticlockwise about +Z turns the gear the other way about +X, at z1 / z2 of the angle.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each: Shaft 1 and Shaft 2 (both grounded), Pinion, Gear. Each created where it belongs, its
  own z along its axis and its origin at the apex (Matrix3D.setWithCoordinateSystem).
- The blanks from TemporaryBRepManager cones put in through a base feature; each tooth lofted from its heel
  outline to its toe outline (the heel scaled by k towards the apex), joined, then patterned round the axis.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: revolute between each gear and its shaft. Try the joint directions and keep the one that really
  turns the part about its axis, measuring the sign of the turn. A Motion Link between the two revolute joints at
  the ratio of the teeth, its direction set from the measured signs.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the two cone angles, R, z_v1, z_v2, the contact ratio and a version tag.
- Defaults: delta1 = 26.565 deg, delta2 = 63.435 deg, R = 44.721 mm, z_v1 = 22.36, z_v2 = 89.44, contact ratio 1.712.
- The pinion driven 90 deg about +Z turns the gear -45 deg about +X, measured on the gear's body.
- No interference at rest; no tooth collision while the pinion turns through one tooth pitch (18 deg).
- Refused: b = 16 (more than R / 3 = 14.91); z1 = 15 (z_v1 = 16.02).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
