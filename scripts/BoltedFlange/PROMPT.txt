Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds two round flange plates clamped together by hexagon bolts and nuts
on a bolt circle, assembled as one rigid joint, and that reports the preload, the tightening torque and the safety
factors of the joint under an external tensile load.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- bolt size (one of M5, M6, M8, M10, M12), M8
- number of bolts N (count), 4
- bolt circle diameter BCD (mm), 60
- plate thicknesses t1 and t2 (mm), 10 and 10
- external tensile load on the joint F_ext (N), 20000

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- metric coarse thread data, mm: tensile stress area At (M5 14.2, M6 20.1, M8 36.6, M10 58.0, M12 84.3);
  clearance hole d_h, ISO 273 medium (5.5, 6.6, 9, 11, 13.5); hexagon across flats s and head height k, ISO 4017
  (8 and 3.5, 10 and 4, 13 and 5.3, 16 and 6.4, 18 and 7.5); nut height m, ISO 4032 (4.7, 5.2, 6.8, 8.4, 10.8)
- property class 8.8: proof stress Sp = 580 MPa; steel E = 207000 MPa
- grip l = t1 + t2; proof load Fp = At * Sp; preload Fi = 0.75 * Fp; tightening torque T = K * Fi * d with K = 0.2
- bolt stiffness (fully threaded bolt, ISO 4017) kb = At * E / l
- member stiffness, two steel members: km = 0.5774 * pi * E * d / (2 * ln(5 * (0.5774 l + 0.5 d) / (0.5774 l + 2.5 d)))
- joint constant C = kb / (kb + km); load per bolt P = F_ext / N
- bolt load Fb = Fi + C * P; clamping force left Fm = Fi - (1 - C) * P
- yield factor np = Fp / Fb; factor against separation n0 = Fi / (P * (1 - C))
- plate diameter BCD + 4 * d_h; bolt length = l + m + 2 mm

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Room for a socket between neighbouring bolts: BCD * sin(pi / N) >= 2 * s, or refuse.
- The joint must not separate: n0 > 1, or refuse, giving n0.
- The bolts must stay below their proof load: np > 1, or refuse.
- Each bolt goes through a clearance hole in both plates; bolt and hole never touch.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created where they belong: Plate 1 (grounded) from z = -t1 to 0; Plate 2 from z = 0 to
  t2; Bolt 1 .. Bolt N, each a hexagon head on top of plate 2 and a plain shank of diameter d down through both
  plates; Nut 1 .. Nut N, each a hexagon with a hole of diameter d, under plate 1. Bolt i on the bolt circle at angle
  360 * (i - 1) / N degrees. Each hexagon is one closed polyline with shared sketch points.
- The API works in cm: convert every mm input.
- Joints, As-built only, all rigid: Plate 2, each Bolt, and each Nut to Plate 1.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives At, Fp, Fi, T, kb, km, C, P, Fb, Fm, np, n0 and a version tag.
- M8, 4 bolts, 10 + 10 mm, 20 kN: Fp = 21228 N, Fi = 15921 N, T = 25.47 N.m, kb = 378810 N/mm,
  km = 1665398 N/mm, C = 0.18531, P = 5000 N, Fb = 16848 N, Fm = 11848 N, np = 1.260, n0 = 3.908.
- 4 bolts and 4 nuts, each bolt at its angle on the bolt circle; no interference anywhere.
- Refused: 12 bolts (60 * sin(15 deg) = 15.5 mm < 26 mm); F_ext = 80000 N (n0 = 0.977, the joint separates).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
