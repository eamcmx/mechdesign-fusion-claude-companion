Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the bag gripper and puts it, as Parts 9 and 10 left it through Boothroyd and
Dewhurst's test for unnecessary parts, in the model, and then makes the three fixings the test found missing, so that
the designer sees which pieces must be separate, which could go, and the gripper with every part held.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the machine: the only machine the test is answered for is the bag gripper, bag

DERIVED (never typed):
- RangeSweep.py's machine('bag'): the gripper as its detail chapter built it, with its stops
- lib/bom_detail.py: each component's line of the bill (Chapter 64)
- lib/dfma.py: each line's answer to the test (which question keeps how many of its pieces, or none), each piece's
  operation and time (Matuszek et al., 2021, Table 2), the theoretical minimum, the assembly time and the design
  efficiency E = 3 Nmin / t (Range, 2013); the three kinds with no fixing and the fixing each takes; the layout from
  lib/bag_gripper.py

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build the gripper through RangeSweep.py's machine(), loaded, not copied.
- Answer each question for a future redesign, not for the parts as they are (Dewhurst & Meeker, 2014, p. 5).
- Give every piece of the bill its answer as an attribute of its occurrence, the weldment's pieces through its tubes,
  and count the pieces, the theoretical minimum and the questions from the attributes read back, not from the lib.
- Colour the pieces: blue the essential, red the candidates, yellow the kinds with no fixing and the new screws.
- The fixings, each with its holes cut: the eight tines of each jaw joined to its tube; each end block joined to its
  end plate, the eight M8 x 30 between them removed and their holes filled; two ISO 4762 M8 x 20 through the beam's
  side walls into each spigot, at the spigot's mid-depth and the beam's mid-height, clearance holes in the walls,
  tapped holes in the spigot; two ISO 4762 M8 x 30 from inside each end plate into its cylinder bracket's root, 30 apart
  up the arm, clearance holes in the plate, tapped holes in the bracket.
- Check the new screws for interference against every other piece of the gripper (the bag and the station left out).
- Count the bill again after the fixings, write everything to results/dfa.json, leave the design open, save nothing.
- Refuse a machine the test is not answered for.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- a new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), as RangeSweep.py makes it; the machine's own as-built joints
  left as they are; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the pieces, the essential ones by question, the pieces after the fixings, what was joined, removed
  and added, the new screws' interferences, the mass before and after, and a version tag.
- 114 pieces answered, every first-level line of the bill among them; 19 essential read back (base 1, move 15,
  separate 3); E = 3 x 19 / 640.2 s.
- 16 tines and 2 end blocks joined; the eight M8 x 30 removed; four M8 x 20 and four M8 x 30 added.
- The bill after: no tine or end block line, ten M8 x 20, four M8 x 30, 96 pieces.
- No interference of the new screws; the mass changes by less than 0.2 kg.
- Refused: a machine called crane.
