Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the drive of an aircraft's trailing-edge flap: a ball screw
whose nut pushes a link to the flap's horn, swinging the flap down about its hinge, assembled and ready to move
with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- lead L (mm), 5
- screw diameter d0 (mm), 16
- horn radius r, hinge to the link's pin (mm), 100
- horn angle phi0 with the flap up, from straight up towards aft (deg), -45
- nut line zs, above the hinge (mm), 60
- link l (mm), 150
- flap chord c (mm), 400
- flap span s (mm), 1500

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- x aft, z up, the hinge at the origin; with the flap down by d the horn's tip is at r (sin(phi0 + d), cos(phi0 + d));
  the nut's pin on z = zs, forward of the tip by sqrt(l^2 - (zs - tip z)^2)
- the nut's travel from 0 to 40 deg of flap and the screw's turns for it; the smallest transmission angle between
  link and horn; the nut must move aft all the way
- the ball screw: lead angle atan(L / (pi d0)); with the balls' contact angle 45 deg and a rolling friction
  coefficient of 0.005, the efficiency driving (cos a - mu tan l) / (cos a + mu cot l) and back-driving
  (cos a - mu cot l) / (cos a + mu tan l) (Childs, 2014, Eq. 16.33); self-locking above mu = tan l cos a
- the air load at 40 m/s (q = rho V^2 / 2, rho 1.225 kg/m3): at full flap a normal force coefficient of 1.2 on the
  flap's area at 45 % of its chord from the hinge, taken as rising linearly with d; the nut's force M / (dx/dd) by
  virtual work; the screw's torque F L / (2 pi eta) to drive and F L eta' / (2 pi) put back by the load

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- The smallest transmission angle at least 40 deg (Norton, 2008, Sec. 3.3), or refuse giving it.
- The nut moving aft steadily from 0 to 40 deg, or refuse; a link that cannot reach the horn, refuse.
- A ball screw that would lock itself (mu at least tan l cos a), refuse: it is chosen to run free, with a brake.
- A right-hand screw: a turn about +x moves the nut towards -x; the flap goes down as the nut runs aft.
- Each part on its own level along y, joined by pins: the screw and the nut at y = 0, the horn from -10 to 10, the
  link from 17 to 27 mm; the hinge's brackets outboard of the flap. Nothing touches anything else.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created where they belong: Wing (the rear spar, the hinge's brackets and pin;
  grounded), Flap (slab, nose round the hinge, horn and its pin), Screw, Nut (with its pin), Link.
- Bodies from TemporaryBRepManager through base features; joint points from an XZ sketch through
  modelToSketchSpace (Chapter 11).
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints, the direction tried and its sign measured (Chapter 17): revolute about x for the screw, slider along
  x for the nut, revolute about y for the flap on its hinge, the link on the nut's pin and the link on the horn's pin
  (closing the loop). A Motion Link of 360 deg of the screw for L of the nut, its direction set from the signs.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the lead angle, the travel and turns, the transmission angle, both efficiencies, the hinge moment,
  the largest nut force, the screw's torques and a version tag.
- Defaults: lead angle 5.681 deg; 66.939 mm of nut, 13.388 turns; smallest transmission angle 49.09 deg; efficiency
  0.9330 driving, 0.9283 back-driving, self-locking only above 0.0703; q = 980.0 Pa; hinge moment 127.008 N m at 40
  deg; largest nut force 1245.1 N at 40 deg, 1.0620 N m to drive, 0.9197 N m put back.
- 5 components with one body each; 4 revolute and 1 slider As-built Joints; 1 Motion Link.
- No interference at rest.
- The screw turned in 144 deg steps to 40 deg of flap: the nut at the lead per turn and the flap at the angle worked
  out independently for the nut's position, measured on the bodies, at every step; nothing interfering.
- Refused: zs = 100 mm (transmission angle 33.74 deg); mu = 0.08 (self-locking above 0.0703); l = 10 mm (the link
  cannot reach).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
