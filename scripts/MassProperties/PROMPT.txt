Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of Part 9's nine detailed assemblies, the bag gripper with
Chapter 56's stops, and weighs it: every group's mass, centre and moments of inertia about its centre, and for the
robot each of its eleven moving bodies in the form Chapter 43's cycle reads, so that the cycle can be run again on the
detailed robot.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the machine: robot, wrist, flap, axle, bag, clamp, vacuum, changer or unit; robot

DERIVED (never typed):
- RangeSweep.py's machine(): the build, through each detail script, the bag gripper with its stops
- the parts' materials: Chapter 51's library materials, as the detail scripts give them

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build each machine through RangeSweep.py's machine(), loaded, not copied; copy none of the detail scripts.
- Weigh with Fusion's own physical properties of each part that has bodies: the mass, the centre of mass, and the
  moments of inertia about the world's axes moved to the centre by the parallel-axis rule.
- A group is a top-level occurrence and all the parts under it; a group's name that comes twice is numbered, so that
  no group is lost from the sum.
- For the robot at q = 0: the turret, the lower arm, the crank and counterweight, the parallel rod, levelling rod 1,
  the triangle, the upper arm, levelling rod 2, the wrist housing, the tool flange and the balancer, each with its mass,
  its layer y, its centre against its own pivot and the moments of inertia its motion needs, in
  lib/palletizer_weighed.json's form; the base apart.
- Write each machine's result to results/mass_<machine>.json.
- Refuse a machine that is not one of the nine.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Each machine's own new design document; masses in kg, lengths in mm, moments of inertia in kg m2.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the machine, its parts, its mass and centre, its groups, the machine's own first line, and a
  version tag.
- Each of the nine weighed, its groups' masses summing to its total, its file written.
- The robot's eleven moving bodies written, each with a mass, each within 10 % of the concept's mass and moments of
  inertia.
- Refused: a machine called crane.
