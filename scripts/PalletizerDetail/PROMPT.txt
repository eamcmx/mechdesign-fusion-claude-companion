Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of the palletizer's assemblies of Chapters 41 to 48 in detail
(the bag gripper by default, the robot, the compliance unit or the vacuum gripper), each with its own script, and then
gives every made part its material and every fitting face its class and roughness as Chapter 51 decided them, with this
chapter's decisions by hand where they differ from the rules.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the assembly, bag
- the plugs' fillet weld leg (mm), 4

DERIVED (from lib/palletizer_detail.py and Chapter 51's MaterialsFits.py, never typed):
- eyes(), SRB: each rod eye's shoulder, seat and DIN 472 groove, its pin's collar, seat and end washer, from SKF's
  abutment dimensions for the 22207 E and the 22213 E
- robot_bearings(): each robot bearing held on both rings, its shaft's class and the clearance class it needs
- jaw_ends(), bag_6206(): the jaws' locating and floating ends, the 6206's clearance class
- plug_weld(): the fillet weld's stresses and its size against Shigley's Table 9-6
- gland(), gallery_plug(): the O-ring's groove to ERIKS's dynamic table, the DIN 908 plugs
- vacuum_joint(): the plate's through bolts, their length and preload
- HIWIN: the guideways' order codes
- MaterialsFits.apply(): the materials and the fits on the open design

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build with each assembly's own script (PalletizerRobot.py; StandardParts.py, the bag gripper as Chapter 50 detailed
  it; ComplianceUnit.py; VacuumGripper.py) and apply MaterialsFits.py's apply(); copy none of them. Change the parts by
  replacing, before the build, the functions that draw them, each replacement recognising only the calls it means and
  passing every other through.
- The robot: each rod eye bored to its bearing's outside with a shoulder of 64 (22207 E) or 110 (22213 E) on its pin's
  root side and a DIN 472 groove and circlip on the free side; each pin with a collar of 43 or 76 from its link to
  the inner ring, the seat through the ring, its end 0.5 mm short, an end washer and an ISO 4762 screw.
- The bag gripper: in each end plate the locating boss holds its 6206's outer ring between a lip 56 across and a DIN
  472 62 circlip, the floating boss leaves it 1 mm either way; the bosses lengthened to 451 mm; the short stubs with a
  shoulder 36 across and a DIN 471 30 circlip; the plugs set 4 mm into the tube's ends and a fillet weld all round.
- The compliance unit: the O-ring's groove 24.30 across and 3.40 wide; the O-ring drawn squeezed; six DIN 908
  M10 x 1 plugs with their collars in counterbores 15 across; the tube's outside split where it leaves its boss
  before the census.
- The vacuum gripper: the plate held by ISO 4762 M6 through bolts, a washer under each head and under each nut, the
  plate's holes 6.6 through.
- By hand after the census: H7 in the rods' eyes, H7 in the floating bosses, the jaws' tubes, stubs and welds in 1020,
  the tube's zones s6 Ra 0.8 in the boss and g6 Ra 0.4 below, the stubs' seats marked ground after welding, and the
  clearance classes and the HIWIN codes in the descriptions.
- Refuse a weld leg under Table 9-6's minimum for the 4 mm wall, or over the wall.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Each assembly's own new design document and groups; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message names the assembly, the faces given by hand and the descriptions, and a version tag.
- The bag gripper: the bosses' lip, seat and groove; four DIN 472 and two DIN 471 circlips; four welds from 401 to
  405 mm; the short stubs' shoulder and groove; the jaws in 1020; J7 at the locating ends and H7 at the floating; the
  6206-2RS1/C4; no interference closed nor open.
- The robot: the six eyes and pins as above, their circlips, washers and screws; the eyes H7, the triangle K7; the
  clearance classes in the designations; no interference at the pick pose nor at full reach.
- The compliance unit: the groove, the squeezed O-ring, the six plugs, the tube's two zones, the HIWIN codes; no
  interference at rest, nor risen but for the springs.
- The vacuum gripper: 16 through bolts, 32 washers, 16 nuts, no tapped hole; no interference.
- Refused: a weld leg of 3 mm.
