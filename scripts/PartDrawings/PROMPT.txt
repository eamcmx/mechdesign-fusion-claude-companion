Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, makes an engineering drawing for one part of each machine, the part
whose drawing Part 10 changed most, and puts on each sheet what Part 10 and Chapter 51 decided for it, so that a shop
that never sees the model makes the part that passed the checks; and a sheet of the bag gripper's opening stop put
together, which carries what the fitter must do.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the part: all, bracket, segment, spacer, fitting, disc or stop; all

DERIVED (never typed):
- lib/drawings_detail.py: the five parts and the stop's items, their numbers, their machines, and every value a sheet
  carries
- RangeSweep.py's machine(): each machine as its detail chapter built it; for the tool changer, MaterialsFits.apply()
  first, which the other four builds already run
- the part by its name in the machine; its classes and roughnesses from the attributes MaterialsFits.py left on its
  faces; its material from lib/materials_fits.py

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build each machine through RangeSweep.py's machine(), loaded, not copied.
- Take the part's body out into a design of its own, in the part's frame: z along its largest cylinder's axis, x
  towards its centre of mass, the origin on that axis at the body's lowest point.
- Carry into the model what Part 10 decided for its shape before drawing it: the opening bracket's arm root filleted
  2.5 mm on its four edges (Chapter 60); the same in the opening stop's assembly, so that its bracket is the one its
  own sheet draws.
- Save each design to the folder the script is given in the cloud project, under '<number> <title>', and never over a
  file already there; ask Fusion for its automatic ISO drawing (A3, millimetres), asking again while the design is
  not yet ready; close the auto-dimension dialog it leaves open.
- Read the classes off the faces; do not decide them again.
- On each sheet, find the curves by their geometry, not by their order:
  the bracket: the two bolt holes' size, their distance between centres, a position frame of diameter 1.0 at maximum
  material from datum A, the foot's bolting face;
  the segment: datum A its top face, datum B its 15 deg face; each wedge face's profile of 0.02, the 15 deg face's
  from A and the 5 deg face's from B, in its table (a profile frame from the API stops Fusion's drawing command);
  the spacer: the bore's size; datum A the face on cone A and the other face parallel to it within IT5, in its table
  (a datum on a round part's end face stops Fusion's drawing command);
  the spar fitting: the bores' size where they show round, and the bolt holes';
  the stop disc: the bore's size.
- The opening stop: its six kinds of item (the bracket, the DYSC-25 and its lock nut, the M8 screws, washers and
  nuts of jaw A's opening bracket) as one component each with its part number and designation, a piece of each where
  the machine has it, in the bracket's frame; Fusion's assembly sheet with its parts list; a table with the seat, the
  screws' torque and preload of Chapter 60, and the absorber's lock nut.
- Write on each part's sheet a table of requirements, one row each with the chapter that decided it: the material, the rows
  of drawings_detail.rows() and the classes read off the faces, the general tolerance ISO 2768-mK.
- Export each sheet as a PDF to results/drawings/<number>.pdf and write what was made, added and read back to
  results/part_drawings.json.
- Refuse a part that is not one of the six.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- ISO drawings, first-angle projection, millimetres; the API's cm in the model, mm on the sheet.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives each drawing's views, dimensions, symbols and PDF, the folder, and a version tag.
- Six drawings, each A3, each with its PDF on disk: a part's one sheet of at least two views, the stop's two sheets
  (Fusion's view with the parts list, then the projections).
- Each sheet gains the dimensions and symbols asked of it, counted on the sheet after the script.
- The bracket's model has four faces of 2.5 mm radius at its root and two holes of 9; the stop's bracket its root
  filleted the same.
- The stop's six kinds with their pieces, and its torque row.
- Every file in the folder once.
- Refused: a part called crane.
