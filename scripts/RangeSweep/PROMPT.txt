Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of Part 9's nine detailed assemblies with its own script,
steps it through its whole range of motion, and at every state looks for interference among all its parts and
measures how close the parts that move against each other come. For the bag gripper it also adds this chapter's
stops: a shock absorber at each end of each jaw's travel.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the machine: wrist, flap, axle, robot, bag0 (the bag gripper as Part 9 left it), bag (with its stops), clamp,
  vacuum, changer or unit; wrist

DERIVED (from lib/range_sweep.py, never typed):
- STATES: each machine's states from its own limits: the wrist's pitch from the hard stop's contact at -128 deg to
  +128 every 8 deg with the software limit and the switch, and its roll round a turn every 30 deg at the pitch's two
  limits and level; the flap from 0 to 40 deg every 2; the axle's pinion round a turn every 15 deg straight and every
  30 with the spider turned 2.5 deg; the robot round its working range's outline and at full reach; the bag
  gripper's jaws to the cylinders' retracted end (bag0) or to the opening stop at 45 deg (bag) every 3 deg, the
  clamp's jaws from 200 to 650 mm, the vacuum gripper's cups, the tool changer's lock and the compliance unit's float
  over their strokes
- GAP 10 mm: pairs whose boxes come within it are measured; STEADY 0.05 mm: a pair whose gap changes by less is a
  fixed gap or a designed contact
- the stops: STRIKER, AXIS_X, DYSC (Festo's DYSC sizes), jaw_stops() (the size for both ends), stop_layout() (where
  each absorber and bracket sits), bracket_bolts() (the brackets' M8 and their length)

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build each machine with its detail script's own build() (WristDetail, FlapDetail, AxleDetail, PalletizerDetail for
  the robot, the bag gripper, the vacuum gripper and the compliance unit, ClampGripper, ToolChanger) and put it in a
  state with that script's own pose or set_state, or the axle's two joints; copy none of them.
- For the bag gripper with its stops: before the build, wrap PalletizerDetail's own patch of BagGripper so that each
  lever is carried on past its pin to 200 mm, 15 thick, as a striker, and the end plates are drilled 9 across for the
  brackets' bolts; after the build add to the frame, for each end plate (the -x end's turned half a turn about z):
  the closing bracket (foot, lip over the plate's end, lug) and the opening bracket (foot, arm, round lug) in steel
  1045, a DYSC-25-25-Y1F in each in its end position with its lock nut, and each bracket's two ISO 4762 M8 x 40 with
  an ISO 7089 washer and an ISO 4032 nut inside the plate; describe them.
- Leave out the parts that are not the machine: the clamp's carton, the vacuum gripper's carton and pack, the bag
  gripper's bag and rollers; and the vacuum gripper's and the compliance unit's springs, which are drawn rigid at
  their installed length.
- Group the parts by how they move: put the machine in four of its states, and parts whose transforms change alike
  against the first are one rigid group. Measure only pairs from different groups.
- Before the sweep, measure the pairs whose boxes come within GAP at the four states, and keep only those whose gap
  varies by STEADY or more or that are not near at all four.
- At every state: Fusion's interference among all the parts, touching faces not counted; and the minimum distance of
  every kept pair whose boxes come within GAP. Key the pairs by the parts, not their names.
- Report a pair only if its gap changes by STEADY or more over the sweep; a pair that reaches 0 is a contact made in
  motion. Count the pairs at each step. Return to the first state at the end.
- Refuse a machine that is not one of the ten.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Each machine's own new design document; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the machine, its states, its parts and groups, the states with interference, the contacts made in
  motion, the three closest approaches with their states, the machine's own first line, and a version tag.
- Each machine, the bag gripper both ways: no interference at any state.
- The stops: each closing absorber touching its striker closed, each opening one the lever's bar at 45 deg, and
  touching nothing else.
- Each machine's contacts made in motion, closest approaches and pair counts kept in results/range_sweep.json.
- Refused: a machine called crane.
