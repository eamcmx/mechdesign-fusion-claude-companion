Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, measures the small gaps Chapter 56's range sweep found, each with its
direction, so that the tolerance stack-ups know which dimensions close it: for each gap, build its machine, put it in
the state of the closest approach, and measure the least distance between the two parts, its two points and the
direction from one to the other; then in the states either side, to see how fast the gap closes with the motion.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the gap: all, unit, flap, wrist, changer, axle_casting or axle_cap; all

DERIVED (from lib/stackups.py, never typed):
- GAPS: each gap's name, its machine, its state, its two parts (by the start of their names) and the states either
  side: the compliance unit's M10 x 25 and its carriage risen 30 mm; the flap's rod end and its horn at 40 deg; the
  wrist's stop disc and the left stop cap's ISO 10642 M4 x 12 at pitch 24 deg; the tool changer's cam and the tool plate
  locked; the axle's crown wheel bolts and the carrier casting driving straight, and cap C1 in a turn

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Build each machine through RangeSweep.py's machine(), the bag gripper with its stops, loaded, not copied; build a
  machine once for all its gaps, in the order of GAPS.
- Measure with Fusion's minimum distance between every body of every part whose name starts with the first name and
  every body of every part whose name starts with the second, leaving out the names a gap excludes (the horn's pin
  from the horn); keep the least, its two points and the unit vector
  from the first point to the second.
- Return the machine to the gap's own state at the end of each gap.
- Write every gap's rows to results/stack_gaps.json.
- Refuse a gap that is not one of the six.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Each machine's own new design document; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives each gap's parts, its least distance and its direction, and a version tag.
- Each gap as Chapter 56's sweep found it: 0.707, 0.859, 1.000, 1.500, 1.000 and 1.000 mm, within 0.002.
- The states either side measured and printed.
- Refused: a gap called crane.
