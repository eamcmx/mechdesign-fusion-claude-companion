Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the bag gripper with its bought parts described by their full
designations and its driven stub shafts detailed with their standard parts, as Chapter 50 decided them.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the shaft's shoulder (mm), 36
- the key's length (mm), 25

DERIVED (from lib/standard_parts.py and lib/detail_inventory.py, never typed):
- stub_detail(): the shoulder from bearing_6206() (the first whole millimetre over SKF's da min), SKF's band, the
  bearing's d, D, B, d1 and D2, the key's row of DIN 6885-1 (b, h, t1, t2) and its length from lever_key(), the hub,
  the spacer, the end washer and the end screw, the shaft's end 1 mm inside the hub's face
- dowel_adapter(): the ISO 8734 dowel's made length and its depth in the adapter
- decide(): every bought designation's full designation; detail_numbers(): the new kinds' numbers
- number_map(), kind_of() and Chapter 49's PartNumbers.py for the numbers

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a shoulder outside SKF's band (35.6 to 40.3 mm), a key shorter than crushing in the brake stop allows
  (23.70 mm) and a key length DIN 6885-1 does not make at 8 mm wide.
- Build the gripper with BagGripper.py's own build() and number it with PartNumbers.py's number(); copy neither. Change
  the gripper's parts by replacing, before the build, three of BagGripper's functions: the jaw's bodies (the driven
  stub shaft, the lever, and the parts the detail adds), the tube that draws the bearings, and the cylinder that
  draws the dowel and its hole; each replacement recognises only the calls it means and passes every other through.
- The driven stub shaft: the end cap in the tube, the shoulder from the tube's end to the bearing, the 30 mm seat to
  1 mm inside the hub's face, a form-A keyseat t1 deep on top, a tapped hole for the end screw at its nominal
  diameter.
- The four 6206-2RS1: d 30, D 62, B 16, the seals between d1 and D2 set in 0.5 mm from the rings' faces.
- The lever's hub lengthened into a boss 5 mm longer than the key, bored 30 and keywayed t2 deep through; the key
  2.5 mm inside each end of the boss; a spacer, 36 outside and 30 inside, from the inner ring to the boss; an end
  washer 40 x 5 with a 9 mm hole, and an ISO 4762 M8 x 20 through it into the shaft's end.
- The adapter's dowel ISO 8734 10 x 22, 13 mm in the adapter and 9 in the tool plate.
- Both jaws alike: the -y jaw's parts are the +y jaw's turned, as the gripper's own script makes them.
- Numbers: Chapter 49's; the detail's new made kinds the next free items of P13 in the order of their names, the key
  the next free serial. Descriptions: a bought part's full designation from decide().

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- The gripper's script makes its own new design document; the numbers and descriptions go into Component.partNumber
  and Component.description; no joint is added and nothing is moved; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside mdfc_parts.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the shoulder and SKF's band, the bearing, the spacer, the hub and the key, the end washer and the
  screw, the dowel, the bought components described, the new numbers and a version tag.
- 82 parts: the gripper's 74 and four more on each jaw's driven end.
- Every bought component described by its full designation; the new kinds numbered alike on both jaws.
- The shoulder measured on the model inside SKF's band; the bearings' faces at d, d1, D2 and D, 16 wide.
- The key inside the hub's boss, 2.5 mm from each end; the stack closed from the shoulder to the end washer, the
  washer 1 mm clear of the shaft's end; the end screw's thread at least 1.5 d.
- The dowel 22 long, 13 in the adapter.
- No interference closed on the bag, nor with the jaws open 45 deg and the press up.
- Refused: a shoulder of 35 mm; a key of 22 mm; a key of 26 mm.
