Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the star gearbox of a geared turbofan: a planetary gear set
whose carrier is held, so that the sun, driven by the low-pressure turbine, turns star gears on fixed pins and the
stars turn the ring, which drives the fan the other way; assembled and ready to turn with Drive Joints.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- module m (mm), 3
- sun teeth Zs, 32
- star teeth Zp, 33
- number of stars N, 5
- pressure angle alpha (deg), 20
- face width b (mm), 40
- backlash on the pitch circle (mm), 0.3
- star pin diameter (mm), 20
- sun shaft diameter, from the turbine (mm), 30
- fan shaft diameter (mm), 60
- carrier and ring plates (mm), 8
- ring wall outside the tooth spaces (mm), 10

DERIVED (computed from the inputs, never typed):
- ring teeth Zr = Zs + 2 Zp; centre distance a = m (Zs + Zp) / 2; star i at theta_i = 2 pi i / N
- star phases and the ring's phase as in Chapter 15: psi_i = [theta_i (Zs + Zp) + pi (Zp - 1)] / Zp (mod 2 pi / Zp);
  the ring's tooth spaces at k 2 pi / Zr + phi_r with phi_r = 0 for Zp odd, pi / Zr for Zp even
- with the carrier held the ring turns -Zs / Zr of the sun's angle; the ratio Zr / Zs; each star -Zs / Zp of it
- for 20 MW from a turbine at 10 000 rpm: the sun's torque, the ring's (times Zr / Zs), the carrier's reaction (their
  sum), the tangential force at each sun mesh (the sun's torque over N times its pitch radius), and the heat if
  0.5 % of the power is lost in the meshes
- whether the stars' phases are all equal: (Zs + Zp) / N whole

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Every gear at least 18 teeth (Chapter 12), or refuse.
- (Zs + Zr) / N whole, or refuse: the stars cannot be spaced evenly.
- Neighbouring stars must not touch: 2 a sin(pi / N) > m (Zp + 2), or refuse.
- The carrier on the turbine's side, its pins through the stars; the ring closed on the fan's side by a plate and
  the fan shaft, 1 mm clear of the gears' faces. Nothing touches anything else.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- Create a new Fusion design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), not a part design.
- Components, one body each, created where they belong: Star carrier (grounded), Ring (teeth, plate and fan shaft
  as one body), Sun (with its shaft), Star 1 to N.
- Gears as in Chapter 15: a root disc, one tooth outline as a closed polyline, joined and patterned; the ring an
  annulus with one tooth space cut and patterned.
- The API works in cm and radians.
- AsBuiltJoints: revolute about z for the sun, the ring and each star, all on the star carrier. Motion Links: the sun
  to the ring at Zr : Zs and the sun to each star at Zp : Zs, all reversed.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives Zr, a, the ratio, the fan speed, the torques, the mesh force, the heat and a version tag.
- Defaults: Zr = 98, a = 97.500 mm, ratio 3.0625 : 1, fan -3265.3 rpm; sun 19 098.6 N m, ring 58 489.4 N m, carrier
  77 588.0 N m; 79 577 N at each sun mesh; 100 kW of heat; (Zs + Zr) / N = 26, (Zs + Zp) / N = 13.
- 8 components with one body each; 7 revolute As-built Joints; 6 Motion Links.
- No interference at rest.
- The sun driven 90 deg in 30 deg steps: the ring -29.388 deg and each star -87.273 deg, measured on the bodies.
- No tooth collision while the sun turns through one tooth pitch (11.25 deg) in 0.9 deg steps.
- Refused: N = 4 ((Zs + Zr) / N = 32.50); Zs = 20, Zp = 40 (the stars touch); Zs = 16 (undercut).

Give me one Python file and its .manifest.
