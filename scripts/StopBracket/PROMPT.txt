Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the bag gripper with Chapter 56's stops and changes its two
opening stop brackets as Chapter 62 decided: the arm turned 45 deg about its own axis, so that the shock absorber's
push lies square to a face of the arm instead of along its diagonal, and the arm's root rounded; then measures the
change and sweeps the gripper through its range, so that the designer sees the bracket the drawings will carry, in
the machine it works in.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the root radius of the arm (mm), 2.5

DERIVED (never typed):
- lib/lugs_brackets.py: the turn (45 deg), the arm turned (its sides along the absorber's axis and the bolts' line,
  its corners on the foot's face), the clearance from each M8's head to the arm and the largest radius it allows,
  the bracket's volume worked out by hand
- lib/range_sweep.py: the opening stop's layout (the foot, the arm as Chapter 56 made it, the lug, the absorber's
  axis), the DYSC-25's thread, the bag gripper's states
- scripts/RangeSweep/RangeSweep.py: the bag gripper with its stops, and its sweep, loaded and called, not copied

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- The bracket changed in the machine's own model, in the base feature that holds it: the arm as Chapter 56 made it
  cut off, the arm turned put back, the lug made whole again and bored again for the absorber's M30 x 1.5; the foot,
  its two M8 holes, the lug and the absorber where they were.
- Both ends: jaw B's bracket is jaw A's turned half a turn about z.
- The root's four edges filleted with the radius given, as a fillet feature on the bracket's own component.
- The bracket keeps its material, steel 1045.
- Measured on each bracket: the arm's sides against the absorber's axis, the faces of the root's radius, the gap
  from each of its two M8 heads to the arm, its volume and mass.
- The whole bag gripper swept through Chapter 56's states with RangeSweep's own sweep: interference, the contacts
  made in motion, the closest approaches of the brackets.
- Save nothing; refuse a radius below 0 or at or above the one at which the fillet's toe reaches the M8 heads.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- the machine's new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), as RangeSweep makes it; the body changed with
  temporary bodies (cut, union), in world millimetres turned into the component's frame, and BaseFeature.updateBody;
  the machine's joints and as-built joints left as RangeSweep made them, none added; lengths in mm, the API's cm
  inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the arm's sides against the push, the root's radius and its faces, each bracket's volume and mass
  against the hand volume, the two heads' gaps, the sweep's states, interference and contacts, and a version tag.
- Every side of the arm square to the absorber's axis or along it, within 0.01 deg.
- Four faces of the root's radius on each bracket.
- Each bracket's volume within 0.01 % of the hand volume in lib/lugs_brackets.py.
- Each M8 head clear of the arm by the hand gap less 0.01 mm or more.
- No interference anywhere over the range; the contacts made in motion those of Chapter 56.
- Refused: a root radius of 7 mm.
