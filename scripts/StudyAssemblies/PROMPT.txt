Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of the real assemblies Chapter 62's Simulation studies are
run on: the part under study and the neighbours that carry its load, copied out of the machine's own detailed model
into a design of their own, one component each with its material, where they sit in the machine, so that the study
supports and loads the part the way the machine does.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the assembly: one of stop, stop_diagonal, fitting or hinge, stop

DERIVED (never typed):
- scripts/RangeSweep/RangeSweep.py: the bag gripper with its stops and the flap system as their detail chapters left
  them, loaded and called, not copied
- scripts/StopBracket/StopBracket.py: Chapter 62's change to the opening bracket, called on the machine's own bracket
- lib/range_sweep.py: the opening stop's layout, the absorber's axis, the design's push and the DYSC's rating
- lib/flap_design.py: the spar fitting's and the hinges' layout, the actuator's ultimate push, the hinges' ultimate
  load
- lib/lugs_brackets.py: the opening bracket's hand volume, turned and on its diagonal; the spar web patch's

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- stop: jaw A's opening bracket with its arm turned 45 deg and its root rounded 2.5 mm, its DYSC-25-25-Y1F, the lock
  nut and the end plate; stop_diagonal: the same with the arm on its diagonal and the same root.
- fitting: the spar fitting, both GE 20 ES and both trunnions, and the rear spar web cut to 200 mm either side of the
  base plate.
- hinge: the right hinge bracket, its rib, the GE 20 ES in its eye, the hinge pin and the flap's two hinge fittings.
- Every body copied from the machine's model, none rebuilt; the bolts left out, for the study's bolt connectors.
- The faces each study picks found on the bodies: the bolt holes, the fixed faces, the face the load goes on.
- Save nothing; refuse an assembly that is not one of the four.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- a new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType) for the assembly, parametric, each part a component of the root
  with its bodies in a base feature, no joints and no as-built joints (the study's contacts and connectors join
  them); the machine's document closed unsaved; lengths in mm, the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the assembly, each part's bodies, material and volume, the picks found, and a version tag.
- Every part the machine's own volume and material; the opening bracket's within 0.01 % of lib/lugs_brackets.py's,
  turned for stop and on its diagonal for stop_diagonal; the web patch's within 0.01 % of its hand volume.
- Every pick found on a face.
- The loads: the stop's push of 2180.0 N along the absorber's axis and the DYSC's 4000 N; the fitting's 20 848.4 N
  both ways; the hinge's 13 956.0 N.
- Refused: an assembly called crane.
