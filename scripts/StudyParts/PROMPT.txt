Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds one of the parts the Simulation studies of Chapters 58, 59 and 62
are run on, each in a new design of its own as one body with its material, and prints the faces each study picks, so
that the designer sets the study up in the Simulation window on a part whose every size the hand calculation also uses.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with this default):
- the part: one of the six (cantilever, keyseat, tine, bracket_sharp, bracket_r25, lug), cantilever

DERIVED (never typed):
- lib/fea_studies.py: each part's sizes, the hand calculations the studies are set against, and each part's volume
  worked out by hand
- lib/bag_gripper.py (the tine's leg and foot), lib/range_sweep.py (the opening stop bracket's arm and foot),
  lib/fatigue_detail.py (the long stub's keyseat), lib/flap_design.py (the spar fitting's lug), through fea_studies

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- One body in the root component, its material from Fusion's material library: Steel for the five steel parts,
  Aluminum 7075 for the lug.
- The cantilever 200 x 20 x 10 along x from x = 0, its 10 mm depth along z.
- The keyseat: the 30 mm stub, 150 long along x, with a DIN 6885 form A keyseat 8 wide, 4 deep and 25 long at its
  middle, its round ends cut by the end mill, its floor's four edges rounded to r/d = 0.02, 0.6 mm.
- The tine as it hangs from the jaw: its leg 250 long, 25 along y and 10 along x, its top at z = -230; its foot
  255 along y and 25 deep under the leg's lower end.
- The bracket: the foot 12 thick and 70 square, the arm 24 square and 48 long from the foot's face to the push;
  bracket_sharp with the root sharp, bracket_r25 with the drawing's 2.5 mm fillet all round the arm's root.
- The lug: 12 thick, 55 wide, its eye's end round on the hole's centre with radius 27.5, the hole 35 across for the
  GE 20 ES's outer ring, its shank 100 long to the spar.
- Each part's faces for its study found on the body and printed: the fixed face, and the face the load goes on.
- Save nothing; refuse a part that is not one of the six.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- a new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType), parametric, the body made from temporary bodies in a base feature
  and the fillets as fillet features on its edges; no joints and no as-built joints, the part is one body; lengths
  in mm, the API's cm inside.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the part, its volume, its material, its faces, its mass, the faces to pick found, and a version tag.
- Each part's volume within 0.01 % of the hand volume in lib/fea_studies.py.
- The keyseat's floor fillets present: the body has 12 faces; the bracket's root fillets: 15 faces.
- Every face to pick found on the body with its outward normal.
- Refused: a part called crane.
