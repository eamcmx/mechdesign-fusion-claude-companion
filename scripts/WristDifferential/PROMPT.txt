Write a Fusion Python script that, when run, builds the pitch-roll wrist of Chapter 39: a miter bevel differential
whose two side gears turn on the pitch axis and whose output gear turns the tool flange about the roll axis, each
side gear driven by a toothed belt from a servo motor with a planetary gearhead, the two motors on the frame, so
that the wrist can be posed at any pitch and roll and its parts checked for interference and weighed.

INPUTS (a command dialog, with these defaults):
- the bevel set: module 1.5 mm, 40 teeth, face width 11 mm

DERIVED (from lib/wrist_design.py, never typed):
- bevel(): the set in Shigley's proportions (Table 13-3): addendum m, clearance 0.188 m + 0.05 mm, the toe's
  section the heel's scaled about the apex; the front face through the toe's root
- layout(): every axial position from the gear set and the bearings: the carrier's faces, the trunnions' flanges,
  the spacers, the 61902s and 6002s, the pulley on each side gear's hub, the cheeks, the roll shaft's collar and
  bearings, the output gear's back and hub, the tool flange; the belt's centre distance from its stock length
- MOTOR39 and GEARHEAD: the motors' and gearheads' outlines (assumed)

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Refuse a set whose face is above 0.3 R or 10 m, or under R / 4, or whose front face is nearer the apex than the
  carrier needs (the roll bearing's radius, its seat's wall, the trunnion's flange and a shim space: 22.5 mm), or
  whose teeth fail the duty or the drives' torque limit; refuse any set but the 40-tooth, 60 mm one, for which the
  layout is drawn and the geometry factors were read.
- A gap of the output gear faces each side gear's tooth at rest.
- Each side gear runs on two 61902s on its trunnion, the one at its front in the gear, the other inside the
  pulley's hub; the inner rings, the spacers and the frame's 6002 clamped by an end washer and an M5 screw.
- The carrier: 7075, its walls 3 mm beside the roll bearings' seats, chamfered at 45 deg under the output gear's
  toe; the upper roll bearing held by a DIN 472 ring, the lower one free to slide in its seat.
- The two motors side by side under the carrier, each in its own belt's plane with its body across the frame and
  through a window in the far cheek; the small pulleys flanged both sides.
- Threads drawn as plain holes at their nominal size.
- Nothing touches anything else, at rest and at the poses of the check.

FUSION CONVENTIONS:
- A new design document (FusionDesignDocumentType). Origin at the gears' apex, y along the pitch axis, z along the
  roll axis at rest, x forward; L is +y, as in Chapter 31.
- Groups: the frame (grounded); the carrier, on an As-built revolute joint about y; the roll output on the
  carrier, about z; the two inputs on the carrier, about y; on each motor's axis a body-less belt helper on the
  frame and the motor's output on it.
- Motion links, two joints each: gear L turns -psi and gear R +psi on the carrier; each helper turns 2 phi; motor L
  turns -2 psi and motor R +2 psi on its helper; the pitch limited to +-120 deg.
- pose(pitch, roll) in degrees. The API works in cm and radians.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
- A message gives the set, its front face, the safety factors, the motors and the parts list with a version tag.
- 89 components in 34 kinds.
- Pitch alone, roll alone and both: every gear and motor at its equation's angle.
- No interference at rest and at eight poses.
- The moving groups' masses and inertias measured.
- Refused: module 1.25 with 40 teeth; a face of 13 mm; module 2 with 30 teeth.
